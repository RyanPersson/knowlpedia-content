## Checked applications

**Expansion of squares.** The ordinary second moment is divided by the square of the unipotent covolume ν. The link hypothesis carries a factor ν². With C ≥ 0 and primitive-root/admissibility assumptions, the cut remaining-term moment is ≤ C + b⁻ᵏᶠ M_id, and the cut error is ≤ b⁻⁴⁰ M_id. Minkowski yields the stated square-root bound after kf ≥ 40.
