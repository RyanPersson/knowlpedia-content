A **distinguished triangle** is a [[algebra-category-theory/triangle|triangle]]
\[
X\to Y\to Z\to X[1]
\]
belonging to a specified class that is required to satisfy the triangle axioms. It abstracts the [[algebra-homological/mapping-cone|mapping-cone]] sequence of a chain map and plays the role that a [[algebra-modules/short-exact-sequence|short exact sequence]] plays in an [[algebra-category-theory/abelian-category|abelian category]].

The class is closed under isomorphism and rotation, and every morphism must occur as the first map of some distinguished triangle. The precise coherence requirements distinguish a [[algebra-category-theory/pretriangulated-category|pretriangulated category]] from an arbitrary shifted [[algebra-category-theory/preadditive-category|preadditive category]].
