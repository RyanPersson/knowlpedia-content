A **category** \(\mathcal C\) consists of
**[[algebra-category-theory/object|objects]]**,
**[[algebra-category-theory/morphism|morphisms]]** between them, an associative
**[[algebra-category-theory/composition-category|composition]]** of morphisms,
and an **[[algebra-category-theory/identity-morphism|identity morphism]]** for
each object, satisfying the
[[algebra-category-theory/category-axioms|category axioms]] below.
