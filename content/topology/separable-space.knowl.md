A [[topology/topological-space|topological space]] \(X\) is **separable** if it contains a [[shared-foundations/countable-set|countable]] [[topology/dense-set|dense subset]].

## Equivalent formulation

This means that some countable subset meets every nonempty open subset of \(X\).

## Metric spaces

For a [[topology/metric-space|metric space]], separability says that the whole space can be approximated arbitrarily closely by countably many points. A Banach or Hilbert space is called separable when it is separable in the topology induced by its norm.
