A [[topology/topological-space|topological space]] is **totally disconnected** if every [[topology/connected-set|connected subset]] has at most one point.

## Equivalent formulation

Every connected component is a singleton. The empty space is also totally disconnected.

## Examples

Discrete spaces, the rationals with their usual subspace topology, and the Cantor set are totally disconnected. Total disconnectedness does not imply discreteness.

## Locally compact groups

Total disconnectedness is one of the defining conditions of a [[topology/locally-profinite-group|locally profinite group]]. The existence of compact open subgroups in that setting is an additional theorem, not part of the definition of total disconnectedness.
