**Heine–Borel theorem:** In [[linear-algebra/euclidean-space|Euclidean space]] \(\mathbb{R}^n\) with its usual metric (and induced topology), a set \(K\subseteq \mathbb{R}^n\) is [[topology/compact-set|compact]] if and only if it is both [[topology/closed-set|closed]] and [[topology/bounded-set|bounded]].

## Context

This specializes general implications like [[topology/compactness-implies-boundedness|compactness implies boundedness]] and [[topology/compactness-implies-closedness|compactness implies closedness]] into a complete characterization in Euclidean settings, and it is frequently paired with [[topology/bolzano-weierstrass-theorem|Bolzano–Weierstrass]] and sequential criteria such as [[topology/sequentially-compact-set|sequential compactness]].
