**Compact-to-Hausdorff homeomorphism criterion.** Let \(f:X\to Y\) be a continuous bijection. If \(X\) is compact and \(Y\) is [[topology/hausdorff-space|Hausdorff]], then \(f\) is a [[topology/homeomorphism|homeomorphism]].

## Remarks

Indeed, every closed subset of \(X\) is compact, its image under \(f\) is compact, and [[topology/compact-subset-of-hausdorff-is-closed|compact subsets of \(Y\) are closed]]. Thus \(f\) is a closed map, so \(f^{-1}\) is continuous.
