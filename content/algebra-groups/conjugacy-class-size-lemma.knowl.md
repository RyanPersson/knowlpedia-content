Let \(G\) be a finite [[algebra-groups/group|group]] and \(g\in G\). If \(\mathrm{Cl}(g)\) is the [[algebra-groups/conjugacy-class|conjugacy class]] of \(g\) and \(C_G(g)\) its [[algebra-groups/centralizer|centralizer]], then
\[
|\mathrm{Cl}(g)| = [G : C_G(g)],
\]
where \([G:C_G(g)]\) is the [[algebra-groups/index-of-subgroup|index]] of \(C_G(g)\) in \(G\).

## Remarks

This is the [[algebra-groups/orbit-stabilizer-theorem|orbit–stabilizer theorem]] applied to the [[algebra-groups/conjugation-action|conjugation action]] of \(G\) on itself.
