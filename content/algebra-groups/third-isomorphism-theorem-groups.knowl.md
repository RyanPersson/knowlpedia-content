**Third Isomorphism Theorem (Groups).**
Let \(G\) be a [[algebra-groups/group|group]], and let \(N\subseteq K\) be [[algebra-groups/normal-subgroup|normal subgroups]] of \(G\). Then \(K/N\trianglelefteq G/N\), and there is a canonical isomorphism of [[algebra-groups/quotient-group|quotient groups]]
\[
(G/N)/(K/N) \cong G/K,
\]
induced by \(gN\mapsto gK\).

## Remarks

The map \(G/N\to G/K\), \(gN\mapsto gK\), is surjective with kernel \(K/N\), so the result follows from the [[algebra-groups/first-isomorphism-theorem-groups|first isomorphism theorem]].
