**Sylow's First Theorem.**
Let \(G\) be a finite [[algebra-groups/group|group]], and write \(|G|=p^am\), where \(p\) is prime and \(p\nmid m\). Then \(G\) has a [[algebra-groups/subgroup|subgroup]] of order \(p^a\). Such a subgroup is called a [[algebra-groups/sylow-subgroup|Sylow \(p\)-subgroup]].

## Remarks

The case \(a=1\) includes [[algebra-groups/cauchys-theorem-groups|Cauchy's theorem]]. Together, the three Sylow theorems describe the maximal [[algebra-groups/p-group|\(p\)-subgroups]] of \(G\).
