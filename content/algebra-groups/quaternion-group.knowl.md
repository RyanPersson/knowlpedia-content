The **quaternion group** \(Q_8\) is the [[algebra-groups/group|group]]
\[
Q_8=\{1,-1,i,-i,j,-j,k,-k\},
\]
whose multiplication is determined by
\[
i^2=j^2=k^2=ijk=-1.
\]
## Multiplication rules

Thus \(ij=k\), \(jk=i\), and \(ki=j\), while reversing the order of any two of \(i,j,k\) changes the sign.

## Properties

The element \(-1\) is central, every element other than \(1\) and \(-1\) has order \(4\), and \(Q_8\) is nonabelian. The displayed rules give a closed associative multiplication; equivalently, \(Q_8\) is the subgroup generated by \(i\) and \(j\) inside the units of the Hamilton [[algebra-rings/division-ring|division ring]] of quaternions.
