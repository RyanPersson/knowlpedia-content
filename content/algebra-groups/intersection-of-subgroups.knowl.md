**Proposition (Intersection of subgroups).**
Let \(G\) be a [[algebra-groups/group|group]] and let \(\{H_i\}_{i\in I}\) be a family of [[algebra-groups/subgroup|subgroups]] of \(G\). Then
\[
H=\{g\in G:g\in H_i\text{ for every }i\in I\}
\]
is a subgroup of \(G\). Thus \(H=\bigcap_{i\in I}H_i\), with the empty intersection understood to be \(G\).

## Consequence

The intersection of all subgroups containing a subset \(S\subseteq G\) is the smallest subgroup containing \(S\), called the subgroup generated by \(S\).
