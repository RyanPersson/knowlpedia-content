Let \(G\) be a [[algebra-groups/group|group]]. A **proper subgroup** of \(G\) is a [[algebra-groups/subgroup|subgroup]] \(H\le G\) such that \(H\ne G\), equivalently \(H\) is a [[shared-foundations/proper-subset|proper subset]] of \(G\).

## Examples

- \(2\mathbb{Z}\) is a proper subgroup of \(\mathbb{Z}\).
- \(A_n\) is a proper subgroup of \(S_n\) for \(n\ge 2\).
- The trivial subgroup \(\{e\}\) is proper whenever \(G\) is nontrivial.

## Remarks

A nontrivial group is simple precisely when its only normal subgroups are the trivial subgroup and the whole group.
