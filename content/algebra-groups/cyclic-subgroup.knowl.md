Let \(G\) be a [[algebra-groups/group|group]] and \(g\in G\). The **cyclic subgroup generated by \(g\)** is
\[
\langle g\rangle=\{g^n:n\in\mathbb Z\}.
\]
It is the smallest [[algebra-groups/subgroup|subgroup]] of \(G\) containing \(g\), hence a special case of the [[algebra-groups/generated-subgroup|subgroup generated by a subset]].

A group is **cyclic** if it equals \(\langle g\rangle\) for some \(g\).

## Examples

- In \((\mathbb Z,+)\), \(\langle 3\rangle=3\mathbb Z\).
- In \(\mathbb C^\times\), \(\langle i\rangle=\{1,i,-1,-i\}\).
- The subgroup \(\langle e\rangle=\{e\}\) is trivial.
