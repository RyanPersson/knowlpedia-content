Let \((V,q)\) be a nondegenerate real
[[linear-algebra/quadratic-form|quadratic space]] of signature \((p,q)\), with
negative directions listed first, and form its
[[differential-geometry/clifford-algebra|Clifford algebra]] using
\(v^2=-q(v)1\). The **indefinite pin group** is the subgroup
\[
\operatorname{Pin}(p,q)
\subseteq\operatorname{Cl}(V,q)^\times
\]
generated by the vectors \(v\in V\) satisfying \(q(v)=\pm1\).

## Orthogonal covering

The twisted adjoint action on \(V\) gives a surjective homomorphism
\[
\rho:\operatorname{Pin}(p,q)\longrightarrow O(p,q)
\]
with kernel \(\{\pm1\}\). A generating unit vector acts as the reflection in
its orthogonal hyperplane, and the Cartan–Dieudonné theorem supplies
surjectivity.

The even part of the pin group is the
[[lie-groups/indefinite-spin-group|indefinite spin group]]
\(\operatorname{Spin}(p,q)\), which maps onto \(SO(p,q)\).

## Convention

With the alternative Clifford convention \(v^2=+q(v)\), the names
\(\operatorname{Pin}(p,q)\) and \(\operatorname{Pin}(q,p)\) may be
interchanged. A pin-group statement should therefore specify both the
signature order and the Clifford relation.
