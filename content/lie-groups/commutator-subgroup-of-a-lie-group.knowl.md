Let \(G\) be a [[fiber-bundles/lie-group|Lie group]]. Its **commutator subgroup** (or **derived subgroup**) is the abstract subgroup
\[
[G,G]=\langle ghg^{-1}h^{-1}:g,h\in G\rangle.
\]

It is the smallest normal subgroup \(N\triangleleft G\) for which \(G/N\) is abelian; equivalently, \(G/[G,G]\) is the algebraic abelianization of \(G\).

## Remarks

If \(G\) is connected and \(\mathfrak g=\operatorname{Lie}(G)\), then \([G,G]\) has a natural immersed Lie-subgroup structure and its Lie algebra is the [[lie-groups/derived-subalgebra|derived subalgebra]] \([\mathfrak g,\mathfrak g]\). Connectedness matters: components of a disconnected group can contribute additional commutators.

In general, \([G,G]\) need not be closed. The quotient by its closure \(\overline{[G,G]}\) is the maximal Hausdorff abelian quotient of \(G\); the closure is a Lie subgroup by the [[lie-groups/closed-subgroup-theorem|closed subgroup theorem]].

The group \(G\) is [[lie-groups/abelian-lie-group|abelian]] precisely when \([G,G]\) is trivial.
