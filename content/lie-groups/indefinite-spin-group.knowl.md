Let \((V,q)\) be a real [[linear-algebra/quadratic-form|quadratic space]] of
signature \((p,q)\), where this collection lists the \(p\) negative and then
the \(q\) positive directions, and form its
[[differential-geometry/clifford-algebra|Clifford algebra]] using
\(v^2=-q(v)1\). The **indefinite spin group** is the even part of the
[[lie-groups/indefinite-pin-group|indefinite pin group]]:
\[
\operatorname{Spin}(p,q)=\operatorname{Pin}(p,q)\cap\operatorname{Cl}^0(V,q).
\]
Equivalently, it is generated by products of an even number of vectors
\(v\) satisfying \(q(v)=\pm1\). The ambient
\(\operatorname{Pin}(p,q)\) records products of arbitrary parity.

## Orthogonal covering

The restriction of the twisted adjoint action is a surjective homomorphism
\[
\rho:\operatorname{Spin}(p,q)\longrightarrow SO(p,q)
\]
with kernel \(\{\pm1\}\). In indefinite signature, \(\operatorname{Spin}(p,q)\)
need not be connected. The full preimage of the identity component and its
component convention are treated by the
[[lie-groups/restricted-spin-group|restricted spin group]].

## Convention warning

Authors also use Clifford relations \(v^2=+q(v)\) or interchange the order of
\(p\) and \(q\). These choices can alter the notation for the Clifford
algebra and its components but not the associated [[lie-groups/orthogonal-group|orthogonal group]]. The
positive-definite construction is treated separately at
[[lie-groups/spin-group|spin group]].
