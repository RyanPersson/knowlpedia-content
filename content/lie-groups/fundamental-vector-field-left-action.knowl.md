For a [[lie-groups/smooth-action-lie-group|smooth left action]] of a Lie group \(G\) on \(M\), the **fundamental vector field** associated with \(X\in\mathfrak g\), in the positive-exponential convention, is
\[
X_M(p)=\left.\frac{d}{dt}\right|_{t=0}\exp_G(tX)\cdot p.
\]
Its flow is \(p\mapsto\exp_G(tX)\cdot p\), so this [[fiber-bundles/vector-field|vector field]] is complete. The [[lie-groups/exponential-map-lie-group|exponential]] specifies the sign as well as the direction.

## Bracket sign

For this convention,
\[
[X_M,Y_M]=-[X,Y]_M.
\]
Indeed, on \(G\) acting on itself by left multiplication these are right-invariant vector fields, whose bracket has the negative Lie-algebra sign. Orbit maps carry these related fields to the action on \(M\).

Consequently \(X\mapsto-X_M\) preserves brackets. This is the sign that appears when [[lie-groups/derived-pullback-representation|differentiating inverse pullback on functions]].

## Comparison with a right action

For a right action, the analogous positive-exponential curve is \(p\cdot\exp(tX)\). That is the [[fiber-bundles/convention-fundamental-vector-field-x-is-defined-using-the-right-action|principal-bundle convention]] used elsewhere in this collection. One must specify the side of the action and the sign in the exponential before comparing formulas.
