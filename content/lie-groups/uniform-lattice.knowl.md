A [[lie-groups/lattice-in-lie-group|lattice]] \(\Gamma\le G\) is **uniform**, or **cocompact**, if \(\Gamma\backslash G\) is [[topology/compact-set|compact]]. It is **nonuniform** if this quotient is noncompact.

## Finiteness is retained

Both uniform and nonuniform lattices have finite covolume. A discrete subgroup with infinite covolume is not a nonuniform lattice: it is not a lattice at all.

For \(G=\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb C)\), compactness is equivalent to compactness of \(\Gamma\backslash\mathbb H^3\). [[lie-groups/bianchi-group-nonuniform-lattice|Bianchi groups]] give nonuniform lattices.
