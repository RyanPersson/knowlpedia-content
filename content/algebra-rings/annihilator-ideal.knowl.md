Let \(R\) be a ring and let \(S\subseteq R\) be a [[shared-foundations/subset|subset]]. The **left annihilator** of \(S\) is
\[
\operatorname{Ann}_\ell(S)=\{r\in R : rs=0 \text{ for all } s\in S\},
\]
and the **right annihilator** is
\[
\operatorname{Ann}_r(S)=\{r\in R:sr=0\text{ for all }s\in S\}.
\]
The left annihilator is a left [[algebra-rings/ideal|ideal]], the right annihilator is a right ideal, and they coincide when \(R\) is commutative.

## Remarks

In a commutative ring, a nonzero element is a zero divisor exactly when its annihilator is nonzero. Annihilators also record which scalars kill elements or subsets of a module.

## Examples

- In \(\mathbb Z/6\mathbb Z\), the annihilator of the class of \(2\) is the ideal generated by the class of \(3\).
- In an integral domain, the annihilator of every nonzero element is \(\{0\}\).
- In \(k[x,y]/(xy)\), the annihilator of the class of \(x\) is generated by the class of \(y\).
