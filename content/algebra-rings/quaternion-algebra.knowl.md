Let \(F\) be a field of characteristic different from two. A **quaternion algebra** over \(F\) is an associative unital \(F\)-[[algebra-modules/algebra-over-ring|algebra]] isomorphic to
\[
(a,b)_F=F\oplus Fi\oplus Fj\oplus Fij,\qquad a,b\in F^\times,
\]
whose multiplication is determined by \(i^2=a\), \(j^2=b\), and \(ij=-ji\). The four displayed elements form an \(F\)-basis. The pair \((a,b)\) is a presentation, not an invariant unique to the algebra.

## Examples

The real algebra \((-1,-1)_{\mathbb R}\) is the [[linear-algebra/quaternion-division-algebra|Hamilton quaternion algebra]]. Quaternion algebras need not be division algebras: the matrix algebra \(M_2(F)\) is the [[algebra-rings/split-quaternion-algebra|split example]].

## Scope

This presentation is sufficient over number fields. Characteristic two uses different defining relations and is outside this knowl's convention.
