A **ring monomorphism** is a [[algebra-rings/ring-homomorphism|ring homomorphism]] \(\varphi:R\to S\) that is [[shared-foundations/injective-function|injective]] as a function.

## Remarks

Such a map identifies \(R\) with the subring \(\varphi(R)\subseteq S\). Equivalently, its [[algebra-rings/kernel-ring|kernel]] is zero.

## Examples

- The inclusion \(\mathbb Z\hookrightarrow\mathbb Q\) is a ring monomorphism.
- The inclusion \(k[x]\hookrightarrow k[x,y]\) is a ring monomorphism.
- The reduction map \(\mathbb Z\to\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\) is not a monomorphism for \(n\ge2\).
