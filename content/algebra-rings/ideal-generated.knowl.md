Given a [[shared-foundations/subset|subset]] \(A\subseteq R\) of a [[algebra-rings/ring|ring]] \(R\), the **two-sided ideal generated by \(A\)**, denoted \((A)\), is the smallest [[algebra-rings/ideal|two-sided ideal]] of \(R\) containing \(A\). Equivalently, \((A)\) consists of all finite sums
\[
\sum_{i=1}^n r_i a_i s_i
\]
with \(n\ge 0\), \(a_i\in A\), and \(r_i,s_i\in R\); the sum for \(n=0\) is \(0\). If \(R\) is commutative, one may take every \(s_i=1\).

## Remarks

When \(A=\{a\}\) is a singleton and \(R\) is commutative, \((A)\) is a [[algebra-rings/principal-ideal|principal ideal]].

## Examples

- In \(\mathbb Z\), the ideal generated by \(\{6,10\}\) is \((2)\).
- In \(k[x,y]\), the ideal generated by \(\{x,y\}\) is \((x,y)\).
- In \(M_2(k)\), the two-sided ideal generated by a nonzero matrix is all of \(M_2(k)\).
