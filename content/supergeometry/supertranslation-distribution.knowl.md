Let \(\mathbb M^{V|S}\) be
[[supergeometry/super-minkowski-space|super-Minkowski space]] determined by a
supertranslation bracket
\(\Gamma:\operatorname{Sym}^2S\to V\). Its **supertranslation
distribution** \(\mathcal D\) is the rank \(0|{\dim S}\) distribution spanned
by the odd [[lie-groups/left-invariant-vector-field|left-invariant vector fields]] corresponding to \(S\).

The Levi bracket of \(\mathcal D\) is induced by \(\Gamma\):
\[
\mathcal D\otimes\mathcal D
\longrightarrow
T\mathbb M/\mathcal D,
\qquad
(s,t)\longmapsto\Gamma(s,t).
\]
Thus \(\mathcal D\) is nonintegrable whenever \(\Gamma\ne0\). If \(\Gamma\) is
surjective, brackets of its odd sections generate every even translation
direction, so \(\mathcal D\) is bracket-generating.

This distribution is additional structure on the underlying affine
[[supergeometry/supermanifold|supermanifold]]. It records the
[[mathematical-physics/supertranslation-algebra|supertranslation algebra]]
geometrically.
