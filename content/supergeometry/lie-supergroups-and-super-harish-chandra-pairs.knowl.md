There is an equivalence of categories
\[
\left\{
\begin{array}{c}
\text{finite-dimensional smooth}\\[-2pt]
\text{real Lie supergroups}
\end{array}
\right\}
\simeq
\left\{
\begin{array}{c}
\text{finite-dimensional smooth real}\\[-2pt]
\text{super Harish--Chandra pairs}
\end{array}
\right\}.
\]
The forward functor sends a [[supergeometry/lie-supergroup|Lie supergroup]]
\(G\) to
\[
\left(G_{\mathrm{red}},\operatorname{Lie}(G)\right),
\]
with the conjugation action of \(G_{\mathrm{red}}\) on its
[[supergeometry/lie-superalgebra-of-lie-supergroup|Lie superalgebra]].

Conversely, a [[supergeometry/super-harish-chandra-pair|super
Harish–Chandra pair]] \((G_0,\mathfrak g)\) determines a Lie supergroup whose
reduced group is \(G_0\) and whose infinitesimal superalgebra is
\(\mathfrak g\). One sheaf-level construction uses
\[
\mathcal O_G(U)
=\operatorname{Hom}_{U(\mathfrak g_{\bar0})}
  \bigl(U(\mathfrak g),C^\infty(U)\bigr)
\]
with the compatible \(G_0\)-action and Hopf-superalgebra structure.

## Exact scope

This theorem concerns the finite-dimensional smooth real
Berezin–Leites/Kostant category and even morphisms. Analogous equivalences
exist in complex-analytic and algebraic settings under their appropriate
hypotheses, but those are distinct statements. The equivalence also explains
why a Lie superalgebra by itself does not choose the global topology of the
reduced Lie group.
