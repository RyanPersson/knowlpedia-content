Let \(k\) be a field of characteristic different from \(2\), and let \(V\) be a
[[supergeometry/super-vector-space|super vector space]]. Its **symmetric
superalgebra** is
\[
\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathrm s}(V)
=T(V)\Big/\left\langle
v\otimes w-(-1)^{|v||w|}w\otimes v
\right\rangle,
\]
where the relations range over homogeneous \(v,w\) and
\(T(V)\) is the [[algebra-modules/tensor-algebra|tensor algebra]]. It is the
free [[supergeometry/supercommutative-algebra|supercommutative algebra]]
generated by \(V\).

## Even and odd generators

Writing \(V=V_{\bar0}\oplus V_{\bar1}\), multiplication induces an isomorphism
of superalgebras
\[
\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathrm s}(V)
\cong
\operatorname{Sym}(V_{\bar0})\otimes\Lambda(V_{\bar1}).
\]
Even generators commute and produce the ordinary
[[algebra-modules/symmetric-algebra|symmetric algebra]], whereas odd generators
anticommute and produce the [[algebra-modules/exterior-algebra|exterior
algebra]]. In particular, the square of each odd generator vanishes.

## Universal property

For every supercommutative algebra \(A\), restriction to \(V\) gives a natural
bijection
\[
\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{sCAlg}_k}
  (\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathrm s}(V),A)
\cong
\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{SuperVect}_k}(V,A).
\]
The maps on the right are even linear maps. This universal property, rather
than a choice of bases, characterizes the construction.

## Relation to Lie superalgebras

The symmetric superalgebra appears as the associated graded algebra in the
[[lie-groups/pbw-theorem|Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt theorem]] for the
[[supergeometry/universal-enveloping-algebra-of-lie-superalgebra|universal
enveloping algebra of a Lie superalgebra]].
