**Density of \(\mathbb{R}\setminus\mathbb{Q}\) in \(\mathbb{R}\)**: If \(a<b\) are [[shared-foundations/real-numbers|real numbers]], then there exists an irrational number \(x\in\mathbb{R}\setminus\mathbb{Q}\) such that
\(a<x<b.\)

## Remarks

This shows that irrationals are just as ubiquitous as rationals and prevents "gaps" of irrationality; it is often used in constructing sequences with prescribed properties.
