Let \((R,\mathfrak m)\) be a [[algebra-commutative/local-ring|local ring]]. The **residue field** of \(R\) is
\[
k(R):=R/\mathfrak m.
\]
It is a [[algebra-rings/field|field]] because \(\mathfrak m\) is [[algebra-commutative/maximal-ideal-local-ring|maximal]].

The canonical surjection
\[
R \twoheadrightarrow R/\mathfrak m
\]
is sometimes called the **residue map**.

More generally, for a prime ideal \(\mathfrak p\subset R\), the **residue field at \(\mathfrak p\)** is
\[
\kappa(\mathfrak p):=R_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak pR_{\mathfrak p},
\]
where \(R_{\mathfrak p}\) is the [[algebra-commutative/localization-at-prime|localization at \(\mathfrak p\)]]. This is the residue field of the local ring \(R_{\mathfrak p}\).

## Examples

1. **\(\mathbb Z_{(p)}\).** Its maximal ideal is \(p\mathbb Z_{(p)}\), and
   \(
   \mathbb Z_{(p)}/p\mathbb Z_{(p)}\cong \mathbb F_p.
   \)

2. **\(k[x]_{(x)}\).** Its maximal ideal is \((x)\), so
   \(
   k[x]_{(x)}/(x)\ \cong\ k
   \)
   via evaluation at \(x=0\).

3. **\(k[x,y]_{(x,y)}\).** Its maximal ideal is generated by \(x\) and \(y\), and its residue field is \(k\).
