Let \(A\) be a [[algebra-commutative/noetherian-ring|Noetherian ring]] and \(f\in A\). If a prime ideal \(\mathfrak p\) is minimal among the prime ideals containing \((f)\), then
\[
\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak p)\le 1.
\]

Here \(\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak p)\) is the [[algebra-commutative/height-of-prime|height]] of \(\mathfrak p\).

## Geometric form

In the [[algebra-commutative/prime-spectrum|prime spectrum]] \(\operatorname{Spec}(A)\), every irreducible component of \(V(f)\) has codimension at most \(1\).

## Generalization

Krull's height theorem states that if an ideal \(I\) can be generated by \(r\) elements, then every prime ideal minimal over \(I\) has height at most \(r\).

## Example

For a [[algebra-rings/field|field]] \(k\), the prime ideals \((x)\) and \((y)\) are minimal over \((xy)\) in \(k[x,y]\), and each has height \(1\).
