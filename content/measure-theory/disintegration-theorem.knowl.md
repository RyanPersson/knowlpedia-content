Let \(p:X\to Y\) be measurable between [[measure-theory/standard-borel-space|standard Borel spaces]], let \(\mu\) be a Borel probability on \(X\), and let \(\nu=p_*\mu\). There is a [[measure-theory/probability-kernel|probability kernel]] \(y\mapsto\mu_y\), unique for \(\nu\)-almost every \(y\), such that \(\mu_y(p^{-1}\{y\})=1\) almost everywhere and
\[
\int_X f\,d\mu=\int_Y\left(\int_X f\,d\mu_y\right)d\nu(y)
\]
for every bounded Borel \(f\). It is called the **disintegration of \(\mu\) over \(p\)**.

## Conditional expectation

The function \(x\mapsto\int f\,d\mu_{p(x)}\) is a version of conditional expectation onto the sigma-algebra generated by \(p\). Work on Borel models before completing measures; the component measures can be singular with respect to \(\mu\), so they need not extend to every set in its completion simultaneously.
