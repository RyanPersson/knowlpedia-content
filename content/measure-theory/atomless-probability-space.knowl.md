An **atomless probability space** is a [[probability/probability-space|probability space]] with no [[measure-theory/atom-of-measure|atoms]]. Equivalently, every positive-probability measurable set contains a measurable subset of strictly smaller positive probability.

## Examples

Uniform probability on an interval and standard Gaussian probability on \(\mathbb R^d\), \(d\geq1\), are atomless. Merely checking that singletons have measure zero is not a substitute for this definition on arbitrary measurable spaces.
