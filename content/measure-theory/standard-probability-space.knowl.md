A **standard probability space** is a probability space that, [[measure-theory/measure-space-isomorphism|modulo null sets]], is the [[measure-theory/measure-completion|completion]] of a Borel probability measure on a [[measure-theory/standard-borel-space|standard Borel space]].

## Why the hypothesis appears

This regularity permits concrete measurable models, disintegration, and classification statements such as [[ergodic-theory/ergodic-decomposition-theorem|ergodic decomposition]] and the [[ergodic-theory/halmos-von-neumann-theorem|Halmos–von Neumann theorem]]. It is not a hypothesis in the elementary fixed-function criterion for ergodicity.
