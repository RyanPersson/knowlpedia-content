A **reductive dual pair** in a [[lie-groups/symplectic-group|symplectic group]] \(\operatorname{Sp}(W)\) is a pair of reductive subgroups \(G,H\) such that each is the full [[algebra-groups/centralizer|centralizer]] of the other in \(\operatorname{Sp}(W)\).
