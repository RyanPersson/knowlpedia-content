In §6, the **\(C^*\)-algebra of field observables** \(\mathfrak A\) is the operator-norm closure of the \(*\)-algebra generated by the Weyl unitaries \(\exp(iR(z))\) based on finite-dimensional subspaces. As a concrete \(C^*\)-algebra, it is a norm-closed \(*\)-subalgebra of bounded operators and satisfies \(\|A^*A\|=\|A\|^2\).

## Remarks

- \(\mathfrak A\) is abstractly independent of the chosen representation.
- Each symplectic transformation \(T\) induces a \(*\)-automorphism \(\theta(T)\) of \(\mathfrak A\).
