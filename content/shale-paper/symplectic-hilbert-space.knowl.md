A **symplectic Hilbert space** is a real [[linear-algebra/hilbert-space|Hilbert space]] \(K\) equipped with a continuous [[shale-paper/symplectic-form|symplectic form]] \(B:K\times K\to\mathbb R\); thus \(B\) is bilinear, skew-symmetric, and nondegenerate.

## Shale's setting

If \(H\) is a complex Hilbert space, its underlying real Hilbert space \(H_{\mathbb R}\) becomes symplectic with
\[
B(z_1,z_2)=\operatorname{Im}\langle z_1,z_2\rangle.
\]
Its bounded real-linear symplectic automorphisms form [[shale-paper/symplectic-group-spk|\(\operatorname{Sp}(K)\)]].
