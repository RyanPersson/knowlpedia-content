Let \(M\) be a [[algebra-modules/module|module]] over a [[algebra-rings/commutative-ring|commutative ring]] \(R\). Its **symmetric algebra** is
\[
\operatorname{Sym}_R(M)
=T_R(M)\big/\langle x\otimes y-y\otimes x:x,y\in M\rangle,
\]
where \(T_R(M)\) is the [[algebra-modules/tensor-algebra|tensor algebra]]. It is a commutative \(R\)-algebra graded by tensor degree, and its degree-one part receives the canonical map \(M\to\operatorname{Sym}_R(M)\).

## Universal property

For every commutative unital \(R\)-algebra \(A\) and every \(R\)-linear map \(f:M\to A\), there is a unique unital \(R\)-algebra homomorphism
\[
\widetilde f:\operatorname{Sym}_R(M)\longrightarrow A
\]
extending \(f\). This characterizes \(\operatorname{Sym}_R(M)\) as the free commutative \(R\)-algebra on \(M\).

## Examples

If \(M\) is free with basis \(x_1,\ldots,x_n\), then
\[
\operatorname{Sym}_R(M)\cong R[x_1,\ldots,x_n].
\]
For a vector space \(V\), the degree-\(d\) summand is its \(d\)th symmetric power \(\operatorname{Sym}^d(V)\).
