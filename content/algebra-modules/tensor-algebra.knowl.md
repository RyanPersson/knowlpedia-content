Let \(M\) be a [[algebra-modules/module|module]] over a commutative [[algebra-rings/ring|ring]] \(R\). Its **tensor algebra** is the graded \(R\)-module
\[
T_R(M)=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}M^{\otimes_R n},
\qquad M^{\otimes_R0}=R,
\]
with multiplication given by concatenation of tensors. It is a unital associative [[algebra-modules/algebra-over-ring|\(R\)-algebra]].

## Universal property

For every unital associative \(R\)-algebra \(A\) and every \(R\)-linear map \(f:M\to A\), there is a unique unital \(R\)-algebra homomorphism
\[
\widetilde f:T_R(M)\longrightarrow A
\]
whose restriction to the degree-one summand \(M\) is \(f\). Thus \(T_R(M)\) is the free unital associative \(R\)-algebra generated by \(M\).

## Quotients

Many familiar algebras impose relations on this free algebra. The [[algebra-modules/symmetric-algebra|symmetric algebra]] kills commutators, the [[algebra-modules/exterior-algebra|exterior algebra]] kills squares of generators, and a [[differential-geometry/clifford-algebra|Clifford algebra]] imposes quadratic Clifford relations.
