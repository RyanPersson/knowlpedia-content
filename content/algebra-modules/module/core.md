Let $R$ be a [[algebra-rings/ring|ring]] (often assumed a
[[algebra-rings/unital-ring|unital ring]]). A **left $R$-module** is an abelian
group $(M,+)$ together with a scalar multiplication map $R\times M\to M$,
$(r,m)\mapsto rm$, satisfying the axioms below.

A **right $R$-module** is defined similarly with a map $M\times R\to M$,
$(m,r)\mapsto mr$, satisfying the analogous axioms.

The axioms are collected in [[algebra-modules/module-axioms|module axioms]].
