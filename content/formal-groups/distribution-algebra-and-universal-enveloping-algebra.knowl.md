Let \(k\) be a field of characteristic zero and let \(G\) be a
finite-dimensional formally smooth affine [[formal-groups/formal-group|formal
group]] over \(k\), with tangent Lie algebra
\(\mathfrak g=\operatorname{Lie}(G)\). Inclusion of the primitive
distributions extends uniquely to a canonical isomorphism of filtered Hopf
algebras
\[
U(\mathfrak g)
\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
\operatorname{Dist}(G),
\]
where \(U(\mathfrak g)\) is the
[[lie-groups/universal-enveloping-algebra|universal enveloping algebra]] and
\(\operatorname{Dist}(G)\) is the
[[formal-groups/distribution-algebra|distribution algebra]] at the identity.

## Associated-graded proof

Formal smoothness identifies the associated graded coalgebra and algebra of
finite-order distributions with
\[
\operatorname{gr}\operatorname{Dist}(G)
\cong
\operatorname{Sym}_k(\mathfrak g).
\]
The [[lie-groups/pbw-theorem|Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt theorem]] gives the same
associated graded algebra for the standard filtration on \(U(\mathfrak g)\).
The canonical map is the identity in filtration degree one and induces the
symmetric-algebra isomorphism on associated gradeds, so it is an isomorphism.

## Characteristic dependence

Characteristic zero is essential. In characteristic \(p>0\), divided-power
distributions of higher order generally are not generated by primitive
order-one distributions, and
\[
U(\operatorname{Lie}(G))\longrightarrow\operatorname{Dist}(G)
\]
need not be surjective. This is one manifestation of the
[[formal-groups/positive-characteristic-warning|failure of tangent
classification in positive characteristic]].
