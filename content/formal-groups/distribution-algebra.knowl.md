Let \(k\) be a field and let \(G=\operatorname{Spf}(A)\) be a
finite-dimensional formally smooth affine [[formal-groups/formal-group|formal
group]] over \(k\) whose pointed formal scheme is a [[formal-groups/formal-affine-space|formal disc]]. Write
\(\varepsilon:A\to k\) for the identity section and
\(\mathfrak m=\ker\varepsilon\). The **distribution algebra of \(G\)** is
\[
\operatorname{Dist}(G)
:=
\bigcup_{n\geq0}\operatorname{Dist}_{\leq n}(G),
\qquad
\operatorname{Dist}_{\leq n}(G)
:=
\operatorname{Hom}_k(A/\mathfrak m^{n+1},k).
\]
Its multiplication is convolution against the comultiplication of the
[[formal-groups/coordinate-hopf-algebra|coordinate Hopf algebra]]:
\[
(u*v)(a)=(u\otimes v)\bigl(\Delta(a)\bigr).
\]

## Why convolution is defined

An element of \(\operatorname{Dist}_{\leq n}(G)\) is a linear functional on
\(A\) that vanishes on \(\mathfrak m^{n+1}\); it is a distribution of order at
most \(n\) supported at the identity. Continuity of
\(\Delta:A\to A\widehat\otimes A\) and the augmentation filtration imply
\[
\operatorname{Dist}_{\leq r}(G)*
\operatorname{Dist}_{\leq s}(G)
\subseteq
\operatorname{Dist}_{\leq r+s}(G).
\]
Hence convolution is a well-defined filtered associative product, with unit
\(\varepsilon\).

Dualizing multiplication on the finite quotients gives a cocommutative
coalgebra structure on \(\operatorname{Dist}(G)\), and the antipode of \(A\)
dualizes to an antipode. Thus \(\operatorname{Dist}(G)\) is a filtered
cocommutative [[algebra-coalgebras/hopf-algebra|Hopf algebra]]. It can be
noncommutative: multiplication in \(\operatorname{Dist}(G)\) is dual to the
possibly noncocommutative comultiplication of \(A\).

## Primitive distributions and the Lie bracket

A distribution \(D\) is **primitive** when
\[
\Delta_{\operatorname{Dist}}(D)
=D\otimes1+1\otimes D.
\]
Equivalently, as a functional on \(A\),
\[
D(ab)=D(a)\varepsilon(b)+\varepsilon(a)D(b).
\]
Such functionals are derivations at the identity and form the [[differential-geometry/tangent-space|tangent space]]
\[
\operatorname{Lie}(G)
\cong(\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2)^\vee.
\]
Their commutator for convolution is the
[[formal-groups/tangent-lie-algebra|tangent Lie bracket]]. The universal
property therefore gives a filtered Hopf-algebra homomorphism
\[
U(\operatorname{Lie}(G))
\longrightarrow\operatorname{Dist}(G).
\]

In characteristic zero, the canonical map displayed above is an isomorphism
by the separate
[[formal-groups/distribution-algebra-and-universal-enveloping-algebra|distribution–enveloping-algebra
theorem]]. In positive characteristic,
higher divided-power distributions need not be generated by primitive
elements.

## Terminology

This algebra is also called the **hyperalgebra** of \(G\). Its elements are
algebraic finite-order functionals supported at the identity, not
[[functional-analysis/distribution|Schwartz distributions]] on a manifold and not all continuous linear
functionals on \(A\).
