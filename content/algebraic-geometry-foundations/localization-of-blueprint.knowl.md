Let \(B=A/\!/\mathcal R\) be a [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/blueprint|blueprint]] and let \(S\subseteq A\) be multiplicatively closed. The **localization** \(S^{-1}B\) has underlying monoid \(S^{-1}A\), and its pre-addition is generated by the images of the relations in \(\mathcal R\).

It is characterized by the usual universal property: a blueprint morphism \(f:B\to C\) factors uniquely through \(S^{-1}B\) exactly when every \(f(s)\), \(s\in S\), is a unit of \(C^\bullet\).

## Fractions and relations

Elements are represented by fractions \(a/s\). An additive relation between fractions holds when, after multiplying by a suitable common denominator, it is induced by a relation of \(B\). This simultaneously localizes the multiplicative monoid and transports the formal additive structure.

For \(h\in B\), one writes \(B[h^{-1}]\) for localization at \(\{1,h,h^2,\ldots\}\). These principal localizations define the basic open charts used in [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/spectrum-of-blueprint|blueprint spectra]].
