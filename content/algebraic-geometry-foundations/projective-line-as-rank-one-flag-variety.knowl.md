Let \(k\) be a field and let \(B\) be the split upper-triangular
[[algebraic-geometry-foundations/borel-subgroup|Borel subgroup]] over \(k\).
The orbit map that sends a matrix to the line spanned by its first column
induces
\[
SL_2/B_{SL_2}\;\simeq\;\mathbb P^1_k
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
PGL_2/B_{PGL_2}\;\simeq\;\mathbb P^1_k.
\]
Thus the [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/projective-line|projective line]] is
the complete [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/flag-variety|flag variety]] in
type \(A_1\).

## What this does and does not identify

The displayed isomorphisms concern homogeneous spaces. They do not identify
\(\mathbb P^1\) with either group. Over \(\mathbb C\), \(PGL_2(\mathbb C)\)
also acts as the holomorphic automorphism group of the Riemann sphere, while
in Langlands duality \(PGL_2\) occurs as the dual group of \(SL_2\). These are
different roles of the same algebraic group.
