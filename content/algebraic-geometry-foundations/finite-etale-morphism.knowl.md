A morphism of [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/scheme|schemes]] is **finite étale** if it is both a [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/finite-morphism|finite morphism]] and an [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/etale-morphism|étale morphism]].

Affine-locally, \(\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A\) is finite étale exactly when \(B\) is a finite, flat, finitely presented \(A\)-algebra and [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/relative-kahler-differentials|\(\Omega_{B/A}=0\)]]. Equivalently, \(B\) is a finite projective \(A\)-module and is unramified over \(A\).

For a field extension \(K/F\),
\[
\operatorname{Spec}K\longrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}F
\]
is finite étale exactly when \(K/F\) is finite and [[algebra-fields-galois/separable-extension|separable]].
