Under the fully faithful realization of
[[algebra-hyperstructures/band|bands]] as
[[algebraic-geometry-foundations/ordered-blueprint|ordered blueprints]], an
[[algebra-hyperstructures/idyll|idyll]] \(B\) becomes a purely positive
[[algebraic-geometry-foundations/ordered-blue-field|ordered blue field]] with
[[algebraic-geometry-foundations/ordered-blueprint-with-unique-weak-inverses|unique weak inverses]]
and ambient semiring
\[
B^+=\mathbb N[B^\times].
\]
Its order is generated by
\[
0\leq\sum a_i
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\sum a_i\in N_B.
\]

Conversely, an ordered blue field with these properties determines an idyll:
take its pointed multiplicative monoid as \(B\) and declare a formal sum null
exactly when \(0\leq\sum a_i\). These constructions are inverse on objects
and morphisms.
