For a homomorphism of [[algebra-rings/commutative-ring|commutative rings]] \(A\to B\), the **module of relative Kähler differentials** \(\Omega_{B/A}\) is the \(B\)-module equipped with an \(A\)-derivation
\[
d:B\longrightarrow \Omega_{B/A}
\]
that is universal: every \(A\)-derivation from \(B\) to a \(B\)-module \(M\) factors uniquely through a \(B\)-linear map \(\Omega_{B/A}\to M\).

## Concrete presentation

The module \(\Omega_{B/A}\) is generated by symbols \(db\), subject to
\[
d(b+b')=db+db',\qquad d(bb')=b\,db'+b'\,db,\qquad da=0\quad(a\in A).
\]

For example, \(\Omega_{A[x]/A}\) is a free \(A[x]\)-module with basis \(dx\). If \(B=A[x]/(f)\), then the relation \(df=f'(x)\,dx=0\) is imposed after passing to \(B\).

## Geometric meaning

For a [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/morphism-of-schemes|morphism of schemes]] \(f:Y\to X\), these affine modules glue to the **sheaf of relative Kähler differentials** \(\Omega_{Y/X}\). On an affine piece \(\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A\), it is the sheaf associated to \(\Omega_{B/A}\). Thus \(\Omega_{Y/X}=0\) precisely when this sheaf is zero on every affine piece.

The fiber of \(\Omega_{Y/X}\) at a point is dual to the relative tangent directions there. Its vanishing is therefore the infinitesimal part of being [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/unramified-morphism|unramified]].
