Let \(A\) be a [[algebra-groups/commutative-monoid|commutative monoid with zero]]. Its **monoid blueprint** is
\[
A^{\mathrm{blpr}}=A/\!/\mathcal R_{\min},
\]
where \(\mathcal R_{\min}\) is the smallest [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/pre-addition-on-a-monoid|pre-addition]] on \(A\): apart from the blueprint axioms, it imposes no additive relations.

This construction defines a fully faithful functor
\[
\mathbf{CMon}_0\hookrightarrow\mathbf{Blpr}.
\]
A blueprint morphism between two such objects has no additional additive relations to check, so it is exactly a multiplicative map preserving \(0\) and \(1\).

The [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/semiring-completion-of-a-blueprint|semiring completion]] of \(A^{\mathrm{blpr}}\) is the free [[algebra-rings/commutative-semiring|commutative semiring]] generated by the monoid \(A\), with the monoid zero identified with the additive zero. It is not an addition law on \(A\).
