For \(\theta\in\mathbb R\), the **noncommutative two-torus** \(A_\theta\) is the [[operator-algebras/cstar-algebra-generators-relations|universal unital \(C^*\)-algebra]] generated by unitaries \(u,v\) subject to
\[
vu=e^{2\pi i\theta}uv.
\]
For irrational \(\theta\), it is also called the **irrational rotation algebra**.

## Construction from a circle rotation

For \(T_\theta(x)=x+\theta\), let \(\alpha(f)=f\circ T_\theta^{-1}\) on \(C(\mathbb T)\), and let \(v(x)=e^{2\pi ix}\). The covariance relation \(uvu^*=\alpha(v)=e^{-2\pi i\theta}v\) gives the defining commutation rule. Thus
\[
A_\theta\cong C(\mathbb T)\rtimes_\alpha\mathbb Z.
\]
The group \(\mathbb Z\) is amenable, so full and reduced crossed products agree.

## Completions and the commutative value

For \(\theta=0\), the generators commute and \(A_0\cong C(\mathbb T^2)\). The von Neumann algebra \(L^\infty(\mathbb T)\rtimes\mathbb Z\) is a different completion, using the Haar tracial representation. For irrational \(\theta\), essential freeness and ergodicity make it a type \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor; it is the [[operator-algebras/hyperfinite-ii-one-factor|hyperfinite \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor]].
