\(\operatorname{Spin}(3,\mathbb C)\) is the [[lie-groups/spin-group|spin group]] in the even [[differential-geometry/clifford-algebra|Clifford algebra]] of \(\mathbb C^{3}\) with [[linear-algebra/quadratic-form|quadratic form]] \(Q(v)=\sum_jv_j^2\) and convention \(v^2=-Q(v)1\). It is generated by products of an even number of vectors satisfying \(Q(v)=1\).

## Dimensions and structure

This group has real dimension \(6\), complex dimension \(3\). Conjugation on the generating [[linear-algebra/vector-space|vector space]] gives a two-to-one homomorphism to \(\mathrm{SO}(3,\mathbb C)\) with kernel \(\{\pm1\}\). For dimension at least three it is connected and [[topology/simply-connected-space|simply connected]], so the covering is universal.

Its [[lie-groups/complex-lie-group|complex Lie group]] structure uses holomorphic multiplication and inversion. Forgetting the complex structure gives the [[lie-groups/underlying-real-lie-group|underlying real Lie group]]; real and holomorphic homomorphisms belong to different categories.

Clifford conjugation gives a two-sheeted covering with kernel {±1}. See [[catalog/lie-groups/so-3-c|\(\operatorname{SO}(3,\mathbb C)\)]].

The complex spin group in dimension three is SL(2,C); its vector action gives the two-to-one covering of SO(3,C). See [[lie-groups/sl2-complex-as-real-and-complex-lie-group|\(\operatorname{SL}(2,\mathbb C)\)]].
