The group \(E_8(\mathbb C)_{\mathrm{sc}}\) is the connected [[topology/simply-connected-space|simply connected]] complex [[fiber-bundles/lie-group|Lie group]] whose [[lie-groups/lie-algebra|Lie algebra]] is the complex [[lie-groups/simple-lie-algebra|simple Lie algebra]] of [[lie-groups/dynkin-diagram|Dynkin type]] \(E_8\). The simply connected global form is part of this definition.

## Dimensions and structure

This group has real dimension \(496\), complex dimension \(248\). Its rank is 8. Its center is trivial, so the simply connected and adjoint forms coincide. The compact, split real, and complex entries are separate objects.

Its [[lie-groups/complex-lie-group|complex Lie group]] structure uses holomorphic multiplication and inversion. Forgetting the complex structure gives the [[lie-groups/underlying-real-lie-group|underlying real Lie group]]; real and holomorphic homomorphisms belong to different categories.
