the real [[fiber-bundles/lie-group|Lie group]] \(\mathbb T^{2}\) is \(U(1)^{2}\) with coordinatewise multiplication; equivalently it is \(\mathbb R^{2}/(2\pi\mathbb Z)^{2}\) with addition.

## Dimensions and structure

This group has real dimension \(2\). It is compact, connected and abelian. Its [[lie-groups/universal-covering-group|universal covering group]] is additive \(\mathbb R^{2}\). The complex multiplicative group has a different real dimension and is noncompact.

## Direct verification

Coordinatewise multiplication and conjugation preserve the unit-circle equations. Each circle contributes one real dimension; the exponential map from Rⁿ has kernel 2πZⁿ.
