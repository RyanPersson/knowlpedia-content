\(\operatorname{Spin}(5,\mathbb C)\) is the [[lie-groups/spin-group|spin group]] generated by even products of vectors of squared length 1 in the [[differential-geometry/clifford-algebra|Clifford algebra]] of \(\mathbb C^{5}\) with relation \(v^2=-\sum_jv_j^2\). Its conjugation action on vectors is the standard double covering of \(\mathrm{SO}(5,\mathbb C)\).

## Dimensions and structure

This group has real dimension \(20\), complex dimension \(10\). Its tangent algebra is the skew-symmetric \(5\times5\) matrices. The group is [[topology/simply-connected-space|simply connected]].

Its [[lie-groups/complex-lie-group|complex Lie group]] structure uses holomorphic multiplication and inversion. Forgetting the complex structure gives the [[lie-groups/underlying-real-lie-group|underlying real Lie group]]; real and holomorphic homomorphisms belong to different categories.

The complex spin group in dimension five is Sp(4,C), where 4 is the complex matrix size. See [[catalog/lie-groups/sp-4-c|\(\mathrm{Sp}(4,\mathbb C)\)]].
