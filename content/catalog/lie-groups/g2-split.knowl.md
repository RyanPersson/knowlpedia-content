The catalogue \({G_2}^{\mathrm{split}}_{\mathrm{ad},0}\) is the connected adjoint real [[fiber-bundles/lie-group|Lie group]]
\[ {G_2}^{\mathrm{split}}_{\mathrm{ad},0}=\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathbb R\text{-Lie}}(\mathfrak g_2^{\mathrm{split}})^0, \]
where [[catalog/lie-algebras/g2-split|\(\mathfrak g_2^{\mathrm{split}}\)]] is the split real [[lie-groups/simple-lie-algebra|simple Lie algebra]] of Dynkin type \(G_2\) and the superscript 0 denotes the [[lie-groups/identity-component-of-a-lie-group|identity component]]. This definition fixes the global form, rather than only its local [[lie-groups/lie-algebra|Lie algebra]].

## Dimensions and structure

This group has real dimension \(14\). Its real rank is 2. It is centerless and noncompact. The familiar Killing-signature notation is \(G_{2(2)}\), but that notation alone does not specify an adjoint group, a finite central cover, or a topological [[topology/universal-cover|universal cover]]. The catalogue uses the connected adjoint form throughout the split exceptional lane.
