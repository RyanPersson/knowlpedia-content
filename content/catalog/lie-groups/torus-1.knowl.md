the real [[fiber-bundles/lie-group|Lie group]] \(\mathbb T^{1}\) is \(U(1)^{1}\) with coordinatewise multiplication; equivalently it is \(\mathbb R^{1}/(2\pi\mathbb Z)^{1}\) with addition.

## Dimensions and structure

This group has real dimension \(1\). It is compact, connected and abelian. Its [[lie-groups/universal-covering-group|universal covering group]] is additive \(\mathbb R^{1}\). The complex multiplicative group has a different real dimension and is noncompact.

The one-dimensional torus is the circle group by its definition. See [[lie-groups/example-u1-circle|\(\operatorname{U}(1)\)]].

## Direct verification

Coordinatewise multiplication and conjugation preserve the unit-circle equations. Each circle contributes one real dimension; the exponential map from Rⁿ has kernel 2πZⁿ.
