\(\operatorname{Spin}(3,\mathbb R)\) is the [[lie-groups/spin-group|spin group]] in the even [[differential-geometry/clifford-algebra|Clifford algebra]] of \(\mathbb R^{3}\) with [[linear-algebra/quadratic-form|quadratic form]] \(Q(v)=\sum_jv_j^2\) and convention \(v^2=-Q(v)1\). It is generated by products of an even number of vectors satisfying \(Q(v)=1\).

## Dimensions and structure

This group has real dimension \(3\). Conjugation on the generating [[linear-algebra/vector-space|vector space]] gives a two-to-one homomorphism to \(\mathrm{SO}(3,\mathbb R)\) with kernel \(\{\pm1\}\). For dimension at least three it is connected and [[topology/simply-connected-space|simply connected]], so the covering is universal.

Clifford conjugation gives a two-sheeted covering with kernel {±1}. See [[lie-groups/example-so3|\(\operatorname{SO}(3,\mathbb R)\)]].

The compact spin group in dimension three is isomorphic to SU(2), with the spin map corresponding to the adjoint double cover of SO(3). See [[lie-groups/example-su2|\(\operatorname{SU}(2)\)]].
