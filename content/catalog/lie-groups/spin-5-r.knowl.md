\(\operatorname{Spin}(5,\mathbb R)\) is the [[lie-groups/spin-group|spin group]] generated by even products of vectors of squared length 1 in the [[differential-geometry/clifford-algebra|Clifford algebra]] of \(\mathbb R^{5}\) with relation \(v^2=-\sum_jv_j^2\). Its conjugation action on vectors is the standard double covering of \(\mathrm{SO}(5,\mathbb R)\).

## Dimensions and structure

This group has real dimension \(10\). Its tangent algebra is the skew-symmetric \(5\times5\) matrices. The group is [[topology/simply-connected-space|simply connected]].

The compact spin group in dimension five is the compact quaternionic group Sp(2). See [[catalog/lie-groups/sp-2|\(\operatorname{Sp}(2)\)]].
