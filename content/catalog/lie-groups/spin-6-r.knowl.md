\(\operatorname{Spin}(6,\mathbb R)\) is the [[lie-groups/spin-group|spin group]] generated by even products of vectors of squared length 1 in the [[differential-geometry/clifford-algebra|Clifford algebra]] of \(\mathbb R^{6}\) with relation \(v^2=-\sum_jv_j^2\). Its conjugation action on vectors is the standard double covering of \(\mathrm{SO}(6,\mathbb R)\).

## Dimensions and structure

This group has real dimension \(15\). Its tangent algebra is the skew-symmetric \(6\times6\) matrices. The group is [[topology/simply-connected-space|simply connected]].

The compact spin group in dimension six is SU(4). See [[catalog/lie-groups/su-4|\(\operatorname{SU}(4)\)]].
