\(\operatorname{SO}(1,\mathbb R)\) is the [[lie-groups/special-orthogonal-group|special orthogonal group]]
\[ \operatorname{SO}(1,\mathbb R)=\{A\in M_{1}(\mathbb R):A^{\mathsf T}A=I,\ \det A=1\}, \]
with matrix multiplication. The preserved form is the positive-definite real Euclidean form.

## Dimensions and structure

This group has real dimension \(0\). Its tangent algebra consists of skew-symmetric matrices, \(X^{\mathsf T}+X=0\). The determinant-one subgroup is trivial.

The determinant-one subgroup includes by the identity on matrices. See [[catalog/lie-groups/o-1-r|\(\operatorname{O}(1,\mathbb R)\)]].
