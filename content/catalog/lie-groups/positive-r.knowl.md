The positive real multiplicative [[fiber-bundles/lie-group|Lie group]] is \(\mathbb R_{>0}\) with multiplication, identity 1, and inversion \(x\mapsto x^{-1}\).

## Dimensions and structure

This group has real dimension \(1\). Logarithm identifies it with the additive real line.

The logarithm is a smooth [[algebra-groups/group-isomorphism|group isomorphism]], since \(\log(xy)=\log x+\log y\), with inverse the real exponential. See [[catalog/lie-groups/additive-1-r|\((\mathbb R^{1},+)\)]].

## Direct verification

Multiplication is smooth on the positive real numbers, inversion is x ↦ 1/x, and logarithm is a smooth group isomorphism to the additive real line.
