\(\operatorname{Spin}(2,\mathbb R)\) is the [[lie-groups/spin-group|spin group]] in the even [[differential-geometry/clifford-algebra|Clifford algebra]] of \(\mathbb R^{2}\) with [[linear-algebra/quadratic-form|quadratic form]] \(Q(v)=\sum_jv_j^2\) and convention \(v^2=-Q(v)1\). It is generated by products of an even number of vectors satisfying \(Q(v)=1\).

## Dimensions and structure

This group has real dimension \(1\). Conjugation on the generating [[linear-algebra/vector-space|vector space]] gives a two-to-one homomorphism to \(\mathrm{SO}(2,\mathbb R)\) with kernel \(\{\pm1\}\). In dimension two it is isomorphic to \(U(1)\). The double cover is a squaring map after identifying both groups with that torus; this cover is not universal.

Clifford conjugation gives a two-sheeted covering with kernel {±1}. See [[catalog/lie-groups/so-2-r|\(\operatorname{SO}(2,\mathbb R)\)]].

The even Clifford algebra in dimension two identifies its norm-one subgroup with the circle in the real case, and with C* in the complex case. Under the same identification of SO(2), the [[topology/covering-space|covering map]] is squaring. See [[lie-groups/example-u1-circle|\(\operatorname{U}(1)\)]].

## Two-dimensional calculation

Writing \(e_1^2=e_2^2=-1\), the even Clifford algebra has basis \(1,e_1e_2\) and \((e_1e_2)^2=-1\). Its spin elements have the form \(s=a+b e_1e_2\) with \(a^2+b^2=1\). Over the reals, \(s\mapsto a+ib\) identifies the group with the circle. Over the complex numbers the same formula identifies it with \(\mathbb C^\times\): its inverse uses \(a=(z+z^{-1})/2\) and \(b=(z-z^{-1})/(2i)\). Conjugating the two basis vectors shows that the orthogonal action corresponds to \(z\mapsto z^2\).
