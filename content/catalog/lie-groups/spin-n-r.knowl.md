For every integer \(n\ge1\), \(\operatorname{Spin}(n,\mathbb R)\) is the [[lie-groups/spin-group|spin group]] in the even [[differential-geometry/clifford-algebra|Clifford algebra]] of \(\mathbb R^{n}\) with [[linear-algebra/quadratic-form|quadratic form]] \(Q(v)=\sum_jv_j^2\) and convention \(v^2=-Q(v)1\). It is generated by products of an even number of vectors satisfying \(Q(v)=1\).

## Dimensions and structure

This group has real dimension \(n(n-1)/2\). Conjugation on the generating [[linear-algebra/vector-space|vector space]] gives a two-to-one homomorphism to \(\mathrm{SO}(n,\mathbb R)\) with kernel \(\{\pm1\}\). It is disconnected at n=1, connected at n>=2, and [[topology/simply-connected-space|simply connected]] at n>=3; the two-dimensional double cover is not universal.

Clifford conjugation gives a two-sheeted covering with kernel {±1}. See [[lie-groups/special-orthogonal-group|\(\operatorname{SO}(n,\mathbb R)\)]].
