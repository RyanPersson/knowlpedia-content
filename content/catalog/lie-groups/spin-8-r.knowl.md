\(\operatorname{Spin}(8,\mathbb R)\) is the [[lie-groups/spin-group|spin group]] generated by even products of vectors of squared length 1 in the [[differential-geometry/clifford-algebra|Clifford algebra]] of \(\mathbb R^{8}\) with relation \(v^2=-\sum_jv_j^2\). Its conjugation action on vectors is the standard double covering of \(\mathrm{SO}(8,\mathbb R)\).

## Dimensions and structure

This group has real dimension \(28\). Its tangent algebra is the skew-symmetric \(8\times8\) matrices. The group is [[topology/simply-connected-space|simply connected]]. Dimension eight is the setting for [[lie-groups/spin8-triality|triality]]; the vector and two [[lie-groups/half-spin-representation|half-spin representations]] live naturally on the spin group.
