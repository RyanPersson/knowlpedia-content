The **split real [[lie-groups/lie-algebra|Lie algebra]] of type E8** is the real span of a [[lie-groups/chevalley-basis|Chevalley basis]] of [[lie-groups/exceptional-lie-algebra-e8|\(\mathfrak{e}_{8}(\mathbb C)\)]], with the inherited bracket.

## Construction and dimensions

The real dimension is 248 and the real split rank is 8. The basis has 8 Cartan directions and 240 root directions. Tensoring with \(\mathbb C\) recovers the complex exceptional algebra. Different compatible Chevalley-basis choices give isomorphic real [[lie-groups/lie-algebra|Lie algebras]].

The common signature notation is \(\mathfrak{e}_{8(8)}\); the parenthesized number is the number of positive minus negative Killing-form directions. It is not the dimension.
