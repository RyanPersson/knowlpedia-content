The **2-dimensional abelian complex [[lie-groups/lie-algebra|Lie algebra]]** is \(\mathbb C^{2}\) with its usual vector-space operations and identically zero bracket:

\[
[x,y]=0\qquad\text{for all }x,y.
\]

## Morphisms

Every complex-linear map to another abelian complex [[lie-groups/lie-algebra|Lie algebra]] preserves the bracket. Hence endomorphisms form \(M_{2}(\mathbb C)\) and automorphisms are the invertible matrices. A homomorphism from an arbitrary Lie algebra to this object must kill every commutator.
