The **compact real Lie algebra of type E7** is the [[lie-groups/compact-real-form|compact real form]] of [[lie-groups/exceptional-lie-algebra-e7|\(\mathfrak{e}_{7}(\mathbb C)\)]]. Equivalently it is a real [[lie-groups/lie-algebra|Lie algebra]] whose complexification is that simple complex algebra and whose [[lie-groups/killing-form|Killing form]] is negative definite. It is unique up to real [[lie-groups/lie-algebra-isomorphism|Lie algebra isomorphism]].

## Construction and dimensions

In a compatibly normalized root basis, a concrete model is the real span of

\[
i h_j,\qquad e_\alpha-e_{-\alpha},\qquad i(e_\alpha+e_{-\alpha})\quad(\alpha>0).
\]

Its real dimension is 133; its complexification has complex rank 7. This compact real object is distinct from the split real form and from the underlying real algebra of the complex form.
