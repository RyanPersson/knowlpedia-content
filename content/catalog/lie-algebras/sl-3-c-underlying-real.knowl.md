The **underlying real [[lie-groups/lie-algebra|Lie algebra]]** \(\mathfrak{sl}(3,\mathbb C)_{\mathbb R}\) has the same trace-zero complex matrices and commutator bracket as [[lie-groups/complex-lie-algebra-sl3|\(\mathfrak{sl}(3,\mathbb C)\)]], but only real scalar multiplication is part of its structure.

## Retained and forgotten structure

Its real dimension is 16. Multiplication by \(i\) is an additional real-linear operator \(J\) with \(J^2=-1\) and \([JX,Y]=J[X,Y]=[X,JY]\). A real-linear homomorphism is complex-linear precisely when it commutes with \(J\).

This operation differs from taking a real form: \(\mathfrak{sl}(3,\mathbb R)\) has only 8 real dimensions.
