The **1-dimensional abelian real [[lie-groups/lie-algebra|Lie algebra]]** is \(\mathbb R^{1}\) with its usual vector-space operations and identically zero bracket:

\[
[x,y]=0\qquad\text{for all }x,y.
\]

## Morphisms

Every real-linear map to another abelian real [[lie-groups/lie-algebra|Lie algebra]] preserves the bracket. Hence endomorphisms form \(M_{1}(\mathbb R)\) and automorphisms are the invertible matrices. A homomorphism from an arbitrary Lie algebra to this object must kill every commutator.
