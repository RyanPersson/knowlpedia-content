The **split real [[lie-groups/lie-algebra|Lie algebra]] of type F4** is the real span of a [[lie-groups/chevalley-basis|Chevalley basis]] of [[lie-groups/exceptional-lie-algebra-f4|\(\mathfrak{f}_{4}(\mathbb C)\)]], with the inherited bracket.

## Construction and dimensions

The real dimension is 52 and the real split rank is 4. The basis has 4 Cartan directions and 48 root directions. Tensoring with \(\mathbb C\) recovers the complex exceptional algebra. Different compatible Chevalley-basis choices give isomorphic real [[lie-groups/lie-algebra|Lie algebras]].

The common signature notation is \(\mathfrak{f}_{4(4)}\); the parenthesized number is the number of positive minus negative Killing-form directions. It is not the dimension.
