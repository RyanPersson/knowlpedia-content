The **real exceptional [[lie-groups/lie-algebra|Lie algebra]]** \(\mathfrak{e}_{7(-25)}\) is the noncompact real form of [[lie-groups/exceptional-lie-algebra-e7|\(\mathfrak{e}_{7}(\mathbb C)\)]] characterized, up to real [[lie-groups/lie-algebra-isomorphism|Lie algebra isomorphism]], by a maximal compact subalgebra isomorphic to \(\mathfrak e_6^{\mathrm{compact}}\oplus\mathfrak{so}(2)\). Its real dimension is 133.

## Signature convention and magic square

The signature convention is positive minus negative Killing-form directions. In a Cartan decomposition \(\mathfrak g=\mathfrak k\oplus\mathfrak p\) it equals \(\dim\mathfrak p-\dim\mathfrak k=133-2\cdot79=-25\). This labels a real form, not its complex dimension. The complexification is the simple complex type \(E_7\) algebra.

It occurs among the singly split composition-algebra magic-square outputs. Some sources use the opposite sign for this signature; specifying the maximal compact subalgebra removes that ambiguity.
