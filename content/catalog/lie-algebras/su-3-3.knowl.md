The **indefinite special unitary Lie algebra** \(\mathfrak{su}(3,3)\) is the real [[lie-groups/lie-algebra|Lie algebra]]

\[
\mathfrak{su}(3,3)=\{X\in M_6(\mathbb C):X^*I_{3,3}+I_{3,3}X=0,\ \operatorname{tr}X=0\},\qquad I_{3,3}=\operatorname{diag}(I_3,-I_3).
\]


Its bracket is \([X,Y]=XY-YX\) and scalar multiplication is over \(\mathbb R\).

## Dimension

Multiplication by \(I_{3,3}\) identifies the skew-adjoint equation with the usual skew-Hermitian equation. Its trace is purely imaginary, so trace zero imposes one real condition on 36 parameters. The real dimension is therefore 35.
