The **first Janko group** \(J_1\) is the group with the following complete [[algebra-groups/group-presentation|presentation]]. With \(c=abab^{-1}\) as an abbreviation for a word, set
\[
J_1=\left\langle a,b\;\middle|\;
\begin{aligned}
a^2&=b^3=(ab)^7=1,\\
(abc^3)^5&=1,\\
\bigl(abc^6abab(ab^{-1})^2\bigr)^2&=1
\end{aligned}\right\rangle.
\]
Here \(c\) is not an additional generator. Every displayed relation is part of the definition.

## Order and structure

This is a nonabelian finite [[algebra-groups/simple-group|simple group]] of order
\[
|J_1|=175560=2^3\cdot3\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11\cdot19.
\]
It is one of the six sporadic groups in the pariah display block. The labels \(J_1\), [[catalog/finite-groups/sporadic/j3|\(J_3\)]], and [[catalog/finite-groups/sporadic/j4|\(J_4\)]] name distinct sporadic groups, rather than the first members of a family defined for every integer subscript.

## A relationship to the O'Nan group

The [[catalog/finite-groups/sporadic/on|O'Nan group]] contains a maximal subgroup isomorphic to \(J_1\), of index \(2624832\). This gives an injective [[algebra-groups/group-homomorphism|group homomorphism]] \(J_1\hookrightarrow\mathrm{O'N}\); its image is proper.
