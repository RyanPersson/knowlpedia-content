The **Mathieu group** \(M_{22}\) is the [[algebra-groups/generated-subgroup|subgroup generated]] by the following [[algebra-groups/permutation-cycle|permutations]] in the [[algebra-groups/symmetric-group|symmetric group]] \(S_{22}\):
\[
\begin{aligned}a&=(1\,13)(2\,8)(3\,16)(4\,12)\\
&\quad{}(6\,22)(7\,17)(9\,10)(11\,14),\\
b&=(1\,22\,3\,21)(2\,18\,4\,13)(5\,12)(6\,11\,7\,15)\\
&\quad{}(8\,14\,20\,10)(17\,19).
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus \(M_{22}=\langle a,b\rangle\), with permutation composition as the group operation. Cycles list successive images, unlisted points are fixed, and products act from right to left.

## Order and structure

This is a nonabelian finite [[algebra-groups/simple-group|simple group]], with
\[
|M_{22}|=443520=2^{7}\cdot3^{2}\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11.
\]
It belongs to the Mathieu display block of the sporadic groups. This grouping records the ATLAS organization, without asserting a direct embedding into another displayed group.

## The natural permutation action

The action on \(22\) points is \(3\)-transitive: any ordered \(3\)-tuple of distinct points can be carried to any other such tuple.

The orbit of \(\{1,2,3,10,18,20\}\) is a collection of \(77\) blocks of size \(6\). Every \(3\)-element subset lies in exactly one block, so these blocks form a Steiner system \(S(3,6,22)\). The generators preserve the system.

The natural [[catalog/finite-groups/sporadic/mathieu-point-stabilizers|point stabilizers]] connect the Mathieu groups.
