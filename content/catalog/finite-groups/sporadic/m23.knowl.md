The **Mathieu group** \(M_{23}\) is the [[algebra-groups/generated-subgroup|subgroup generated]] by the following [[algebra-groups/permutation-cycle|permutations]] in the [[algebra-groups/symmetric-group|symmetric group]] \(S_{23}\):
\[
\begin{aligned}a&=(1\,2)(3\,4)(7\,8)(9\,10)\\
&\quad{}(13\,14)(15\,16)(19\,20)(21\,22),\\
b&=(1\,16\,11\,3)(2\,9\,21\,12)(4\,5\,8\,23)(6\,22\,14\,18)\\
&\quad{}(13\,20)(15\,17).
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus \(M_{23}=\langle a,b\rangle\), with permutation composition as the group operation. Cycles list successive images, unlisted points are fixed, and products act from right to left.

## Order and structure

This is a nonabelian finite [[algebra-groups/simple-group|simple group]], with
\[
|M_{23}|=10200960=2^{7}\cdot3^{2}\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11\cdot23.
\]
It belongs to the Mathieu display block of the sporadic groups. This grouping records the ATLAS organization, without asserting a direct embedding into another displayed group.

## The natural permutation action

The action on \(23\) points is \(4\)-transitive: any ordered \(4\)-tuple of distinct points can be carried to any other such tuple.

The orbit of \(\{1,2,3,4,5,11,17\}\) is a collection of \(253\) blocks of size \(7\). Every \(4\)-element subset lies in exactly one block, so these blocks form a Steiner system \(S(4,7,23)\). The generators preserve the system.

The natural [[catalog/finite-groups/sporadic/mathieu-point-stabilizers|point stabilizers]] connect the Mathieu groups.
