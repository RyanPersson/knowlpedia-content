The **Mathieu group** \(M_{11}\) is the [[algebra-groups/generated-subgroup|subgroup generated]] by the following [[algebra-groups/permutation-cycle|permutations]] in the [[algebra-groups/symmetric-group|symmetric group]] \(S_{11}\):
\[
\begin{aligned}a&=(2\,10)(4\,11)(5\,7)(8\,9),\\
b&=(1\,4\,3\,8)(2\,5\,6\,9).
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus \(M_{11}=\langle a,b\rangle\), with permutation composition as the group operation. Cycles list successive images, unlisted points are fixed, and products act from right to left.

## Order and structure

This is a nonabelian finite [[algebra-groups/simple-group|simple group]], with
\[
|M_{11}|=7920=2^{4}\cdot3^{2}\cdot5\cdot11.
\]
It belongs to the Mathieu display block of the sporadic groups. This grouping records the ATLAS organization, without asserting a direct embedding into another displayed group.

## The natural permutation action

The action on \(11\) points is \(4\)-transitive: any ordered \(4\)-tuple of distinct points can be carried to any other such tuple. The element doing so is unique; this is called sharp transitivity.

The orbit of \(\{1,2,3,4,5\}\) is a collection of \(66\) blocks of size \(5\). Every \(4\)-element subset lies in exactly one block, so these blocks form a Steiner system \(S(4,5,11)\). The generators preserve the system.

The natural [[catalog/finite-groups/sporadic/mathieu-point-stabilizers|point stabilizers]] connect the Mathieu groups.
