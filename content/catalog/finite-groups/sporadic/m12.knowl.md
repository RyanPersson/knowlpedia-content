The **Mathieu group** \(M_{12}\) is the [[algebra-groups/generated-subgroup|subgroup generated]] by the following [[algebra-groups/permutation-cycle|permutations]] in the [[algebra-groups/symmetric-group|symmetric group]] \(S_{12}\):
\[
\begin{aligned}a&=(1\,4)(3\,10)(5\,11)(6\,12),\\
b&=(1\,8\,9)(2\,3\,4)(5\,12\,11)(6\,10\,7).
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus \(M_{12}=\langle a,b\rangle\), with permutation composition as the group operation. Cycles list successive images, unlisted points are fixed, and products act from right to left.

## Order and structure

This is a nonabelian finite [[algebra-groups/simple-group|simple group]], with
\[
|M_{12}|=95040=2^{6}\cdot3^{3}\cdot5\cdot11.
\]
It belongs to the Mathieu display block of the sporadic groups. This grouping records the ATLAS organization, without asserting a direct embedding into another displayed group.

## The natural permutation action

The action on \(12\) points is \(5\)-transitive: any ordered \(5\)-tuple of distinct points can be carried to any other such tuple. The element doing so is unique; this is called sharp transitivity.

The orbit of \(\{1,2,3,4,5,9\}\) is a collection of \(132\) blocks of size \(6\). Every \(5\)-element subset lies in exactly one block, so these blocks form a Steiner system \(S(5,6,12)\). The generators preserve the system.

The natural [[catalog/finite-groups/sporadic/mathieu-point-stabilizers|point stabilizers]] connect the Mathieu groups.
