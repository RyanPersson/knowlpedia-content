The **fourth Janko group** \(J_4\) is the [[algebra-groups/generated-subgroup|subgroup generated]] by the fixed pair of \(112\times112\) [[linear-algebra/matrix|matrices]] \(A,B\) in dataset `J4G1-f2r112B0`:
\[
J_4=\langle A,B\rangle\leq\mathrm{GL}_{112}(\mathbb F_2).
\]
The files `J4G1-f2r112B0.m1` and `J4G1-f2r112B0.m2` specify \(A\) and \(B\), respectively; their entries are part of this concrete definition. Here \(\mathbb F_2=\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\) is the [[algebra-fields-galois/finite-field|field with two elements]], \(\mathrm{GL}_{112}(\mathbb F_2)\) consists of invertible matrices, and the group operation is matrix multiplication over that field. The exact generator files are linked in References.

## Order and structure

This group is a nonabelian finite [[algebra-groups/simple-group|simple group]], with
\[
|J_4|=86775571046077562880
=2^{21}\cdot3^3\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11^3\cdot23\cdot29\cdot31\cdot37\cdot43.
\]
It belongs to the pariah display block of sporadic groups. The [[catalog/finite-groups/sporadic/j1|first Janko group]] and [[catalog/finite-groups/sporadic/j3|third Janko group]] are different objects, with different orders.

## Using the concrete model

The realization acts faithfully on \(\mathbb F_2^{112}\). Its matrix degree \(112\) is distinct from the order of the group. The fixed pair uses the type I generator convention. The defining data are the specified matrices; conditions on the orders of two unspecified generators do not replace those data.
