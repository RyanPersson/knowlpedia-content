The **Mathieu group** \(M_{24}\) is the [[algebra-groups/generated-subgroup|subgroup generated]] by the following [[algebra-groups/permutation-cycle|permutations]] in the [[algebra-groups/symmetric-group|symmetric group]] \(S_{24}\):
\[
\begin{aligned}a&=(1\,4)(2\,7)(3\,17)(5\,13)\\
&\quad{}(6\,9)(8\,15)(10\,19)(11\,18)\\
&\quad{}(12\,21)(14\,16)(20\,24)(22\,23),\\
b&=(1\,4\,6)(2\,21\,14)(3\,9\,15)(5\,18\,10)\\
&\quad{}(13\,17\,16)(19\,24\,23).
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus \(M_{24}=\langle a,b\rangle\), with permutation composition as the group operation. Cycles list successive images, unlisted points are fixed, and products act from right to left.

## Order and structure

This is a nonabelian finite [[algebra-groups/simple-group|simple group]], with
\[
|M_{24}|=244823040=2^{10}\cdot3^{3}\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11\cdot23.
\]
It belongs to the Mathieu display block of the sporadic groups. This grouping records the ATLAS organization, without asserting a direct embedding into another displayed group.

## The natural permutation action

The action on \(24\) points is \(5\)-transitive: any ordered \(5\)-tuple of distinct points can be carried to any other such tuple.

The orbit of \(\{1,2,3,4,5,11,17,24\}\) is a collection of \(759\) blocks of size \(8\). Every \(5\)-element subset lies in exactly one block, so these blocks form a Steiner system \(S(5,8,24)\). The generators preserve the system.

The natural [[catalog/finite-groups/sporadic/mathieu-point-stabilizers|point stabilizers]] connect the Mathieu groups.
