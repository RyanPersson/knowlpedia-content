The **Hall–Janko group** \(J_2\), also written \(\mathrm{HJ}\), is the group with [[algebra-groups/group-presentation|presentation]]
\[
J_2=\langle a,b\mid a^2=b^3=(ab)^7=[a,b]^{12}=w^6=1\rangle,
\qquad w=ababab^{-1}abab^{-1},
\]
where the [[algebra-groups/commutator|commutator convention]] is \([a,b]=a^{-1}b^{-1}ab\). These are defining relations for the group, not just conditions for recognizing generators in an already known group.

## Order and a permutation model

This is a nonabelian [[algebra-groups/simple-group|simple group]] of order
\[
|J_2|=604\,800=2^7\cdot3^3\cdot5^2\cdot7.
\]
A [[algebra-groups/faithful-action|faithful action]] on 100 points is specified by the pair `J2G1-p100B0.g1`, `J2G1-p100B0.g2`. A point stabilizer has three orbits of sizes \(1,36,63\).

## Geometry and neighboring groups

The Hall–Janko graph has 100 vertices and valency 36. Its full automorphism group is \(J_2:2\); \(J_2\) is the simple subgroup of index two. The graph is not uniquely specified by its strongly regular parameters alone.

The group \(J_2\) embeds in the [[catalog/finite-groups/sporadic/suz|sporadic Suzuki group]]: \(\mathrm{Suz}\) has a maximal subgroup \(J_2:2\), containing \(J_2\) with index two. Thus the embedded \(J_2\) itself is not maximal there.
