The **finite matrix group \(\mathrm{GL}_2(3)\)** consists of [[linear-algebra/matrix-inverse|invertible]] \(2\times2\) matrices over the [[catalog/arithmetic/f3|field \(\mathbb F_3\)]], with matrix multiplication.

## Order and nonsimplicity

The independent-column count gives \((3^2-1)(3^2-3)=48\) elements. The determinant maps onto \(\mathbb F_3^\times\cong C_2\), with kernel \(\mathrm{SL}_2(3)\) of order 24. This proper nontrivial [[algebra-groups/normal-subgroup|normal subgroup]] proves nonsimplicity.

## Determinant quotient

Identifying \(\mathbb F_3^\times=\{1,-1\}\) with the additive group \(C_2\), the determinant is a surjective homomorphism \(\mathrm{GL}_2(3)\to C_2\). Its kernel is \(\mathrm{SL}_2(3)\).
