The **alternating group \(A_1\)** consists of the [[shared-foundations/permutation-sign|even]] [[shared-foundations/finite-permutation|permutations]] of \(\{1\}\), with composition as multiplication. The unique permutation has sign \(+1\), so this extends the convention to one letter.

## Order and simplicity

Its order is \(1\). There is only the identity; the trivial group is not [[algebra-groups/simple-group|simple]].
