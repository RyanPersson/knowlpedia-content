the **cyclic group \(C_{1}\)** is the [[algebra-groups/group|group]] \(\mathbb Z/1\mathbb Z\) of residue classes, with addition modulo \(1\).

## Order and simplicity

It has \(1\) elements, generated by the residue class of \(1\). Every subgroup is cyclic, and every subgroup is normal because the group is abelian. Its only element is the identity, so it is not [[algebra-groups/simple-group|simple]].

## Catalogue convention

This is an additive group object. The ring with the same residue classes has additional multiplication, and belongs to different categories. The [[algebra-groups/finite-cyclic-isomorphic-zn|cyclic-group classification]] identifies every cyclic group of this order with this model.
