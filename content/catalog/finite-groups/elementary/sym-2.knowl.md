The **symmetric group \(S_2\)** is the [[algebra-groups/group|group]] of all [[shared-foundations/bijective-function|bijections]] of \(\{1,\ldots,2\}\), with composition as multiplication.

## Order and normal subgroup

There are 2 permutations: choose the images of the 2 letters successively. Its nonidentity permutation is the transposition (12), so it is cyclic of prime order and simple.
