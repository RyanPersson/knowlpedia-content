The **finite matrix group \(\mathrm{SL}_2(3)\)** consists of [[linear-algebra/determinant|determinant-one]] \(2\times2\) matrices over the [[catalog/arithmetic/f3|field \(\mathbb F_3\)]], with matrix multiplication.

## Order and nonsimplicity

The determinant divides the [[catalog/finite-groups/elementary/gl-2-3|general linear group]] order by \(3-1\), giving \(24\) elements. The scalar matrices \(\{I,-I\}\) form a proper nontrivial center, so this group is not simple.
