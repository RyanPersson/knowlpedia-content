The **alternating group \(A_7\)** consists of the [[shared-foundations/permutation-sign|even]] [[shared-foundations/finite-permutation|permutations]] of \(\{1,\ldots,7\}\), with composition as multiplication.

## Order and simplicity

Its order is \(2520\). It is nonabelian [[algebra-groups/simple-group|simple]], as holds for [[algebra-groups/alternating-group|alternating groups]] of every degree at least five.
