the **cyclic group \(C_{3}\)** is the [[algebra-groups/group|group]] \(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z\) of residue classes, with addition modulo \(3\).

## Order and simplicity

It has \(3\) elements, generated by the residue class of \(1\). Every subgroup is cyclic, and every subgroup is normal because the group is abelian. A group of prime order has no proper nontrivial subgroup by Lagrange’s theorem, so this group is [[algebra-groups/simple-group|simple]].

## Catalogue convention

This is an additive group object. The ring with the same residue classes has additional multiplication, and belongs to different categories. The [[algebra-groups/finite-cyclic-isomorphic-zn|cyclic-group classification]] identifies every cyclic group of this order with this model.
