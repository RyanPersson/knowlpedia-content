The **symmetric group \(S_6\)** is the [[algebra-groups/group|group]] of all [[shared-foundations/bijective-function|bijections]] of \(\{1,\ldots,6\}\), with composition as multiplication.

## Order and normal subgroup

There are 720 permutations: choose the images of the 6 letters successively. The even permutations form the proper nontrivial [[algebra-groups/normal-subgroup|normal subgroup]] \(A_6\), the kernel of the sign map. Therefore \(S_6\) is not simple.
