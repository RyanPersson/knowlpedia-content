The **alternating group \(A_3\)** consists of the [[shared-foundations/permutation-sign|even]] [[shared-foundations/finite-permutation|permutations]] of \(\{1,\ldots,3\}\), with composition as multiplication.

## Order and simplicity

Its order is \(3\). Its elements are the identity, \((123)\), and \((132)\). Thus it is cyclic of prime order and simple.
