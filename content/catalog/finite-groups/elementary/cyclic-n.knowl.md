For an integer \(n\geq1\), the **cyclic group \(C_{n}\)** is the [[algebra-groups/group|group]] \(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\) of residue classes, with addition modulo \(n\).

## Order and simplicity

It has \(n\) elements, generated by the residue class of \(1\). Every subgroup is cyclic, and every subgroup is normal because the group is abelian. It is [[algebra-groups/simple-group|simple]] exactly when \(n\) is prime. If \(n>1\) is composite, a proper divisor gives a proper nontrivial subgroup. The case \(n=1\) is the trivial group and is excluded from simplicity.

## Catalogue convention

This is an additive group object. The ring with the same residue classes has additional multiplication, and belongs to different categories. The [[algebra-groups/finite-cyclic-isomorphic-zn|cyclic-group classification]] identifies every cyclic group of this order with this model.
