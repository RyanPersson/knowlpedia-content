The **symmetric group \(S_1\)** is the [[algebra-groups/group|group]] of all [[shared-foundations/bijective-function|bijections]] of \(\{1\}\), with composition as multiplication.

## Order and normal subgroup

There are 1 permutations: choose the images of the 1 letters successively. This group is trivial and hence not simple.
