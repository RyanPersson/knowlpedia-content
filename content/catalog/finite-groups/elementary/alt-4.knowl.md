The **alternating group \(A_4\)** consists of the [[shared-foundations/permutation-sign|even]] [[shared-foundations/finite-permutation|permutations]] of \(\{1,\ldots,4\}\), with composition as multiplication.

## Order and simplicity

Its order is \(12\). The subgroup \(\{1,(12)(34),(13)(24),(14)(23)\}\) is a normal [[catalog/finite-groups/elementary/klein-4|Klein four-group]], so \(A_4\) is not simple.
