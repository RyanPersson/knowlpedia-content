The **Tits group** \({}^2F_4(2)'\) is the [[algebra-groups/commutator-subgroup|commutator subgroup]] of [[catalog/finite-groups/lie-type/ree-f4-2|\({}^2F_4(2)\)]]:
\[
 {}^2F_4(2)'=\langle aba^{-1}b^{-1}:a,b\in {}^2F_4(2)\rangle.
\]
Its multiplication is inherited from the specified [[algebra-groups/finite-group|finite group]]. This definition uses the full group at its exceptional small parameter before taking the commutator subgroup.

## Order and simplicity

Its order is
\[ |{}^2F_4(2)'|=17971200. \]

It is a nonabelian [[algebra-groups/simple-group|simple group]]. The exact order is 17,971,200.

## Identification and index

It is a [[algebra-groups/normal-subgroup|normal subgroup]] of index \(2\) in \({}^2F_4(2)\). This is the Tits group. Its order factors as \(2^{11}3^3 5^2 13\).
 It is a special group at the boundary of the exceptional Lie-type families. It is not counted among the 26 sporadic simple groups.
