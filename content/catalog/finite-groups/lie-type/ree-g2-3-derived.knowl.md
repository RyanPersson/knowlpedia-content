\({}^2G_2(3)'\) is the [[algebra-groups/commutator-subgroup|commutator subgroup]] of [[catalog/finite-groups/lie-type/ree-g2-3|\({}^2G_2(3)\)]]:
\[
 {}^2G_2(3)'=\langle aba^{-1}b^{-1}:a,b\in {}^2G_2(3)\rangle.
\]
Its multiplication is inherited from the specified [[algebra-groups/finite-group|finite group]]. This definition uses the full group at its exceptional small parameter before taking the commutator subgroup.

## Order and simplicity

Its order is
\[ |{}^2G_2(3)'|=504. \]

It is a nonabelian [[algebra-groups/simple-group|simple group]]. The exact order is 504.

## Identification and index

It is a [[algebra-groups/normal-subgroup|normal subgroup]] of index \(3\) in \({}^2G_2(3)\). There is an exceptional isomorphism \({}^2G_2(3)'\cong\operatorname{PSL}_2(8)\).
