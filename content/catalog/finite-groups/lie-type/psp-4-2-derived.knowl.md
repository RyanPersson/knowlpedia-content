\(\operatorname{PSp}_4(2)'\) is the [[algebra-groups/commutator-subgroup|commutator subgroup]] of [[catalog/finite-groups/lie-type/psp-4-2|\(\operatorname{PSp}_{4}(2)\)]]:
\[
 \operatorname{PSp}_4(2)'=\langle aba^{-1}b^{-1}:a,b\in \operatorname{PSp}_{4}(2)\rangle.
\]
Its multiplication is inherited from the specified [[algebra-groups/finite-group|finite group]]. This definition uses the full group at its exceptional small parameter before taking the commutator subgroup.

## Order and simplicity

Its order is
\[ |\operatorname{PSp}_4(2)'|=360. \]

It is a nonabelian [[algebra-groups/simple-group|simple group]]. The exact order is 360.

## Identification and index

It is a [[algebra-groups/normal-subgroup|normal subgroup]] of index \(2\) in \(\operatorname{PSp}_{4}(2)\). Under \(\operatorname{PSp}_4(2)\cong S_6\), its derived subgroup corresponds to \(A_6\).
