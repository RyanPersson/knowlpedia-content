For \(q=2\), \(G_2(2)\) is the [[catalog/finite-groups/lie-type/finite-chevalley-central-quotient|finite Chevalley central quotient]] of type \(G_2\):
\[
 G_2(2)=\mathbf G_{G_2,\mathrm{sc}}(\mathbb F_{2})/Z\bigl(\mathbf G_{G_2,\mathrm{sc}}(\mathbb F_{2})\bigr).
\]
Here \(\mathbf G_{G_2,\mathrm{sc}}\) is the split simply connected [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/algebraic-group|algebraic group]] of the displayed root-system type. Its finite points are fixed by coordinatewise \(q\)-power Frobenius, and multiplication is multiplication of cosets of their finite center.

## Order and simplicity

Its order is
\[ |G_2(2)|=12096. \]

It is not simple: its [[algebra-groups/commutator-subgroup|commutator subgroup]] has index two and is isomorphic to PSU(3,3). The exact order is 12,096.

## Rank and central quotient

The absolute rank is \(2\). The finite center of the simply connected group has order \(1\); the notation here always denotes the quotient by that center. The value \(q=2\) defines a group of order 12,096, but only its derived subgroup \(G_2(2)'\) is simple, of order 6,048. The simple-family tile therefore starts at \(q=3\).
