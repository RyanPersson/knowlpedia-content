For \(q=2\), \(E_6(2)\) is the [[catalog/finite-groups/lie-type/finite-chevalley-central-quotient|finite Chevalley central quotient]] of type \(E_6\):
\[
 E_6(2)=\mathbf G_{E_6,\mathrm{sc}}(\mathbb F_{2})/Z\bigl(\mathbf G_{E_6,\mathrm{sc}}(\mathbb F_{2})\bigr).
\]
Here \(\mathbf G_{E_6,\mathrm{sc}}\) is the split simply connected [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/algebraic-group|algebraic group]] of the displayed root-system type. Its finite points are fixed by coordinatewise \(q\)-power Frobenius, and multiplication is multiplication of cosets of their finite center.

## Order and simplicity

Its order is
\[ |E_6(2)|=214841575522005575270400. \]

It is a nonabelian [[algebra-groups/simple-group|simple group]]. The exact order is 214,841,575,522,005,575,270,400.

## Rank and central quotient

The absolute rank is \(6\). The finite center of the simply connected group has order \(\gcd(3,q-1)\); the notation here always denotes the quotient by that center. There are no omitted small-field simplicity exceptions for this type.
