\(G_2(2)'\) is the [[algebra-groups/commutator-subgroup|commutator subgroup]] of [[catalog/finite-groups/lie-type/g2-2|\(G_2(2)\)]]:
\[
 G_2(2)'=\langle aba^{-1}b^{-1}:a,b\in G_2(2)\rangle.
\]
Its multiplication is inherited from the specified [[algebra-groups/finite-group|finite group]]. This definition uses the full group at its exceptional small parameter before taking the commutator subgroup.

## Order and simplicity

Its order is
\[ |G_2(2)'|=6048. \]

It is a nonabelian [[algebra-groups/simple-group|simple group]]. The exact order is 6,048.

## Identification and index

It is a [[algebra-groups/normal-subgroup|normal subgroup]] of index \(2\) in \(G_2(2)\). There is an exceptional isomorphism \(G_2(2)'\cong\operatorname{PSU}_3(3)\).
