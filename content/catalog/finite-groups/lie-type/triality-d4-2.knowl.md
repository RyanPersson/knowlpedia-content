For the fixed value \(q=2\), \({}^3D_4(2)\) is the central quotient of the [[catalog/finite-groups/lie-type/steinberg-fixed-point-group|Steinberg fixed-point group]]
\[
 {}^3D_4(2)=\mathbf G_{D_4,\mathrm{sc}}^F/Z(\mathbf G_{D_4,\mathrm{sc}}^F),\qquad F=\gamma F_q.
\]
The [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/algebraic-group|algebraic group]] is split and simply connected over \(\overline{\mathbb F}_p\), where \(q\) is a power of \(p\). The pinned diagram automorphism \(\gamma\) has order \(3\) and cyclically permutes the three outer nodes of the \(D_4\) diagram. It commutes with coordinatewise Frobenius \(F_q\), so \(F^3=F_{q^3}\). Multiplication is multiplication of cosets of the finite center.

## Order and simplicity

Its order is
\[ |{}^3D_4(2)|=211341312. \]

It is a nonabelian [[algebra-groups/simple-group|simple group]] for every admitted parameter. The exact order is 211,341,312.

## Field and center convention

Here \(q\) names the graph–Frobenius parameter; some sources write \(q^3\) in the group name instead. The absolute rank is \(4\). The finite fixed-point group has trivial center.
