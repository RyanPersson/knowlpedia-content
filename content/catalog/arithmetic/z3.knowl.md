The **\(3\)-adic integer ring** is the closed unit ball
\[
\mathbb Z_3=\{x\in\mathbb Q_3:|x|_3\le1\}
\]
in [[catalog/arithmetic/q3|\(\mathbb Q_3\)]], with inherited addition, multiplication and \(3\)-adic topology. Equivalently it is the inverse limit \(\varprojlim_m\mathbb Z/3^m\mathbb Z\) under reduction maps.

## Digits and residue

Every element has a unique convergent expansion \(\sum_{j\ge0}a_j3^j\), with digits \(a_j\in\{0,\ldots,3-1\}\). The constant digit is reduction modulo the [[algebra-rings/maximal-ideal|maximal ideal]] \(3\mathbb Z_3\); the [[algebra-commutative/residue-field|residue field]] is [[catalog/arithmetic/f3|\(\mathbb F_3\)]].

## Ring rather than field

The unit group consists of elements with nonzero constant digit. The ring is compact, complete and infinite, and \(3\) is not a unit. In particular it is neither the finite ring \(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z\) nor a rational vector space.
