The **field \(\mathbb F_{27}\)** is the [[algebra-fields-galois/finite-field|finite field]]
\[
\mathbb F_{27}=\mathbb F_3[x]/(x^3-x-1).
\]
With \(\alpha\) the residue class of \(x\), its elements are uniquely \(a+b\alpha+c\alpha^2\), with \(a,b,c\in\mathbb F_3\), and multiplication uses \(\alpha^3=\alpha+1\).

## Irreducibility

Each of the three substitutions \(x=0,1,2\) gives the nonzero residue \(2\). A reducible cubic over a field has a linear factor, hence a root, so this polynomial is irreducible. The quotient is a field and the three independent coefficients give 27 elements.

## Automorphisms and linear maps

Frobenius \(a\mapsto a^3\) generates the [[algebra-fields-galois/field-automorphism|field automorphism]] group of order three. In contrast, the invertible \(\mathbb F_3\)-linear maps form \(\mathrm{GL}_3(\mathbb F_3)\). A [[linear-algebra/linear-map|linear map]] need not fix the unit or preserve the defining multiplication.
