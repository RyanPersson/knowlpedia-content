For a prime \(p\) and an integer \(k\ge1\), **\(\mathbb F_{p^k}\)** denotes a [[algebra-fields-galois/finite-field|field]] with \(p^k\) elements. It can be realized as the roots of \(x^{p^k}-x\) in a chosen [[algebra-fields-galois/algebraic-closure|algebraic closure]] of \(\mathbb F_p\), or as \(\mathbb F_p[x]/(f)\) for a chosen monic [[algebra-rings/irreducible-polynomial|irreducible polynomial]] \(f\) of degree \(k\).

## Maps and field choices

The field is unique up to isomorphism, not up to a unique isomorphism. Its \(\mathbb F_p\)-automorphism group is cyclic of order \(k\), generated by Frobenius \(a\mapsto a^p\). A polynomial presentation specifies coordinates; embeddings into another finite field are extra relationship data.

## Degree and cardinality

The exponent \(k\) is the vector-space dimension over \(\mathbb F_p\), whereas \(p^k\) is the number of elements. This notation must not be confused with the residue ring \(\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z\), which has [[algebra-rings/zero-divisor|zero divisors]] when the exponent exceeds one.
