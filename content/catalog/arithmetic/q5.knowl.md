The **\(5\)-adic number field** \(\mathbb Q_5\) is the [[algebra-fields-galois/completion-at-place|completion]] of \(\mathbb Q\) for \(|x|_5=5^{-v_5(x)}\), where \(v_5\) is the [[shared-foundations/p-adic-valuation|\(5\)-adic valuation]]. It carries the topology from this absolute value.

## Integral elements and topology

The valuation ring is [[catalog/arithmetic/z5|\(\mathbb Z_5\)]]. Its [[algebra-rings/maximal-ideal|maximal ideal]] is \(5\mathbb Z_5\), its [[algebra-commutative/residue-field|residue field]] is \(\mathbb F_5\), and \(5\) is a uniformizer. The field is locally compact, complete, nondiscrete and totally disconnected.

## Category distinctions

As a rational algebra it carries a specified inclusion of \(\mathbb Q\), but it has infinite rational dimension. Topological-field morphisms must be continuous as well as preserve field operations. It has characteristic zero; the characteristic of its residue field is a different invariant.
