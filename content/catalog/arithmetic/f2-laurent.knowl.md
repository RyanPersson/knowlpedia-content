The **formal Laurent-series field \(\mathbb F_2((t))\)** consists of series \(\sum_{j\ge N}a_jt^j\), with \(N\in\mathbb Z\) and \(a_j\in\mathbb F_2\), over the [[catalog/arithmetic/f2|finite field \(\mathbb F_2\)]]. Addition is coefficientwise and multiplication is convolution. Its topology is defined by \(v_t(\sum a_jt^j)=\min\{j:a_j\ne0\}\), with \(v_t(0)=+\infty\).

## Local structure

The ring of integers is [[catalog/arithmetic/f2-power-series|\(\mathbb F_2[\![t]\!]\)]], the [[algebra-rings/maximal-ideal|maximal ideal]] is generated by \(t\), and reduction takes the constant coefficient. With \(|x|=2^{-v_t(x)}\), this is a complete [[algebra-fields-galois/local-field|locally compact nondiscrete field]] of characteristic 2.

## Formal rather than analytic

Only finitely many negative exponents are allowed. These are formal series, with convergence governed by powers of \(t\), not functions evaluated at a complex number. Every nonzero series is a power of \(t\) times a unit, which supplies its inverse.
