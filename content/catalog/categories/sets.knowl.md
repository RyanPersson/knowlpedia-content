The **category of sets** \(\mathbf{Set}\) has sets as objects and all functions between them as morphisms. Its identity on \(X\) is \(x\mapsto x\), and its composition is ordinary function composition. These satisfy the axioms of a [[algebra-category-theory/category|category]].

## Convention and comparison

Forgetting structure sends a group, [[linear-algebra/vector-space|vector space]] or algebra to its underlying set. A bijection in this category need not preserve any of that additional structure.
