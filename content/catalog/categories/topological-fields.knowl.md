The **category of topological fields** used here has fields with a Hausdorff topology making addition, multiplication, additive inversion, and inversion on the nonzero elements continuous. Morphisms are continuous unit-preserving field homomorphisms. Thus every morphism is injective, but is not required to be a topological embedding onto its image by definition.

## Convention and comparison

The category includes archimedean fields and [[algebra-fields-galois/nonarchimedean-local-field|nonarchimedean local fields]] with their specified topologies. A field with the discrete topology is a different topological-field view from the same field with a nondiscrete topology.
