The **category of topological groups** used here has Hausdorff [[topology/topological-group|topological groups]] as objects and continuous [[algebra-groups/group-homomorphism|group homomorphisms]] as morphisms. Group multiplication and inversion are continuous in each object. Identities and composition are those of [[topology/continuous-map|continuous maps]].

## Convention and comparison

An abstract group homomorphism need not be continuous. For finite-dimensional [[fiber-bundles/lie-group|Lie groups]], the catalogue also records the more specific real or complex Lie-group category, whose regularity convention is stated separately.
