The real Lie algebra \(\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(3)\) is the [[lie-groups/direct-sum-of-lie-algebras|direct sum]] of two copies of [[catalog/lie-algebras/su-3|\(\mathfrak{su}(3)\)]], with
\[
[(X_1,X_2),(Y_1,Y_2)]=([X_1,Y_1],[X_2,Y_2]).
\]
It has real dimension \(16\). The two eight-dimensional factors are commuting simple ideals, so the sum is semisimple and not simple.

## Magic-square entry

For the compact real convention,
\[
\mathfrak M(\mathbb C,\mathbb C)\cong
\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(3).
\]
It is the nonsimple entry of the [[catalog/relationships/compact-freudenthal-magic-square|square]]. Each complex input is being used as a real algebra.
