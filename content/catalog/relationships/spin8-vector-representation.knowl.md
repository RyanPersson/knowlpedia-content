The **vector representation** \(8_v\) of compact [[lie-groups/spin-group|\(\operatorname{Spin}(8)\)]] is the real space \(\mathbb R^8\) with action obtained from the canonical double cover
\[
\rho_v:\operatorname{Spin}(8)\longrightarrow SO(8)
\]
and the usual matrix action. Its dimension is \(8\), it is irreducible over \(\mathbb R\), and \(\ker\rho_v=\{1,-1\}\).

## The action is part of the object

The [[catalog/relationships/spin8-positive-half-spin-representation|positive]] and [[catalog/relationships/spin8-negative-half-spin-representation|negative half-spin representations]] also have real dimension eight, but have different actions and central kernels. They are distinct objects in the category of representations of this fixed group. [[lie-groups/spin8-triality|Triality]] permutes their isomorphism classes by twisting the group action.
