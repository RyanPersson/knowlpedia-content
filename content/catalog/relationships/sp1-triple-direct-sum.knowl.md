The real Lie algebra \(\mathfrak{sp}(1)^{\oplus3}\) is the [[lie-groups/direct-sum-of-lie-algebras|direct sum]] of three copies of [[catalog/lie-algebras/sp-1|\(\mathfrak{sp}(1)\)]]. For triples, its bracket is
\[
[(X_1,X_2,X_3),(Y_1,Y_2,Y_3)]
=([X_1,Y_1],[X_2,Y_2],[X_3,Y_3]).
\]
Its real dimension is \(9\), and its three simple factors commute.

## Quaternionic triality

There is a real Lie algebra isomorphism
\[
\operatorname{tri}(\mathbb H)\cong\mathfrak{sp}(1)^{\oplus3}.
\]
This records the full [[catalog/relationships/triality-algebra|triality algebra]]; quaternion derivations alone form only a three-dimensional algebra.
