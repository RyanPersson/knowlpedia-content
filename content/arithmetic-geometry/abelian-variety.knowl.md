An **abelian variety** over a field \(k\) is a proper, smooth, geometrically connected [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/algebraic-group|algebraic group]] over \(k\). Such a group is projective and its group law is commutative.

## Dimension one and products

An [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/elliptic-curve|elliptic curve]] is an abelian variety of dimension one. The product \(E_1\times E_2\) of two elliptic curves is an abelian surface, with group law given by adding the two coordinates separately. Higher-dimensional abelian varieties need not decompose into elliptic factors.
