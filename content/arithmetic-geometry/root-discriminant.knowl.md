For a [[algebra-fields-galois/number-field|number field]] \(E\), its **root discriminant** is \(\operatorname{rd}(E)=|\operatorname{disc}(E)|^{1/[E:\mathbb Q]}\), using the [[algebra-fields-galois/number-field-discriminant|field discriminant]] and the [[algebra-fields-galois/degree-of-extension|extension degree]].

## An imaginary quadratic field

The Gaussian field \(\mathbb Q(i)\) has discriminant \(-4\) and degree two, hence root discriminant \(2\).
