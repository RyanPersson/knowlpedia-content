A **CM field** \(E\) is a quadratic extension of a totally real [[algebra-fields-galois/number-field|number field]] \(E^+\) such that \(E\) has no real [[algebra-fields-galois/field-embedding|embedding]]. Totally real means that every embedding of \(E^+\) into \(\mathbb C\) lands in \(\mathbb R\). The nontrivial automorphism of \(E/E^+\) acts as complex conjugation in every complex embedding of \(E\).

## The smallest examples

An [[algebra-fields-galois/imaginary-quadratic-field|imaginary quadratic field]] has \(E^+=\mathbb Q\). For a higher-degree example, \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_5)\) is quadratic over its real subfield \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\). The letters CM stand for [[arithmetic-geometry/complex-multiplication|complex multiplication]].
