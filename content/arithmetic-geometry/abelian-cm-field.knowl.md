An **abelian CM field** in this setting is a [[arithmetic-geometry/cm-field|CM field]] \(E\) such that \(E/\mathbb Q\) is [[algebra-fields-galois/galois-extension|Galois]] and its Galois group is [[algebra-groups/abelian-group|abelian]]. “Auxiliary” describes the job of \(E\) in the argument. One chooses it to control prime splitting and to organize the [[analytic-number-theory/galois-character|characters]] used in a prime sum.

## A concrete field

For \(E=\mathbb Q(\zeta_5)\), an automorphism is determined by \(\zeta_5\mapsto\zeta_5^a\) with \(a\in(\mathbb Z/5\mathbb Z)^\times\). This group is cyclic of order four. The subgroup generated by complex conjugation fixes \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\). This example illustrates the definition without imposing the additional conditions on the auxiliary fields in the post.
