For a [[arithmetic-geometry/cm-field|CM field]] \(E\), a **CM type** is a subset \(\Phi\) of its complex [[algebra-fields-galois/field-embedding|embeddings]] containing exactly one of each pair \(\tau,\bar\tau\). Hence \(|\Phi|=[E:\mathbb Q]/2\).

## A quadratic example

For \(\mathbb Q(i)\), either embedding \(i\mapsto i\) or \(i\mapsto-i\) alone specifies a CM type.
