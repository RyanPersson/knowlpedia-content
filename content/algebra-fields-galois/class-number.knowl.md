The **class number** of a number field \(K\) is
\[
h_K=|\operatorname{Cl}(K)|,
\]
the cardinality of its [[algebra-fields-galois/ideal-class-group|ideal class group]]. The finiteness theorem for ideal classes ensures that this is a positive integer.

## Meaning of class number one

One has \(h_K=1\) exactly when every ideal of \(\mathcal O_K\) is principal. For example \(h_{\mathbb Q}=1\), since every ideal of \(\mathbb Z\) is generated by a single integer.

## A geometric use

For imaginary quadratic fields, the [[differential-geometry/bianchi-cusp-ideal-class-correspondence|Bianchi cusp correspondence]] interprets this arithmetic number as the number of cusp ends of a hyperbolic orbifold.
