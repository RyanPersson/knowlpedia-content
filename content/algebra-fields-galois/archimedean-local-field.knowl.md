An **archimedean local field** is a
[[algebra-fields-galois/local-field|local field]] whose absolute value is
archimedean. Every such field is isomorphic as a topological field to
\(\mathbb R\) or \(\mathbb C\).

## As a completion

For a [[algebra-fields-galois/number-field|number field]] \(F\), a real
embedding \(F\hookrightarrow\mathbb R\) gives a real place and completion
\(F_v\cong\mathbb R\). A conjugate pair of nonreal embeddings
\(F\hookrightarrow\mathbb C\) gives a complex place and completion
\(F_v\cong\mathbb C\).

[[algebra-fields-galois/global-function-field|Global function fields]] have no
archimedean places.

## Contrast with the nonarchimedean case

Archimedean local fields are connected Lie groups under addition. By contrast,
[[algebra-fields-galois/nonarchimedean-local-field|nonarchimedean local
fields]] are totally disconnected and have compact open valuation rings. This
topological difference leads to different test functions and representation
theory at the two kinds of places.
