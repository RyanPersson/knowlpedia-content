An **involution** on a field \(K\) is a [[algebra-fields-galois/field-automorphism|field automorphism]] \(\sigma:K\to K\) satisfying \(\sigma(\sigma(a))=a\) for all \(a\in K\). This convention allows the identity.

## Example

Complex conjugation is a nonidentity involution of \(\mathbb C\). Its [[algebra-fields-galois/fixed-field|fixed field]] is \(\mathbb R\).
