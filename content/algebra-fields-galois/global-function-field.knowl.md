A **global function field** is a finite
[[algebra-fields-galois/field-extension|field extension]] of
\(\mathbb F_q(t)\) for some [[algebra-fields-galois/finite-field|finite
field]] \(\mathbb F_q\).

Equivalently, it is the function field of a
[[algebraic-geometry-foundations/smooth-projective-curve|smooth projective
geometrically connected curve]] over a finite field, with the finite constant
field understood as part of the global-field structure.

## Places

Every [[algebra-fields-galois/place-of-global-field|place]] corresponds to a
closed point of the associated projective curve and is nonarchimedean. Its
[[algebra-fields-galois/completion-at-place|completion]] is a finite extension
of a Laurent-series field \(\mathbb F_q((t))\), hence a
[[algebra-fields-galois/nonarchimedean-local-field|nonarchimedean local
field]] of positive characteristic.

## Position among global fields

Global function fields are precisely the
[[langlands-letter/knowls/global-local-fields-completions|global fields]] of
positive characteristic. Their curves supply geometric methods that have no
literal number-field counterpart.
