In a field with a surjective [[algebra-fields-galois/valuation-on-a-field|discrete valuation]] \(v:K^\times\to\mathbb Z\), a **uniformizer** is an element \(\pi\) with \(v(\pi)=1\). It generates the maximal ideal of the [[algebra-fields-galois/valuation-ring|valuation ring]] \(\{x:v(x)\ge0\}\), with \(v(0)=+\infty\).
