Let \(A\) be a flat connection on a principal \(G\)-bundle \(P\to M\), and let \(H_A^j\) denote the cohomology of its [[fiber-bundles/deformation-complex-of-a-flat-connection|deformation complex]]. The **deformation-theoretic tangent space** to flat connections modulo gauge at \([A]\) is
\[
T^{\mathrm{def}}_{[A]}\mathcal M_{\mathrm{flat}}
\cong
H_A^1
=
\frac{\ker(d_A:\Omega^1(M;\operatorname{ad}P)\to\Omega^2(M;\operatorname{ad}P))}
{\operatorname{im}(d_A:\Omega^0(M;\operatorname{ad}P)\to\Omega^1(M;\operatorname{ad}P))}.
\]
If \(A\) is [[fiber-bundles/irreducible-connection|irreducible]] and unobstructed so that the gauge quotient is smooth near \([A]\), this is its ordinary [[differential-geometry/tangent-space|tangent space]]. Moreover, \(H_A^0\) is the infinitesimal stabilizer and \(H_A^2\) is the primary obstruction space.

## Linearization

Write a nearby connection as \(A+a\). Its curvature is
\[
F_{A+a}=F_A+d_Aa+\tfrac12[a\wedge a].
\]
Since \(F_A=0\), the linearized flatness equation is \(d_Aa=0\). An infinitesimal [[fiber-bundles/gauge-transformation|gauge transformation]] generated by \(\xi\in\Omega^0(M;\operatorname{ad}P)\) changes \(a\) by \(d_A\xi\), up to the sign convention for the gauge action. Taking solutions modulo these tangent gauge directions gives \(H_A^1\).

The quadratic term defines the first obstruction map \(H_A^1\to H_A^2\). Hence a class in \(H_A^1\) need not integrate to a curve of flat connections.

## Holonomy description

Through the holonomy correspondence, the same cohomology is group cohomology with local coefficients:
\[
H_A^1\cong H^1\!\left(\pi_1(M);\mathfrak g_{\operatorname{Ad}\rho_A}\right)
\]
under the standard comparison hypotheses. It is therefore also the infinitesimal deformation space of the [[fiber-bundles/holonomy-representation|holonomy representation]] \(\rho_A:\pi_1(M)\to G\) modulo infinitesimal conjugation. The local germ is governed by the corresponding differential graded [[lie-groups/lie-algebra|Lie algebra]].

## Smooth and singular points

If \(H_A^2=0\), standard elliptic slice models on a compact base give unobstructedness. If in addition the stabilizer has been removed or is only the unavoidable center, the quotient is locally a smooth space modeled on \(H_A^1\). Nonzero \(H_A^2\) permits obstructions but does not prove that the obstruction map is nonzero.

At a [[fiber-bundles/reducible-connection|reducible connection]], the coarse quotient can be singular because the stabilizer acts on \(H_A^1\). In that case \(H_A^1\) remains the degree-one tangent cohomology of the moduli problem, but it should not automatically be called the Zariski tangent space of the coarse [[lie-groups/orbit-space|orbit space]].
