Let \(\pi:P\to M\) be a [[fiber-bundles/principal-g-bundle|principal \(G\)-bundle]] with a [[fiber-bundles/principal-connection|principal connection]] \(\omega\), and choose \(p\in P_x\). The **holonomy algebra** at \(p\) is
\[
\mathfrak{hol}_p(\omega)
:=\operatorname{Lie}\bigl(\operatorname{Hol}_p(\omega)\bigr)
\subseteq\mathfrak g,
\]
where \(\operatorname{Hol}_p(\omega)\subseteq G\) is the [[fiber-bundles/holonomy-group|holonomy group]] obtained by [[fiber-bundles/parallel-transport|parallel transport]] around piecewise smooth loops based at \(x\), with horizontal lifts beginning at \(p\), and \(\mathfrak g\) is the [[lie-groups/lie-algebra|Lie algebra]] of \(G\).

## Dependence on the basepoint

If \(p\) is replaced by \(p\cdot g\) in the same fiber, then
\(\operatorname{Hol}_{p\cdot g}(\omega)=g^{-1}\operatorname{Hol}_p(\omega)g\).
Thus the holonomy algebra changes by the corresponding adjoint action.

A key structural result is that \(\mathfrak{hol}_p(\omega)\) is generated by curvature (see [[fiber-bundles/ambrosesinger-curvature-span|Ambrose–Singer curvature span]]).

## Examples
1. **Flat connections.** If the [[fiber-bundles/curvature|curvature]] of \(\omega\) vanishes, then \(\mathfrak{hol}_p(\omega)=0\).
2. **Product structures.** On a Riemannian product \(M_1\times M_2\) with the product metric, the holonomy algebra splits as the direct sum of the holonomy algebras of the factors.
