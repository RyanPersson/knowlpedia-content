Let \(G\) be a [[fiber-bundles/lie-group|Lie group]] acting
[[fiber-bundles/smooth-action-of-a-lie-group-on-a-manifold|smoothly]] on a
[[fiber-bundles/smooth-manifold|smooth manifold]] \(M\). Let \(\mathfrak g\)
be the [[lie-groups/lie-algebra-of-a-lie-group|Lie algebra]] of \(G\), and let
\(\mathfrak g^*\) be its real linear dual.

Let \(\Omega^*(M)\) denote the graded algebra of real
[[fiber-bundles/differential-k-form|differential forms]] on \(M\). Let
\(S(\mathfrak g^*)\) denote the
[[algebra-modules/symmetric-algebra|symmetric algebra]] of \(\mathfrak g^*\),
viewed as polynomial functions on \(\mathfrak g\). Assign degree two to each
linear polynomial.

The **Cartan complex** is
\[
\Omega_G^*(M)=\bigl(S(\mathfrak g^*)\otimes_{\mathbb R}\Omega^*(M)\bigr)^G.
\]
Here \(\otimes_{\mathbb R}\) is the
[[algebra-modules/tensor-product|tensor product]], and the superscript \(G\)
means the elements invariant under the induced group action. Total degree
is twice polynomial degree plus form degree.

View an element \(\alpha\) as a polynomial map from \(\mathfrak g\) to
\(\Omega^*(M)\). For \(\xi\in\mathfrak g\), let \(\xi_M\) be the
fundamental vector field generated by the action. The **Cartan differential** is
\[
(d_G\alpha)(\xi)=d(\alpha(\xi))-\iota_{\xi_M}\alpha(\xi),
\]
where \(d\) is the [[fiber-bundles/exterior-derivative|exterior derivative]]
and \(\iota_{\xi_M}\) is
[[fiber-bundles/interior-product-contraction-x|contraction]] with \(\xi_M\).
It has degree one and satisfies \(d_G^2=0\) on the invariant elements.
The **Cartan equivariant cohomology** is the
[[algebra-homological/cohomology-module|cohomology]] of this complex:
\[
H^*_{G,\mathrm{Cartan}}(M)=H^*(\Omega_G^*(M),d_G).
\]

## Group action and sign convention

For \(g\in G\), let \(a_g:M\to M\) be the map \(a_g(x)=g\cdot x\).
Let \(\operatorname{Ad}_g\) denote the
[[lie-groups/adjoint-action-of-a-lie-group|adjoint action]] on \(\mathfrak g\),
and let \((a_{g^{-1}})^*\) denote
[[fiber-bundles/pullback-of-differential-forms|pullback of forms]].
The invariance condition in the Cartan complex means precisely that
\[
\alpha(\operatorname{Ad}_g\xi)=(a_{g^{-1}})^*\alpha(\xi)
\qquad(g\in G,\ \xi\in\mathfrak g).
\]

The sign convention for the fundamental vector field is
\[
\xi_M(x)=\left.\frac{d}{dt}\right|_{0}\exp(t\xi)\cdot x,
\]
where \(\exp\) is the [[lie-groups/exponential-map-lie-group|exponential map]].

## Comparison with topological equivariant cohomology

The Borel definition is \(H_G^*(M;R)=H^*(EG\times_GM;R)\), using the [[fiber-bundles/universal-principal-bundle-egbg|universal principal bundle]] and any coefficient ring \(R\). For compact \(G\), the real Cartan complex above computes \(H_G^*(M;\mathbb R)\). Complexifying it computes complex coefficients; real differential forms do not directly compute arbitrary characteristic-zero coefficients, such as \(\mathbb Q\).

For a noncompact group, the Cartan complex is still defined, but this comparison can fail. For example, \(G=(\mathbb R,+)\) acting on a point has Cartan cohomology \(\mathbb R[u]\), with \(\deg u=2\), whereas its Borel cohomology is just \(\mathbb R\) because \(G\) is contractible.

## Examples for compact groups

- **Point:** \(H^*_{G,\mathrm{Cartan}}(\mathrm{pt})=S(\mathfrak g^*)^G\cong H^*(BG;\mathbb R)\).
- **Trivial action:** \(H^*_{G,\mathrm{Cartan}}(M)\cong H^*(M;\mathbb R)\otimes H^*(BG;\mathbb R)\).
- **Free action:** \(H^*_{G,\mathrm{Cartan}}(M)\cong H^*(M/G;\mathbb R)\).
