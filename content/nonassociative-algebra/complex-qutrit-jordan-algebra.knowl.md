The **complex-qutrit Jordan algebra** is
\[
\mathfrak h_3(\mathbb C)
=\{X\in M_3(\mathbb C):X^*=X\},
\qquad
X\circ Y=\frac12(XY+YX).
\]
It is the nine-dimensional real
[[nonassociative-algebra/euclidean-jordan-algebra|Euclidean Jordan algebra]]
of observables of a complex [[quantum-foundations/qutrit|three-level quantum system]].

## Structure

The unit is \(I_3\), the rank is \(3\), and the Jordan trace and determinant
are the ordinary matrix trace and determinant. The spectral theorem for
Hermitian matrices is precisely the Euclidean-Jordan spectral theorem here.
The algebra is simple and
[[nonassociative-algebra/special-and-exceptional-jordan-algebras|special]].
Its positive trace-one elements are the qutrit density matrices.

## Symmetries

Unitary conjugation \(X\mapsto UXU^*\) defines a Jordan automorphism. Scalar
unitaries act trivially, so the connected automorphism group is \(PU(3)\).
The full real [[nonassociative-algebra/automorphism-group-of-a-jordan-algebra|Jordan automorphism group]] also has a disconnected component
generated by complex conjugation; connected and full stabilizers must
therefore not be conflated.

## Position inside the Albert algebra

Choosing a copy \(\mathbb C\subset\mathbb O\) gives a unital inclusion
\[
\mathfrak h_3(\mathbb C)\subset\mathfrak h_3(\mathbb O).
\]
The compact group \(F_4\) acts transitively on Albert subalgebras isomorphic
to \(\mathfrak h_3(\mathbb C)\). For a chosen copy, its full stabilizer is
not connected; its [[lie-groups/identity-component-of-a-lie-group|identity component]] is
\[
\bigl(SU(3)\times SU(3)\bigr)/\mathbb Z_3.
\]
