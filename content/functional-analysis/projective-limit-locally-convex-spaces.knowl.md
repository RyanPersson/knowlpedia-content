Let \((E_i,\pi_{ij})\) be a projective system of [[functional-analysis/locally-convex-space|locally convex spaces]]: for \(i\le j\), the map \(\pi_{ij}:E_j\to E_i\) is continuous and linear, with \(\pi_{ii}\) the identity and \(\pi_{ik}=\pi_{ij}\pi_{jk}\). Its **projective limit** is
\[
\varprojlim_i E_i
=\{(x_i)\in\prod_iE_i:\pi_{ij}(x_j)=x_i\text{ whenever }i\le j\},
\]
equipped with the [[topology/subspace-topology|subspace topology]] inherited from the product's [[topology/product-topology|product topology]]. The coordinate projections are continuous [[linear-algebra/linear-map|linear maps]] and jointly determine this topology.
In particular, the resulting space is locally convex.

## Universal property

If \(F\) is locally convex and continuous linear maps \(f_i:F\to E_i\) satisfy \(\pi_{ij}f_j=f_i\), there is a unique continuous linear map \(f:F\to\varprojlim_iE_i\) whose \(i\)-th coordinate is \(f_i\). This makes the construction the categorical [[algebra-category-theory/limit|limit]] in locally convex spaces and characterizes it up to canonical topological isomorphism.

## Seminorms and completeness

The limit topology is generated by seminorms \(p\circ\operatorname{pr}_i\), where \(p\) is a continuous seminorm on \(E_i\). If all \(E_i\) are Hausdorff, the compatibility equations define a closed subspace of the product. Consequently, a projective limit of complete Hausdorff locally convex spaces is complete. A countable projective limit of [[functional-analysis/frechet-space|Fréchet spaces]] is again Fréchet.

## Examples and cautions

For a compact [[fiber-bundles/smooth-manifold|smooth manifold]] \(M\), the inclusions \(C^{k+1}(M)\to C^k(M)\) form a projective system of [[linear-algebra/banach-space|Banach spaces]] whose limit is \(C^\infty(M)\) with its usual Fréchet topology. Projective limits reverse the direction of the bonding maps and should not be confused with [[functional-analysis/inductive-limit-locally-convex-spaces|locally convex inductive limits]]. If Hausdorffness is omitted, the compatible-tuple space need not be separated.
