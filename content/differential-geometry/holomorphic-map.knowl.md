A map \(f:U\to\mathbb C^m\), where \(U\subseteq\mathbb C^n\) is open, is **holomorphic** if it is complex differentiable in all variables; equivalently, its component functions are locally given by convergent complex power series.

For [[differential-geometry/complex-manifold|complex manifolds]], a map is holomorphic when its expression in every pair of complex coordinate charts is holomorphic in this Euclidean sense. A bijective holomorphic map with holomorphic inverse is a biholomorphism.
