Let \(M\) carry an [[differential-geometry/almost-complex-structure|almost-complex structure]] \(J\). Extend \(J\) complex-linearly to \(T_{\mathbb C}M=TM\otimes_{\mathbb R}\mathbb C\). Since \(J^2=-1\), its only eigenvalues are \(i\) and \(-i\), and the complexified [[fiber-bundles/tangent-bundle|tangent bundle]] splits as
\[
T_{\mathbb C}M=T^{1,0}M\oplus T^{0,1}M,
\qquad
T^{1,0}M=\ker(J-i),\quad T^{0,1}M=\ker(J+i).
\]
Complex conjugation interchanges the two summands. This is the **type decomposition of the complexified tangent bundle**; it exists for every almost-complex structure and does not require integrability.

## Projection operators

The summands are the images of the smooth bundle projections
\[
P^{1,0}=\tfrac12(1-iJ),\qquad P^{0,1}=\tfrac12(1+iJ).
\]
For a real tangent vector \(X\), its components are \(X^{1,0}=\frac12(X-iJX)\) and \(X^{0,1}=\frac12(X+iJX)\). These formulas also show directly that the summands are complex [[fiber-bundles/complex-vector-bundle|vector bundles]] of complex rank \(\frac12\dim_{\mathbb R}M\).

## Dual decomposition and forms

Dualizing gives
\[
T_{\mathbb C}^*M=T^{*1,0}M\oplus T^{*0,1}M.
\]
Exterior powers then decompose complex-valued differential forms into \((p,q)\)-types. This algebraic decomposition is the starting point for the operators \(\partial\) and \(\bar\partial\) on a [[differential-geometry/complex-manifold|complex manifold]].

## Integrability

The splitting itself is pointwise linear algebra. The additional statement that sections of \(T^{0,1}M\) are closed under the complexified [[fiber-bundles/lie-bracket|Lie bracket]] is equivalent to [[differential-geometry/integrable-almost-complex-structure|integrability]]. Only under that condition do the summands agree locally with the tangent directions generated by holomorphic and antiholomorphic coordinates.
