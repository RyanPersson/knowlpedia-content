For events \(p,q\) in a time-oriented [[differential-geometry/lorentzian-manifold|Lorentzian manifold]], the **causal diamond** from \(p\) to \(q\) is
\[
J^+(p)\cap J^-(q),
\]
where \(J^\pm\) are the [[differential-geometry/chronological-and-causal-future|causal future and past]]. It consists of the events that can be reached causally from \(p\) and from which \(q\) can be reached causally. The diamond is empty when \(q\notin J^+(p)\).

Compactness of all causal diamonds, together with [[differential-geometry/strong-causality|strong causality]], defines a [[differential-geometry/globally-hyperbolic-spacetime|globally hyperbolic spacetime]] in the convention used here.
