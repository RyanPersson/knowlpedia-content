Let \(\Delta\) be a chosen complex
[[differential-geometry/spinor-module|spinor module]]. A **Dirac spinor** is an
element of \(\Delta\). On a spin manifold, a **Dirac spinor field** is a
section of the corresponding complex
[[differential-geometry/spinor-bundle|spinor bundle]].

In even dimension the complex spinor module has the chiral decomposition
\[
\Delta=\Delta^+\oplus\Delta^-.
\]
A Dirac spinor may therefore have components of both chiralities. A spinor
lying entirely in one summand is a
[[differential-geometry/weyl-spinor|Weyl spinor]].

“Dirac spinor” specifies the complex spin representation in which the spinor
takes values; it does not assert that the spinor satisfies the
[[mathematical-physics/dirac-equation|Dirac equation]].
