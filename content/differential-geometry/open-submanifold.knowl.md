Let \(M\) be a smooth manifold. An **open submanifold** of \(M\) is an open
subset \(U\subseteq M\) equipped with the [[fiber-bundles/smooth-structure|smooth
structure]] generated by the restricted charts
\((U\cap V,\varphi|_{U\cap V})\), where \((V,\varphi)\) ranges over the
charts of \(M\).

The inclusion \(U\hookrightarrow M\) is a smooth open embedding, and
\(\dim U=\dim M\).

## Universal property for smooth maps

A map \(f:N\to U\) is smooth exactly when its composite with the inclusion
\(U\hookrightarrow M\) is smooth. Thus the induced structure is uniquely
characterized by the requirement that maps into \(U\) can be tested after
viewing them as maps into \(M\).
