A **horosphere** in [[differential-geometry/hyperbolic-three-space|hyperbolic three-space]] is an isometric image of a horizontal plane \(r=r_0>0\) in the upper-half-space model
\[
\mathbb H^3=\{(z,r):z\in\mathbb C,\ r>0\},\qquad ds^2=(|dz|^2+dr^2)/r^2.
\]
The point \(\infty\) is the ideal center of a horizontal horosphere; isometries transport it to the center of the image horosphere.

## Other centers

Horospheres centered at a finite boundary point are Euclidean spheres tangent to \(r=0\), with the tangency point omitted. The induced metric on a horizontal horosphere is \(|dz|^2/r_0^2\), so it is intrinsically Euclidean.
