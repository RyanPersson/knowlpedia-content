An **open horoball** in hyperbolic three-space is an isometric image of \(\{(z,r):r>r_0\}\), \(r_0>0\), in the upper-half-space model. Its boundary in hyperbolic space is a [[differential-geometry/horosphere|horosphere]]; its ideal center is the same as that of the boundary.

## Models and convention

For a finite ideal center it is the interior of a Euclidean ball tangent to the boundary plane. A closed horoball also includes its bounding horosphere, but never includes the ideal center as a point of hyperbolic space.

## Moving farther into an end

At center \(\infty\), increasing \(r_0\) shrinks the horoball. This produces nested neighborhoods of the same cusp end.
