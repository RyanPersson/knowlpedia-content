A differential relation satisfies the **h-principle** in the nonparametric sense if every [[partial-differential-equations/formal-and-holonomic-solution|formal solution]] is joined by a [[topology/homotopy|homotopy]] through formal solutions to a holonomic solution. The regularity of sections and homotopies is part of the convention.

## Variants

Relative, parametric, and approximation forms impose additional requirements. They are stronger statements and must be specified when used.
