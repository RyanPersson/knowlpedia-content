A **complex manifold of complex dimension \(n\)** is a Hausdorff, second-countable space covered by coordinate charts \(\varphi_i:U_i\to V_i\subseteq\mathbb C^n\) such that each transition map \(\varphi_j\circ\varphi_i^{-1}\) is [[differential-geometry/holomorphic-map|holomorphic]] wherever it is defined.

Forgetting complex coordinates gives an underlying [[fiber-bundles/smooth-manifold|smooth manifold]] of real dimension \(2n\). The complex charts determine an [[differential-geometry/almost-complex-structure|almost-complex structure]] on its tangent bundle; the existence of holomorphic charts is the additional [[differential-geometry/integrable-almost-complex-structure|integrability]] condition.
