A **measure-preserving flow** on a [[probability/probability-space|probability space]] is a jointly measurable family \(\Phi^t:X\to X\), \(t\in\mathbb R\), satisfying
\[
\Phi^0=\mathrm{id},\qquad \Phi^{s+t}=\Phi^s\Phi^t,\qquad
(\Phi^t)_*\mu=\mu.
\]
It is a measure-preserving action of \((\mathbb R,+)\). Ergodicity of the flow concerns sets invariant under every \(\Phi^t\).

## Time-one maps

An ergodic flow can have a nonergodic time-one map. Translation by all real times on \(\mathbb R/\mathbb Z\) is ergodic, but its time-one map is the identity.

## Smooth models

A [[differential-geometry/differentiable-flow|smooth flow]] is generated by a vector field. With an invariant probability measure and strong continuity, its [[ergodic-theory/koopman-generator|Koopman generator]] differentiates observables along that field.
