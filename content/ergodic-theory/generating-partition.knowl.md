For an invertible probability-preserving transformation \(T\), a [[ergodic-theory/measurable-partition|finite measurable partition]] \(\mathcal P\) is **generating** if the sigma-algebra generated by all cells of \(T^{-n}\mathcal P\), \(n\in\mathbb Z\), equals the whole sigma-algebra modulo null sets. For a noninvertible map, a one-sided generator uses \(n\geq0\).

## Example

In a finite-alphabet two-sided Bernoulli shift, the partition according to the coordinate \(x_0\) is generating: its integer translates record every coordinate. One-sided shifts use the corresponding one-sided generator.
