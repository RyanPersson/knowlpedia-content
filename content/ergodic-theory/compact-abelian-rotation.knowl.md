A **compact abelian rotation** is the [[ergodic-theory/measure-preserving-system|probability-preserving system]]
\[
R_a:K\to K,\qquad R_a(x)=x+a,
\]
where \(K\) is a compact metrizable abelian group with normalized [[harmonic-analysis/haar-measure|Haar measure]] and \(a\in K\).

## Characters and ergodicity

For a character \(\chi\), forward pullback gives \(U_{R_a}\chi=\chi(a)\chi\). Since characters form a [[harmonic-analysis/characters-compact-abelian-fourier-basis|Fourier basis]], the rotation is ergodic exactly when \(\chi(a)\ne1\) for every nontrivial character. Equivalently, the cyclic subgroup generated by \(a\) is dense in \(K\).

## Group actions by translations

More generally, translations by elements of a subgroup \(H\subseteq K\) act ergodically exactly when \(\overline H=K\), or equivalently when no nontrivial character is one on every element of \(H\). This is a joint condition on the translating subgroup.
