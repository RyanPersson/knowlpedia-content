An **idyll** is an [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/ordered-blue-field|ordered blue field]] \(F\) with unique weak inverses such that:

1. its partial order is **purely positive**, meaning that it is generated by relations
   \[
   0\leq \sum a_i;
   \]
2. the canonical map \(\mathbb N[F^\times]\to F^+\) is a bijection.

Thus the ambient semiring is the free semiring on the unit group, while all additive information is carried by the null relations \(0\leq\sum a_i\).

## Relation to tracts

The null set
\[
N_F=\left\{\sum a_i\in\mathbb N[F^\times]\;\middle|\;0\leq\sum a_i\right\}
\]
turns an idyll into a [[algebra-hyperstructures/tract|tract]]. Conversely, the tract-to-ordered-blueprint comparison lands in purely positive objects, but categorical equivalence statements require the appropriate ideal-closure condition on null sets. An idyll should therefore not be used as a synonym for every tract.

## Pastures

A [[algebra-hyperstructures/pasture|pasture]] is an idyll whose null ideal is
generated by its three-term null relations under ideal operations and the
fusion rule. Thus every pasture is an idyll and a fusion band, but an arbitrary
idyll need not be fusion-generated by its three-term null relations.
