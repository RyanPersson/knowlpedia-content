Every [[algebra-hyperstructures/doubly-distributive-hyperfield|doubly
distributive hyperfield]] is
[[algebra-hyperstructures/stringent-hyperfield|stringent]].

In other words, if \(F\) is doubly distributive and \(a\ne-b\), then
\[
\lvert a\boxplus b\rvert=1.
\]
The converse is false: stringency alone does not imply double distributivity.

Nathan Bowler and Ting Su,
