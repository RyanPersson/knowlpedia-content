A **pasture** is an [[algebra-hyperstructures/idyll|idyll]] \(P\) whose
[[algebra-hyperstructures/null-set-of-a-band|nullset]] is the fusion ideal
generated by its three-term null relations:
\[
N_P=
\left\langle\!\left\langle
a+b+c\;\middle|\;a,b,c\in P,\ a+b+c\in N_P
\right\rangle\!\right\rangle.
\]
The double brackets denote closure under ideal operations and the
[[algebra-hyperstructures/fusion-rule-for-bands|fusion rule]]. Consequently
every pasture is a [[algebra-hyperstructures/fusion-band|fusion band]].

Morphisms are band morphisms: they preserve multiplication, \(0\), \(1\), and
null sums.

## Equivalent terminology

A band satisfying the displayed generation condition is called a
**hereditary fusion band**. Hence a pasture is equivalently a hereditary
fusion band that is also an idyll. It is not merely an arbitrary ordered blue
field or tract.

Fields, [[algebra-hyperstructures/partial-field|partial fields]], and hyperfields have associated pastures. The
three-term condition is substantive: a general idyll can contain higher-arity
null relations not generated from its three-term ones by fusion.

Three-term null relations encode the Grassmann–Plücker relations used for
weak matroids and cross-ratios. Fusion then derives the higher-arity null
relations by cancelling a term against its additive inverse.
