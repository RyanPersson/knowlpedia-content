A **fusion band** is a [[algebra-hyperstructures/band|band]] \(B\) whose
[[algebra-hyperstructures/null-set-of-a-band|nullset]] \(N_B\) satisfies the
[[algebra-hyperstructures/fusion-rule-for-bands|fusion rule]]:
\[
c+\alpha\in N_B,\qquad -c+\beta\in N_B
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\alpha+\beta\in N_B
\]
for all \(c\in B\) and \(\alpha,\beta\in B^+\).

Equivalently, \(N_B\) is a fusion ideal of \(B^+\). This condition specifies
how existing null relations compose; it does not say that the nullset is
generated by relations of any particular arity.
