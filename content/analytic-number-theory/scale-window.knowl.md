A **window of scales** in this argument is a whole interval \(Y\le x\le Y^\lambda\) with \(Y>1\) and a fixed \(\lambda>1\). A bound on the window must hold for every real \(x\) in the interval. Having arbitrarily late windows means that for each \(Y_0\) one can find such a \(Y\ge Y_0\).

## A window versus a sequence of points

With \(Y=100\) and \(\lambda=3/2\), the interval runs from \(100\) through \(1000\). Checking only the endpoints leaves the behavior between them undetermined. Different choices of the auxiliary field may furnish the estimate on different windows in the [[analytic-number-theory/windowed-landau-criterion|analytic criterion]].
