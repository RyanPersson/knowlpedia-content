The **critical line** of a [[analytic-number-theory/dirichlet-l-function|Dirichlet \(L\)-function]] in classical normalization is the line \(\{s\in\mathbb C:\Re s=1/2\}\). It bisects the [[analytic-number-theory/critical-strip|open critical strip]]. Reflection \(s\mapsto1-s\) sends real part \(\sigma\) to \(1-\sigma\), which equals \(\sigma\) precisely on this line.
