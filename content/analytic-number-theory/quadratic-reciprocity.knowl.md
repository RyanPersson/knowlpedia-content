For distinct odd [[analytic-number-theory/prime-number|primes]] \(p,q\), the [[analytic-number-theory/legendre-symbol|Legendre symbols]] satisfy
\[
 (p/q)(q/p)=(-1)^{((p-1)/2)((q-1)/2)}.
\]

## The sign

Interchanging the primes changes the sign exactly when both primes are \(3\bmod4\).
