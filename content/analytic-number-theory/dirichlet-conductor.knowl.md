The **conductor** of a [[analytic-number-theory/dirichlet-character|Dirichlet character]] \(\chi\bmod q\) is the least positive divisor \(f\mid q\) for which there is a character \(\psi\bmod f\) agreeing with \(\chi\) on every integer coprime to \(q\). The inducing character of modulus \(f\) is [[analytic-number-theory/primitive-dirichlet-character|primitive]].
