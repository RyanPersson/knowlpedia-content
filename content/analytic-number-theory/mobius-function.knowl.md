The **Möbius function** \(\mu\) on positive integers is given by \(\mu(1)=1\), by \(\mu(n)=0\) if the square of a [[analytic-number-theory/prime-number|prime]] divides \(n\), and by \(\mu(n)=(-1)^k\) if \(n\) is the product of \(k\) distinct primes.
