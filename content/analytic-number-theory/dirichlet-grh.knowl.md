In the Dirichlet setting, the **generalized Riemann hypothesis** (GRH) states that for every [[analytic-number-theory/dirichlet-character|primitive Dirichlet character]] \(\chi\), each zero of [[analytic-number-theory/dirichlet-l-function|\(L(s,\chi)\)]] in the open strip \(0<\operatorname{Re}s<1\) lies on \(\operatorname{Re}s=\tfrac12\). The character of modulus \(1\) recovers the [[analytic-number-theory/riemann-hypothesis|Riemann hypothesis]].

## Other moduli

An imprimitive character is induced by a primitive one. Its \(L\)-function differs by finitely many [[langlands/euler-product-and-local-factor|Euler factors]] whose zeros have real part zero. This is why the open-strip assertion for primitive characters covers all moduli.
