## Conventions to watch

This collection uses PSL, the full ring of integers, and hyperbolic curvature −1. The ring-valued SL/PSL companions extend the available vocabulary without changing the existing Lie-group definitions. Bianchi identity and Bianchi classification concern different subjects.
