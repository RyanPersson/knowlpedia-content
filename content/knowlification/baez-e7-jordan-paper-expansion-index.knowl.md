This permanent provenance and reading index records 125 new concept knowls,
the new [[nonassociative-algebra|nonassociative-algebra subject page]], and one
expanded existing entry.  The expansion follows John Baez's *Three
Generations in E7* and John C. Baez and Paul Schwahn's *The Standard Model
Gauge Group from the Exceptional Jordan Algebra*.  The order below starts
with reusable prerequisites and ends with the papers' principal constructions
and theorems.
