# Semantic dependency audit

This audit records the agent review of concepts introduced by the knowls for “Galois Extensions and Bundles.” Candidates were checked against the corpus by ID, title, aliases, and body text, then classified by whether they block comprehension of a definition.

## Added as must-have dependencies

- Sieve on an object, including generated, maximal, and pullback sieves
- Relative Kähler differentials
- Locally of finite type
- Scheme over a base and morphism over a base
- Diagonal morphism
- Group scheme
- Sheaf of groups
- Terminal object
- Proj of a graded ring
- Irreducible topological space and irreducible component
- Direct image of a sheaf
- Morphism of sheaves
- Morphism of locally ringed spaces

## Useful candidates deferred

These concepts are link-worthy future additions, but their current occurrences are either explained inline, secondary to the conversation, or recoverable from existing prerequisites:

- open immersion, affine morphism, and jointly surjective family;
- local properties of scheme morphisms;
- presheaf, ringed space, global sections, restriction of a sheaf, regular function, and distinguished open subset;
- valued point, specialization of points, and scheme-theoretic fiber;
- section of a morphism, descent datum, invariant subring, functor of points, geometric point, reduced scheme, disjoint union of schemes, and locally constant map.

## Boundary rule

The audit stops when each remaining term is either linked to an existing knowl, defined inline sufficiently for this document, or deliberately deferred as non-blocking. It does not create a knowl for every mathematical noun phrase.
