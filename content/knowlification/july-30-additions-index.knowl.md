## Conventions and caveats

- **Formal Lie equivalence.** The finite-dimensional equivalence recorded here
  is over a characteristic-zero field and concerns formally smooth formal
  groups on finite formal discs. It is not an equivalence with global Lie
  groups, and positive characteristic or broader complete/pronilpotent
  settings require additional hypotheses.
- **Lorentz signature.** The relativistic geometry and field-equation
  neighborhood uses the \((-+++)\) metric convention
  \(\eta=-dt^2+dx^2+dy^2+dz^2\). A linked linear-algebra model may explicitly
  use the opposite sign; metric, Clifford, wave-operator, and causal-sign
  formulas must then be translated together.
- **Geometric Langlands roles.** For a reductive group \(G\), the automorphic
  side concerns sheaf categories on \(\operatorname{Bun}_G\), while the
  spectral side concerns the moduli of local systems for the Langlands dual
  group \(\widehat G\). This is a categorical correspondence on moduli stacks,
  not a pointwise identification of \(G\)-bundles with \(G\)-local systems.
- **Semirings, hyperfields, and blueprints.** These structures are related by
  embeddings, avatars, adjunctions, and restricted essential images, not by a
  blanket equivalence. In particular, the tropical semifield has
  single-valued idempotent addition whereas the tropical hyperfield has
  multivalued addition.
