Let \({}_AE_B\) be an
[[operator-algebras/imprimitivity-bimodule|imprimitivity bimodule]]. Its
**linking algebra** is the \(C^*\)-algebra
\[
L(E)=
\begin{pmatrix}
A&E\\
\widetilde E&B
\end{pmatrix},
\]
where \(\widetilde E\) is the conjugate \(B\)-\(A\) bimodule and multiplication
uses the left and right inner products of \(E\). Concretely,
\[
L(E)\cong
\mathcal K_B(E\oplus B).
\]
Under this realization, [[operator-algebras/compact-operator-hilbert-module|\(A\cong\mathcal K_B(E)\)]] and \(B\cong\mathcal K_B(B)\)
are the diagonal corners, while \(E\) and \(\widetilde E\) are the
off-diagonal corners. These four pieces are closed under block multiplication
and involution.

## Block operations

Writing an element as
\(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}a&\xi\\ \widetilde\eta&b\end{smallmatrix}\right)\),
the mixed products recover the module actions, and the products of
off-diagonal entries recover the inner products:
\[
\xi\widetilde\eta={}_A\langle\xi,\eta\rangle,\qquad
\widetilde\xi\eta=\langle\xi,\eta\rangle_B.
\]
The adjoint interchanges the two off-diagonal corners. Thus the entire
imprimitivity structure is encoded by ordinary multiplication and involution
inside one \(C^*\)-algebra.

## Full complementary corners

In the [[operator-algebras/multiplier-algebra|multiplier algebra]]
\(M(L(E))\), the two diagonal projections \(p\)
and \(q=1-p\) satisfy
\[
pL(E)p\cong A,\qquad qL(E)q\cong B,\qquad pL(E)q\cong E.
\]
They are full: the ideals generated by either projection are all of \(L(E)\).
Conversely, complementary full corners of a \(C^*\)-algebra determine an
imprimitivity bimodule. This provides a corner characterization of strong
Morita equivalence.

## Conventions and scope

**Warning.** If \(A\) or \(B\) is nonunital, the corner projections generally
belong to \(M(L(E))\), not to \(L(E)\) itself. A block algebra built from a
nonfull correspondence can still be useful, but its diagonal corners are not
complementary full corners and need not witness Morita equivalence.
