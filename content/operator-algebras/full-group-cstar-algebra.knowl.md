Let \(G\) be a [[topology/locally-compact-group|locally compact group]]. For \(f\) in the [[harmonic-analysis/l1-group-algebra|\(L^1\) group algebra]], define
\[
\|f\|_{\max}=\sup_U\left\|\int_G f(s)U_s\,ds\right\|,
\]
where \(U\) ranges over all [[lie-groups/strongly-continuous-unitary-representation|strongly continuous unitary representations]] of \(G\). The full group \(C^*\)-algebra \(C^*(G)\) is the completion of \(L^1(G)/\ker\|\cdot\|_{\max}\) in this norm. Equivalently, it is the enveloping \(C^*\)-algebra obtained from every [[harmonic-analysis/integrated-form-unitary-representation|integrated form]], rather than from one selected representation.

## Universal representation property

Every strongly continuous unitary representation \(U\) of \(G\) integrates uniquely to a [[operator-algebras/nondegenerate-star-homomorphism|nondegenerate]] [[operator-algebras/cstar-representation|representation]] of the [[operator-algebras/cstar-algebra|\(C^*\)-algebra]] \(C^*(G)\). Conversely, every nondegenerate representation of \(C^*(G)\) comes from a unique \(U\). For nondiscrete \(G\), the individual operators \(U_s\) are generally represented by unitaries in the [[operator-algebras/multiplier-algebra|multiplier algebra]], not by elements of \(C^*(G)\) itself [Williams, chapter 2 and appendix A](https://doi.org/10.1090/surv/134).

## Full versus reduced

The [[harmonic-analysis/regular-representations-locally-compact-group|left regular representation]] supplies a canonical quotient
\[
C^*(G)\twoheadrightarrow C_r^*(G)
\]
onto the [[operator-algebras/reduced-group-cstar-algebra|reduced group \(C^*\)-algebra]]. This quotient is an isomorphism when \(G\) is [[harmonic-analysis/amenable-locally-compact-group|amenable]]; it need not be injective for a nonamenable group. The adjective “full” records that all unitary representations contribute to the defining norm.

## Standard examples

For a discrete group, \(C^*(G)\) is the universal \(C^*\)-algebra generated by unitaries satisfying the group relations. For a locally compact abelian group, Fourier transform identifies \(C^*(G)\) with \(C_0(\widehat G)\), where \(\widehat G\) is the [[harmonic-analysis/pontryagin-dual|Pontryagin dual]].
