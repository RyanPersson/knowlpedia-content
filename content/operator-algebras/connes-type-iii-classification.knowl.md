Let \(M\) be a [[operator-algebras/type-iii-factor|type III factor]]. Its **Connes type** is determined by
\[
S(M)=\bigcap_{\varphi}\operatorname{Sp}(\Delta_\varphi),
\]
where \(\varphi\) ranges over [[operator-algebras/normal-semifinite-faithful-weight|faithful normal semifinite weights]] and \(\Delta_\varphi\) is the associated [[operator-algebras/modular-operator|modular operator]]. The factor is type \(\mathrm{III}_0\) when \(S(M)=\{0,1\}\), type \(\mathrm{III}_\lambda\) for \(0<\lambda<1\) when
\[
S(M)=\{0\}\cup\{\lambda^n:n\in\mathbb Z\},
\]
and type \(\mathrm{III}_1\) when \(S(M)=[0,\infty)\). These mutually exclusive cases exhaust type III factors; the subscript records modular spectral behavior, not a dimension or trace value.

## Meaning of the invariant

Changing a faithful [[operator-algebras/normal-weight|normal weight]] changes its [[operator-algebras/modular-automorphism-group|modular automorphism group]] and modular operator, but the intersection defining \(S(M)\) is intrinsic to \(M\). The three possible spectral patterns above give Connes's subdivision of the Murray–von Neumann type III class [Connes, *Une classification des facteurs de type III*](https://doi.org/10.24033/asens.1247).

For \(0<\lambda<1\), the nonzero part of \(S(M)\) is the discrete multiplicative group generated by \(\lambda\). At the endpoints the behavior changes: type \(\mathrm{III}_0\) retains no nontrivial nonzero scale in \(S(M)\), while type \(\mathrm{III}_1\) has every positive scale.

## Scope and limitations

The label is an invariant, not a complete isomorphism classification within each subtype. Distinct nonisomorphic factors can have the same Connes type. Finer invariants, especially the [[operator-algebras/flow-of-weights|flow of weights]] and its refinements, are needed to distinguish them.

**Warning.** The notation \(\mathrm{III}_\lambda\) with \(0<\lambda<1\) denotes a family of subtypes. The symbols \(\mathrm{III}_0\) and \(\mathrm{III}_1\) are separate boundary cases, so substituting \(\lambda=0\) or \(1\) into the displayed discrete set is not the definition.
