Let \(G\) be a [[topology/locally-compact-group|locally compact group]], choose
a left [[harmonic-analysis/haar-measure|Haar measure]], and let
\(\lambda:G\to\mathcal U(L^2(G))\) be the
[[harmonic-analysis/regular-representations-locally-compact-group|left regular representation]], \((\lambda_s\xi)(t)=\xi(s^{-1}t)\). The **group von Neumann
algebra** of \(G\) is
\[
\operatorname{VN}(G)=\{\lambda_s:s\in G\}''\subseteq\mathcal B(L^2(G)).
\]
Equivalently, it is the
[[operator-algebras/weak-operator-topology|weak-operator closure]] of the
unital \(*\)-algebra generated by the operators \(\lambda_s\), by the
[[operator-algebras/von-neumann-bicommutant-theorem|von Neumann bicommutant theorem]]. The notation \(L(G)\) is especially common for discrete groups.

## Basic structure

The [[operator-algebras/commutant|commutant]] of
\(\operatorname{VN}(G)\) is generated by the appropriately normalized right
[[algebra-representation-theory/regular-representation|regular representation]]. The algebra therefore records the
regular representation rather than merely the abstract multiplication law.
For general locally compact \(G\), its canonical noncommutative integral is
the Plancherel weight; this need not be a finite trace.

## Discrete and abelian cases

If \(G\) is discrete, the vector \(\delta_e\) defines the canonical faithful
normal trace
\[
\tau(x)=\langle x\delta_e,\delta_e\rangle.
\]
In this case \(L(G)\) is a factor exactly when every nonidentity conjugacy
class of \(G\) is infinite.
If \(G\) is abelian, Fourier transformation
identifies \(\operatorname{VN}(G)\) with the multiplication algebra
\(L^\infty(\widehat G)\); thus the group von Neumann algebra is commutative.

## Conventions and scope

**Warning.** The reduced group \(C^*\)-algebra is the norm closure of the
integrated left regular representation, whereas \(\operatorname{VN}(G)\) is
its weak-operator closure. These closures generally differ. Some authors
reserve \(L(G)\) for the discrete case and use only \(\operatorname{VN}(G)\)
for nondiscrete groups.
