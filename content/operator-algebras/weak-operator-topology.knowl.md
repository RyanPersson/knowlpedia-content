Let \(H\) be a [[linear-algebra/hilbert-space|Hilbert space]]. The **weak
operator topology** on the
[[operator-algebras/bounded-operator-cstar-algebra|bounded-operator \(C^*\)-algebra]]
\(B(H)\) is the weakest topology making every matrix
coefficient
\[
T\longmapsto \langle T\xi,\eta\rangle
\]
continuous, for \(\xi,\eta\in H\). Equivalently, a net
\((T_i)\) converges to \(T\) in this topology exactly when
\(\langle T_i\xi,\eta\rangle\to\langle T\xi,\eta\rangle\) for every pair
\(\xi,\eta\in H\). Thus weak operator convergence tests operators on fixed
vectors and then tests the resulting vectors weakly. It is weaker than
operator-norm and strong-operator convergence.

## Locally convex description

The weak operator topology is generated by the seminorms
\[
p_{\xi,\eta}(T)=|\langle T\xi,\eta\rangle|.
\]
Consequently, a basic neighborhood of \(T_0\) imposes finitely many bounds
\(p_{\xi_j,\eta_j}(T-T_0)<\varepsilon\). The same definition applies to
\(B(H,K)\), with \(\xi\in H\) and \(\eta\in K\). Nets, rather than sequences,
are required to describe this topology in full generality.

## Comparison with other weak topologies

The weak operator topology is generally strictly weaker than the Banach-space
weak topology \(\sigma(B(H),B(H)^*)\). The ultraweak topology instead uses all
functionals in the trace-class predual of \(B(H)\). It is finer than the weak
operator topology, but the two induce the same topology on every norm-bounded
subset. In particular, every weak-operator convergent net is norm bounded and
also converges ultraweakly.

## Algebraic continuity

The adjoint operation is weak-operator continuous, and left or right
multiplication by a fixed operator is weak-operator continuous. Joint
multiplication is not weak-operator continuous, even on the unit ball. By
contrast, on uniformly norm-bounded nets, strong convergence of both factors
implies strong convergence of their products; this is a property of the
[[operator-algebras/strong-operator-topology|strong operator topology]].
[[operator-algebras/von-neumann-algebra|Von Neumann algebras]] are precisely
the unital self-adjoint subalgebras of \(B(H)\) that are weak-operator closed.
