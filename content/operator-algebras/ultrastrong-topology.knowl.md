Let \(H\) be a [[linear-algebra/hilbert-space|Hilbert space]]. The **ultrastrong operator topology**, or **\(\sigma\)-strong topology**, on [[operator-algebras/bounded-operator-cstar-algebra|\(\mathcal B(H)\)]] is the [[functional-analysis/topology-generated-by-seminorms|locally convex topology generated by the seminorms]]
\[
p_{\boldsymbol{\xi}}(T)
=\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\|T\xi_n\|^2\right)^{1/2},
\]
where \(\boldsymbol{\xi}=(\xi_n)\) ranges over all square-summable sequences in \(H\). Thus \(T_i\to T\) ultrastrongly exactly when \(p_{\boldsymbol{\xi}}(T_i-T)\to0\) for every such sequence. A concrete [[operator-algebras/von-neumann-algebra|von Neumann algebra]] \(M\subseteq\mathcal B(H)\) carries the topology inherited from this one.

## Equivalent description by normal functionals

On a von Neumann algebra \(M\), the \(\sigma\)-strong topology is generated by
\[
x\longmapsto \varphi(x^*x)^{1/2},
\]
as \(\varphi\) ranges over the [[operator-algebras/positive-linear-functional|positive linear functionals]] on \(M\) that are [[operator-algebras/normal-functional|normal]]. Such a functional on a concrete \(M\) can be expressed using a square-summable family of vector functionals, which recovers the seminorms in the core. This intrinsic formulation shows that the inherited topology does not depend on a chosen faithful [[operator-algebras/normal-representation|normal representation]] [Takesaki, Chapter III, §2].

## Comparison with other operator topologies

Ultrastrong convergence implies [[operator-algebras/strong-operator-topology|strong-operator convergence]], since a sequence with only one nonzero vector is allowed. Conversely, the two topologies induce the same topology on every norm-bounded subset of \(\mathcal B(H)\) [Takesaki, Chapter III, §2]. They differ on the whole algebra in infinite dimension: a strong neighborhood controls finitely many vectors, whereas one ultrastrong seminorm can impose a square-summed condition on an entire countable family.

The ultrastrong topology is stronger than the [[operator-algebras/ultraweak-topology|ultraweak topology]]. On norm-bounded sets, ultrastrong convergence still need not agree with ultraweak convergence.

## Conventions and scope

Some sources write “strong topology” for the topology generated by the normal-functional seminorms when the ambient algebra is already a von Neumann algebra. Here “strong operator topology” means pointwise norm convergence, while “ultrastrong” means the square-summable refinement above. Applying the definition simultaneously to \(x\) and \(x^*\) gives the [[operator-algebras/ultrastrong-star-topology|ultrastrong-star topology]], which is a different topology.
