Let \(X\) be a
[[operator-algebras/cstar-correspondence|\(C^*\)-correspondence]] over
\(A\). The **Toeplitz–Pimsner algebra** \(\mathcal T_X\) is the
[[operator-algebras/universal-cstar-algebra|universal \(C^*\)-algebra]]
generated by a
[[operator-algebras/toeplitz-representation-cstar-correspondence|Toeplitz
representation]] \((i_X,i_A)\) of \(X\): for every Toeplitz representation
\((t,\pi)\) of \(X\) in a \(C^*\)-algebra \(B\), there is a unique
\(*\)-homomorphism
\[
\pi\times t:\mathcal T_X\longrightarrow C^*(\pi(A),t(X))
\]
satisfying \((\pi\times t)(i_A(a))=\pi(a)\) and
\((\pi\times t)(i_X(\xi))=t(\xi)\). Thus \(\mathcal T_X\) imposes exactly the
Toeplitz relations, with no Cuntz–Pimsner covariance relation.

## Fock realization

The creation operators on the
[[operator-algebras/fock-module-cstar-correspondence|Fock module]]
\(\mathcal F(X)\), together with the diagonal left action of \(A\), generate
a concrete copy of \(\mathcal T_X\). This realization proves existence of the
universal algebra and makes tensor degree visible.
The canonical map \(i_A\) is injective because the degree-zero summand carries
the faithful left-multiplication representation of \(A\).

## Gauge action and quotient

There is a circle action \(\gamma:\mathbb T\to\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal T_X)\)
determined by
\[
\gamma_z(i_A(a))=i_A(a),\qquad
\gamma_z(i_X(\xi))=z\,i_X(\xi).
\]
The Cuntz–Pimsner algebra is obtained by quotienting \(\mathcal T_X\) by the
ideal generated by the appropriate covariance relations. Keeping
\(\mathcal T_X\) separate records all Toeplitz representations, including
ones that do not descend to that quotient.

## Examples and conventions

For \(A=X=\mathbb C\), the Fock module is \(\ell^2(\mathbb N)\), the creation
operator is the unilateral shift, and \(\mathcal T_X\) is the classical
Toeplitz algebra. For \(X=\mathbb C^d\), it is generated by \(d\) isometries
with pairwise orthogonal ranges, without requiring their range projections
to sum to the identity.

**Warning.** The name “Toeplitz algebra” also denotes unrelated algebras of
Toeplitz operators. The subscript \(X\) and the universal property specify
the correspondence construction.
