Let \(A\) be a [[operator-algebras/cstar-algebra|\(C^*\)-algebra]]. A
**\(C^*\)-subalgebra** of \(A\) is a complex
[[convex-analysis/linear-subspace|linear subspace]] \(B\subseteq A\) that is
closed under multiplication and involution and is closed in the norm of
\(A\). With the inherited operations and norm, \(B\) is itself a
\(C^*\)-algebra. If \(A\) is unital, the definition does not by itself
require \(1_A\in B\); that extra condition defines a unital
\(C^*\)-subalgebra in the unit-preserving convention.

## Generated \(C^*\)-subalgebras

For a subset \(S\subseteq A\), the \(C^*\)-subalgebra generated by \(S\),
written \(C^*(S)\), is the intersection of all \(C^*\)-subalgebras containing
\(S\). Equivalently, it is the norm closure of the algebraic
\(*\)-algebra generated by \(S\). In a unital context, \(C^*(S,1_A)\) records
that the ambient identity is also adjoined. This notation prevents an
otherwise common ambiguity about whether generated subalgebras are required
to be unital.

## Automatic completeness and spectral behavior

Norm closedness makes \(B\) complete. Moreover, a \(C^*\)-subalgebra has the
same spectral behavior for each of its elements as the ambient algebra after
units are handled consistently; this is [[operator-algebras/spectral-permanence|spectral permanence]]. Consequently
[[operator-algebras/continuous-functional-calculus|continuous functional
calculus]] performed in \(A\) for a normal element \(b\in B\) remains inside
the unital \(C^*\)-subalgebra generated by \(b\). These facts would fail for a
merely algebraic \(*\)-subalgebra.

## Ideals and examples

Every [[operator-algebras/closed-two-sided-ideal|closed two-sided ideal]] is a
\(C^*\)-subalgebra, but most \(C^*\)-subalgebras are not ideals. Diagonal
matrices form a commutative \(C^*\)-subalgebra of \(M_n(\mathbb C)\) but are
not an ideal when \(n>1\). The
[[operator-algebras/compact-operator-cstar-algebra|compact operators]] form
both a \(C^*\)-subalgebra and a [[algebra-rings/two-sided-ideal|two-sided ideal]] in \(B(\mathcal H)\).
Polynomial functions inside \(C([0,1])\) are an algebraic
\(*\)-subalgebra but not a \(C^*\)-subalgebra because they are not norm
closed.
