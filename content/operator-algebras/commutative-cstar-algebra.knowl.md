A **commutative \(C^*\)-algebra**, also called an **abelian
\(C^*\)-algebra**, is a [[operator-algebras/cstar-algebra|\(C^*\)-algebra]]
\(A\) whose multiplication satisfies
\[
ab=ba\qquad\text{for all }a,b\in A.
\]
Commutativity concerns the algebra product, not merely commutation of a
distinguished family of elements. The algebra may be unital or nonunital. Its
involution, norm, and completeness are still part of the \(C^*\)-structure,
so a commutative \(C^*\)-algebra is more than an abstract commutative
\(*\)-algebra.

## Function-algebra model

If \(X\) is a [[topology/locally-compact-space|locally compact]]
[[topology/hausdorff-space|Hausdorff space]], the continuous complex-valued
functions vanishing at infinity form a commutative \(C^*\)-algebra
\(C_0(X)\), with pointwise operations, complex conjugation, and the supremum
norm. It is unital exactly when \(X\) is compact. Familiar special cases
include \(C(X)\) for compact \(X\), \(c_0\) for a countable discrete space,
and \(\mathbb C^n\) for a finite discrete space.

## Gelfand duality

For a commutative \(C^*\)-algebra \(A\), let \(\widehat A\) be its space of
nonzero characters with the
[[functional-analysis/weak-star-topology|weak-star topology]]. The Gelfand
transform
\[
a\longmapsto\widehat a,\qquad \widehat a(\chi)=\chi(a),
\]
is an isometric \(*\)-isomorphism \(A\cong C_0(\widehat A)\). Conversely,
evaluation at points recovers the character space of \(C_0(X)\). This is the
commutative Gelfand--Naimark theorem.

## Subalgebras and generated examples

A family of commuting
[[quantum-foundations/normal-operator|normal operators]] on a
[[linear-algebra/hilbert-space|Hilbert space]] generates a commutative
[[operator-algebras/concrete-cstar-algebra|concrete \(C^*\)-algebra]]. The
[[operator-algebras/continuous-functional-calculus|continuous functional
calculus]] for one normal element \(a\) identifies the unital algebra it
generates with continuous functions on the spectrum of \(a\). By contrast, an
algebra generated by noncommuting operators is generally noncommutative even
when each generator is normal.
