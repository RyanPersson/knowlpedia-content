On 20 November 2026, Levent Alpöge announced an argument developed with Claude for the [[analytic-number-theory/riemann-hypothesis|Riemann hypothesis]] and its [[analytic-number-theory/dirichlet-grh|Dirichlet generalization]]. His note describes [[arithmetic-geometry/iterated-theta-lifts|iterated theta lifts]] as an arithmetic counterpart of multiplicity in [[arithmetic-geometry/stepanov-method|Stepanov’s method]].

Przemek Chojecki wrote the [[posts/chojecki-riemann-hypothesis|post annotated here]]. “Fable+Astra” is the credit he gives for help with his summary. The screenshot gives no further attribution.
