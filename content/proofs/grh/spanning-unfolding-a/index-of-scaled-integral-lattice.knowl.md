**Lemma.** Let \(E\) be a [[?c:CM field|CM field]] (containing a primitive 5040-th root of unity, as assumed in the source), \(\widehat{\mathcal O}\) the ring of integral [[catalog/arithmetic/adeles-number-field-finite|finite adeles]] of \(E\), \(\iota\) a finite set, and \(c\) a unit of the finite adeles with \(c\in\widehat{\mathcal O}\). Then
\[
\bigl[\widehat{\mathcal O}^{\iota}:c\,\widehat{\mathcal O}^{\iota}\bigr]=\bigl[\widehat{\mathcal O}:c\,\widehat{\mathcal O}\bigr]^{\lvert\iota\rvert},
\]
and in particular this index is finite and nonzero. Here \([\widehat{\mathcal O}:c\widehat{\mathcal O}]\) is the [[?lean:New.genIndex|index of the ideal generated by \(c\)]], a positive integer, which equals the inverse of the factor by which multiplication by \(c\) scales [[harmonic-analysis/haar-measure|Haar measure]] on the finite adeles.

## Proof

\(c\,\widehat{\mathcal O}^{\iota}\) is the \(\widehat{\mathcal O}\)-span of the columns of the scalar matrix \(c\cdot1\), which is invertible with integral entries and has determinant \(c^{\lvert\iota\rvert}\). For an invertible matrix \(A\) with integral entries the index of its column lattice in \(\widehat{\mathcal O}^{\iota}\) is the index of \(\det A\) ([[?lean:New.submoduleIndex_colLattice_intVectors|index of a column lattice]]), and the index is multiplicative in the generator, so the index of \(c^{\lvert\iota\rvert}\) is the \(\lvert\iota\rvert\)-th power of that of \(c\).
