This chapter is purely local and algebraic. It studies the action of \(\mathrm{GL}_k(W)\), for a local ring \(W\) with finite residue field of \(q=P^{N}\) elements, on forms in \(N\) sets of \(k\) variables, where the group acts on the \(s\)-th set through a ring map \(\iota_s\colon W\to R\) to a commutative ring \(R\) (in the main theorem a valuation domain \(O\) of characteristic \(0\) in which \(p\) is not a unit; in the counting arguments a field of characteristic \(p\)), the maps being successive \(P\)-th powers of one another modulo \(p\), and alternately through \(g\) and through its inverse transpose. Its outcome, the theorem on elementary divisors of the edge Hecke operator, is used in the divisibility of the composite lift at an inert prime ([[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/elementary-divisors-of-edge-hecke-operator|statement]]; it is applied in the area on Hecke edges).

All ten source files of the folder are translated; every proof was read in full.

## 1. The setting: Frobenius cycles, twisted action, depth and defect

A [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/frobenius-cycle|Frobenius cycle]] \(T\) fixes the prime \(p\), the power \(P=p^{f}\ge5\), the maps \(\iota_s\) for the *letters* \(s\in\mathbb{Z}/N\), their parities, and \(w\) with \(w^{2}=p\). The variables are \(x_{b,s}\) (\(b<k\)), the exponents \(\beta=(\beta_s)_s\) have degree \(n\) in each letter, and \(g\in\mathrm{GL}_k(W)\) acts by the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/twisted-action|twisted action]], whose matrix on coefficient vectors is \(\rho_n(g)=\bigotimes_s\operatorname{Sym}^n(g_s)\). The maps \(\iota_s\) [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/maps-of-cycle-kill-maximal-ideal|send the maximal ideal into \(pR\)]], so parahoric elements act by matrices that are block triangular modulo \(p\).

Two numerical invariants of a monomial are used throughout. The [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/depth-of-monomial|depth]] for a cut \(r\) is the degree in the *deep* variables (even letter and \(b\ge r\), or odd letter and \(b<r\)). The [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/defect-of-monomial|defect]] at a letter \(i\) along a root \((a,a')\) is a real weighted degree whose weights are supported on the two variables \(Y_s,X_s\) of the root in each letter: \(+P^{[s-i]}/(q-1)\) at \(Y_s\) and \(-P^{[s-i]}/(q-1)\) at \(X_s\), with \(1\) added at \(Y_i\), so that the weight of \(Y_i\) is \(q/(q-1)\). It is [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/defect-weight-bounds|bounded by twice the degree]]. Conditions on all translates by a Levi subgroup define the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/translate-conditions|defect conditions and the deep forms]].

## 2. The root lemma

The central tool compares zeros and degree of an auxiliary polynomial in one variable. [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/stepanov-count|Stepanov's count]] says that a nonzero polynomial vanishing to order \(E\) at the images of all residue classes has degree at least \(Eq\). Divisibility by powers of \(w\) shifted by weights of the variables is organised by the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/gauss-filtration|Gauss filtration]], which is [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/substitution-respects-gauss-filtration|compatible with substitutions]]. For a root and a letter one introduces the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/root-data|\(\mathrm{SL}_2\) of the root and the auxiliary substitution]]; expanding the substituted polynomial at infinity and at the \(q\) finite points gives [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/zeros-against-degree|zeros against degree]], and hence the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/root-lemma|root lemma]]: a term of least order plus weight among all \(\mathrm{SL}_2\)-translates has defect \(\le0\). In the quantitative form used later, [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/large-defect-forces-divisibility|a large defect forces a divisible coefficient]].

## 3. An average on polynomials in the entries of a matrix

Independently of Frobenius cycles, one constructs for a Levi subgroup \(L_\tau\) an [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/conjugation-invariant-average|average]]: a linear form on polynomials in a generic matrix, invariant under conjugation by \(L_\tau(W)\), vanishing on monomials of large row weight (and automatically on [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/invariant-forms-vanish-on-unbalanced-monomials|unbalanced monomials]]), and congruent to \(p^{k^{2}f}\) times evaluation at \(1\) on polynomials whose conjugates have no such monomials. The construction uses [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/adjoint-weights|adjoint weights]] from multinomial coefficients, the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/schur-algebra-of-levi|Schur algebra of \(L_\tau\)]] and the operator dual to a polynomial, the fact that [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/levi-points-span-schur-algebra|points of \(L_\tau(W)\) span the Schur algebra]] in degrees below the size of the residue field (via a [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/density-of-points-over-local-ring|density lemma]]), the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/large-weight-submodule|large weight submodule]] \(U_+\), and the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/dual-operator-conjugation|conjugation and trace rules]]. The average is the trace on the tensor power minus the trace on \(U_+\): [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/existence-of-average|existence theorem]].

## 4. The volume of the Pieri cone

A piece of real geometry: for the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/step-map-pieri-cone-slab|step map, the Pieri cone and the degree slab]], the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/interlacing-determinant|interlacing determinant]], the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/determinant-of-step-map|determinant of the step map]] and a [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/signed-count-of-positive-steps|signed count over permutations]] give the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/volume-of-cone-in-slab-signed-sum|share of the cone in a slab as a signed sum]] depending only on \(k\) and \(q\); evaluating it for one letter yields the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/volume-of-pieri-cone-in-slab|volume theorem]]: the cone fills the fraction \(\prod_{l<k}(q-1)/(q^{l+1}-1)\), the inverse of the number of full flags over \(\mathbb{F}_q\).

## 5. Few forms have only deep translates

Over a field of characteristic \(p\), a form all of whose translates have depth \(\ge\tau\) vanishes to order \(\tau\) at the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/unipotent-points-for-deep-forms|points attached to unipotent matrices]]. These points [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/points-satisfy-low-power-equations|satisfy equations with small leading powers]], so a combinatorial Nullstellensatz with multiplicity gives [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/deep-forms-have-heavy-monomial|a monomial of large weight]]; averaging the weight over all monomials bounds the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/sum-of-dimensions-of-deep-forms|sum of the dimensions of the spaces of deep forms]] by the divisor constant \(Nn(P^{-2r}+r^{2}/(kP))\) times the number of exponents.

## 6. Edges, halfway lattices and duality

The cosets of a two-block parahoric subgroup index the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/edges-and-halfway-lattices|edges]]; scaling the coefficient at \(\beta\) by \(w^{\operatorname{depth}\beta}\) passes to the halfway lattice of an edge, which [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/parahoric-preserves-halfway-lattice|is preserved by the stabiliser of the edge]]. The [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/lattices-of-forms-are-stable|lattices of monomials and divided powers are stable]], and an [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/edge-pairing|edge pairing]] makes \(\rho_n(g)\) adjoint to \(\rho_n({}^tg)\). For a finite set of origins and all edges from them, the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/origin-functions|functions on edges that depend only on the origin]] form [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/origin-functions-summand-of-small-colength|a direct summand in which the integral functions on origins have colength bounded by the dimensions of the deep forms]] of section 5. Finally, by the root lemma, [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/halfway-forms-divisible-at-large-defect|forms in all halfway lattices are divisible by powers of \(w\) at monomials of large defect]].

## 7. Frobenius powers of generic matrices

A Frobenius cycle gives no algebraic formula for the maps \(g\mapsto\iota_s(g)\) in general; what it guarantees is the congruence between them modulo \(p\). One therefore substitutes \(N\) generic matrices: the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/substitution-operator|substitution operator]] lets the entrywise \(P^{d}\)-th power of the copy \(s-d\) act on the letter \(s\). [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/substitution-operator-conjugation|Conjugating the factors]] by a block diagonal \(h\) conjugates the operator by \(\rho_n(h)\). With the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/copy-weight|copy weight]] of a monomial in the copies, [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/levels-of-substitution-operator|filtration levels telescope along the product]], the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/conjugated-frobenius-factors-level|conjugated factors keep their level]], and the result is [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/weight-and-defect|weight against defect]]: a monomial whose coefficient in row \(\beta\) is not divisible by \(w^{c}\) has copy weight within \(3kc\) of the defect of \(\beta\).

## 8. The dimension of the forms of bounded defect

In characteristic \(p\), the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/defect-cone|defect cone]] with bound \(0\) is [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/defect-of-steps|the image of the Pieri cone]]. [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/power-packing|Packing]] \(m^{kN}\) forms into one polynomial in \(m\)-th powers (\(m\) a power of \(q\)), taking [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/least-height-term-at-a-flag|a term of least height, which occurs at a flag]], and [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/integral-shift-into-pieri-cone|shifting by an integral vector]] bounds the dimension [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/bounded-defect-dimension-by-lattice-points|by flags times lattice points, hence by a volume]]. With the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/number-of-flags-bound|bound on the number of flags]] and section 4 this gives the theorem that [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/few-forms-of-bounded-defect|few forms have bounded defect]]: the parahoric index times the dimension is at most \((1+\theta)\) times the number of exponents.

## 9. Averaging and the theorem on elementary divisors

The average of section 3 is now applied to the substitution of section 7. Applying linear forms in the copies gives [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/partial-average|contractions and the partial averages \(\mathrm{Av}_J\)]]; a [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/contraction-change-of-frame|change of frame]] by a block diagonal matrix conjugates the remaining copy. Because invariant forms kill unbalanced monomials, the copy weight reduces to a row weight, and one gets [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/weight-and-defect-in-one-copy|weight against defect in one copy]]: bad monomials of the remaining copy correspond to rows of large defect. Adding one copy at a time yields [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/averaging-map|the averaging map]] \(\mathrm{Av}\): its values have no term of defect above \(9kc\) modulo \(w^{c}\) in any block diagonal translate, and \(\langle\mathrm{Av}\,h,h'\rangle_b\equiv p^{Nc_0}\langle h,h'\rangle_b\) modulo \(w^{c}\) for \(h'\) in all halfway lattices.

## 10. The theorem on elementary divisors

The operator of interest is the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/edge-hecke-operator|edge Hecke operator]] \(\mathbb{A}\) attached to edge coordinates; the hypotheses are the [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/large-degree-conditions|conditions of large degree]], with their [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/consequences-of-large-degree|numerical consequences]]. Write \(M\) for the integral functions on origins, \(M_{\mathrm{all}}\) for the functions on edges that depend only on the origin, and \(M_{\mathrm{edge}}\) for all functions on edges. On vectors multiplied by the pairing weights, [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/hecke-operator-factors-through-edges|\(\mathbb{A}\) is the composite]] \(M\subseteq M_{\mathrm{all}}\subseteq M_{\mathrm{edge}}\xrightarrow{\mathrm{Add}}\) (duals), where [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/pairing-map-and-near-inverse|\(\mathrm{Add}\) pairs with the functions on the dual edges and has a near inverse]] built from the averaging map. Sections 8 and 9 produce [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/edge-conditions|conditions on an edge]]: a module \(C\) of congruences modulo \(w^{c}\), \(c=5Nc_0\), satisfied by \(M_{\mathrm{all}}\) and by the values of the near inverse, together with a subspace \(S\) over the residue field of \(O\) that contains the reduction of every vector \(h\) with \(w^{c-1}h\in C\) (not only the reductions of \(C\) itself), of dimension at most \((1+\theta)\) times the number of exponents divided by the number of edges into a lattice. A Nakayama argument, which uses this property of \(S\), then shows that [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/few-further-generators|the image of \(\mathrm{Add}\) on \(M_{\mathrm{all}}\) needs only a proportion \(\theta\) of further generators]] to contain \(p^{2Nc_0}\) times everything. Both ends \(M\subseteq M_{\mathrm{all}}\) have small colength by sections 5 and 6, and colengths add along the chain: [[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/colength-of-factored-operator|colength of the factored operator]]. Transporting along any map \(f\) intertwining \(\mathbb{A}\) with an operator \(Y\) gives the main result:

**Theorem** ([[proofs/grh/upper-elementary-divisors/elementary-divisors-of-edge-hecke-operator|elementary divisors of the edge Hecke operator]]). Let \(O\) be a valuation ring that is a domain of characteristic \(0\) in which \(p\) is not a unit, let \(X,H,I\) be finite with edge coordinates as above, and let \(f\) be an \(O\)-linear map from the functions \(X\to O^{I}\) to an \(O\)-module with \(Y\circ f=f\circ\mathbb{A}\). Under the conditions of large degree, \(Y\) preserves \(L=\operatorname{im}f\), and for every \(m\) the colength of \(Y(L)+p^{m}L\) in \(L\) divides \(p^{l}\) for some \(l\le\bigl(3Nn(P^{-2r}+r^{2}/(kP))+\theta m\bigr)\,\#X\,\#I\).
