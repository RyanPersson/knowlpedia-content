This chapter covers eight files of the part of the argument called *unfolding, and the proof of the moments*. Its subject is the **filling theorem**: a Petersson-type second moment estimate which shows that translates of Poincaré series are spread over the target forms. The chapter sets up the data, analyses the rational elements ("terms") that occur when the second moment is unfolded, and deduces the moment bounds from estimates proved in neighbouring chapters (the remaining terms, the main term). The companion chapter on the first eleven files of the same folder treats cutoffs, amplitude sums, climbing and Poincaré series.

## 1. The data

A [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/filling-data|filling datum]] \(S\) lives over a CM field \(E\) with \(f\) infinite places and has a shape \((\varrho,\kappa,k_0)\): the hermitian space is
\[
V=(\varrho\ \text{hyperbolic planes})\oplus X_0\oplus Ez,\qquad h(z,z)=-N,
\]
with \(X_0\) definite of rank \(k=\lvert\kappa\rvert\); \(n=\varrho+1\) and \(r=2\varrho+k+1\). The *frame* is \(f=(e_0,\dots,e_{\varrho-1},z)\). The datum contains a split prime \(N=\pi\bar\pi\), two level primes \(p_0,p_1\) with chosen places, \(M=p_0p_1\), a generator \(\varpi\) of the different, a free level in \(U(X_0)\), a Bessel system at each infinite place, and many size conditions in a real parameter \(b\). From it one builds the [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/filling-unitary-groups|unitary groups and the maps]] \(\gamma\mapsto\gamma_\infty\) (to the product \(G_\infty\) of real groups, rescaled) and \(\gamma\mapsto\gamma_{\mathrm{fin}}\) (to the finite adelic group in the lattice basis); the [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/filling-level-and-reduction|lattice stabiliser \(K\), the finite residue group \(\bar G\), the reduction and the principal level \(K(M)\)]]; the [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/filling-classes-and-terms|classes \(c\), the terms over a block \((c,s,c')\), the frame products \(Z(\gamma)\) and the discriminant]]; and a list of [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/filling-constants|constants]], among them the unipotent covolume \(\nu\), the *identity second moment* \(\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{id}}\) and the *main second moment* \(\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{main}}\). The [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/filling-admissible|standing hypotheses]] (admissibility) compare all of these with powers of \(b\).

The analytic objects are the [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/class-cutoff|class cutoffs]] \(\beta_c\) on \(G_\infty\), which exist because the unipotent groups of a class are [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/class-unipotent-discrete|discrete]], the notion of a [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/box-partition-of-unity|box partition of unity]], and the [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/filling-amplitude|amplitude]] of a term,
\[
A(c,c';\gamma)=\int_{G_\infty}\beta_c(y)\beta_{c'}(\gamma_\infty^{-1}y)\,W(y)\otimes\overline{W(\gamma_\infty^{-1}y)}\,dy ,
\]
together with the amplitude sums \(A_T(c,s,c')\) over sets \(T\) of terms. The [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/unipotent-character-agreement-stability|unipotent character]] \(\psi_{\mathsf U}\), *stable* sets and the terms of a given link complete the vocabulary; [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/frame-products-at-infinity|frame products at infinity]] are rescaled rational frame products.

## 2. The Bessel level structure

The second file relates the rational, real and finite adelic sides of the Bessel groups \(\mathsf{U}\subset\mathsf{S}=\mathsf{U}H\). Terms [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/terms-compose-like-a-groupoid|compose like a groupoid]] and are described through the [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/class-basis|class basis]]; the Bessel groups [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/bessel-groups-in-the-lattice-basis|do not change under the change to the lattice basis]]. The product formula for the additive character turns the [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/unipotent-character-at-infinity|character of \(\mathsf{U}\) at infinity]] into the conjugate of the finite character, which gives the [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/bessel-function-under-unipotent-translation|transformation law of the Bessel function]]. On the finite side, the form in the lattice basis is [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/lattice-middle-form-is-unimodular|unimodular]], \(\widehat{\mathcal{O}}\) is [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/self-duality-of-integral-adeles|self-dual]] for the character, and [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/principal-level-is-congruence-subgroup|\(K(M)\) is the congruence subgroup modulo \(M\)]]. [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/eichler-map-in-bessel-unipotent-group|Eichler maps]] give explicit unipotent elements, and [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/strong-approximation-for-bessel-unipotent-group|strong approximation for \(\mathsf{U}\)]] shows that [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/rational-unipotent-terms-reach-all-reductions|rational unipotent terms reach every reduction]] with the right [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/unipotent-character-on-level-cosets|character]], and that [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/bessel-subgroup-reductions-reached-by-rational-terms|reductions of the Bessel subgroup are reached]]. Freeness of the level gives that [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/bessel-subgroup-terms-in-principal-level|rational elements of \(\mathsf{S}\) in the principal level are unipotent]]; a general [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/freeness-in-small-rank|freeness statement in rank \(\le k\)]] is used later. The file also defines the [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/finite-head-weight|weight of a head at the finite places]].

## 3. Terms: cell rank, nearness, integrality

For a rational \(\gamma\) one compares the flag of the frame with the flag of its image. The [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/cell-rank-of-a-term|cell rank]] records from which index on the two flags share vectors with leading coefficients \((\zeta,1)\), \(\zeta\bar\zeta=1\). A [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/norm-identity-for-combinations-of-the-moved-frame|norm identity]] shows that the [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/term-discriminant-is-totally-positive-or-zero|discriminant is totally positive or zero]]; its real counterpart is [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/real-gram-discriminant-of-a-term|the real Gram discriminant]], and norm-one pairs [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/norm-one-pairs-at-infinite-places|pass to the infinite places]]. [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/near-term|Near terms]] have [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/bounds-for-near-terms|frame products bounded by powers of \(b\)]], and frame products of terms are [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/frame-products-of-terms-are-integral|algebraic integers]] (via [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/class-coordinates|class coordinates]]). Two terms with [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/terms-with-the-same-frame-image|the same image of the frame]] differ by the Bessel subgroup. Further vocabulary: [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/characters-agree|agreement of characters]], the [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/common-link|common link]], the [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/term-discriminant-norm-and-pair-count-scale|discriminant norm and pair count scale]], and [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/tail-scale-inequalities|inequalities for the tail scale]].

## 4. The link and the link isometry

The link of \(\gamma\) is the set of pairs of classes in \(A^\perp/A=X_0\perp Ez_N\) of \(y\) and \(\gamma^{-1}y\), for \(y\) orthogonal to \(A=\langle e_a\rangle\) and \(\gamma A\) ([[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/link-in-coordinates|in coordinates]]). Links, minors, discriminants and hence cells are [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/link-and-cells-are-invariant-under-the-unipotent-group|invariant under \(\mathsf{U}\) on both sides]] (using [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/norm-one-pairs-under-inversion-and-unipotent-multiplication|the behaviour of norm-one pairs]]); on the big cell [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/discriminant-and-link-on-the-big-cell|the link is a graph and determines the middle isometry]]. Exchanging the \(z\)-coordinates of the two members of a pair ([[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/positive-space-and-link-maps|source and target]]) turns the link, which has [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/rank-of-the-link|rank \(k+1\)]], into the graph of the [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/link-isometry|link isometry]] \(\Upsilon_\gamma\) of the positive space \(X_0\perp Ez^+\) ([[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/link-is-graph-of-link-isometry|theorem]]); equivalently \((A+\gamma A)^\perp/(A\cap\gamma A)\) [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/isotropic-complement-maps-onto-positive-space|is the positive space]], and the link is [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/link-is-its-own-orthogonal-complement|its own orthogonal complement]]. Evaluating \(\Upsilon_\gamma\) [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/link-isometry-on-the-moved-frame|on the moved frame]] gives the criterion: [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/vanishing-of-the-term-discriminant|the discriminant vanishes exactly when the two frames share a nonzero vector]].

## 5. The structure of a term

With the [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/spaces-of-the-two-frames|spaces of the two frames]], the orthogonal complement of both frames modulo \(I_\gamma=A\cap\gamma A\) is [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/frame-complement-and-linked-subspace|isometric to the linked subspace \(X_0^\gamma=X_0\cap\Upsilon_\gamma^{-1}X_0\)]], whose [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/rank-of-the-linked-subspace|rank is \(k-1\) or \(k\)]]; the common link is described [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/common-link-by-link-isometries|by link isometries]]. For agreeing terms of discriminant zero, [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/flags-of-a-term-and-the-cell-rank|\(A=A_{\le j}\oplus I_\gamma\) and the cell rank is \(\varrho-\dim I_\gamma\)]]. All of this is [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/unipotent-group-acts-on-the-structure-of-a-term|compatible with the unipotent group]]. The [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/remaining-terms|remaining terms]] \(\Gamma^*\) form a stable set whose [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/remaining-terms-stable-and-complement|complement among agreeing terms]] consists of the terms with norm-one pairs from index zero.

## 6. The expansion of the squares

Terms with norm-one pairs from index zero are [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/terms-of-cell-rank-zero-are-scalar-multiples-of-the-bessel-subgroup|scalar multiples of the Bessel subgroup]] (see [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/scalar-isometry|scalar isometries]]); so over a set of residues [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/avoiding-scalar-cosets|avoiding the scalar cosets]] the terms off the Bessel subgroup [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/terms-off-the-bessel-subgroup-reduce-to-remaining-terms|reduce to the remaining terms]]. The [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/blocks-of-the-bessel-subgroup|blocks of the Bessel subgroup]] are computed exactly. The [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/expansion-of-the-squares|expansion of the squares]] bounds the second moment of the terms off \(\mathsf{S}\) by a link-by-link bound, and gives the [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/second-moment-near-the-bessel-subgroup|second moment near the Bessel subgroup]], using [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/residues-near-the-reduced-bessel-group|properties of residues near \(\bar R_0\)]].

## 7. The second moment over the whole group

Splitting \(\bar G\) into [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/second-moment-over-the-scalar-cosets|scalar cosets]], [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/second-moment-over-open-elements-of-a-fixer|open elements]] (the main term) and [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/non-open-elements-carry-little|non-open elements]] (small, by a determinant sieve for functions of [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/positive-type-function-is-largest-at-the-identity|positive type]]), and running a maximality argument over [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/second-moment-over-a-full-fixer|fixers of frame vectors]], one obtains the [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/second-moment-over-the-whole-group|bound \((1+9b^{-4})\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{main}}\) for the sum of all second moments]]; see also [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/second-moment-off-the-reduced-bessel-group|second moments off \(\bar R_0\)]].

## 8. Operators and good forms

For a linear map \(P\) from the source \(\mathbb{C}^{\mathcal{C}\times I}\) to a representation \(\rho\) of \(\bar G\) with [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/amplitude-blocks|amplitude blocks]], put \(P_s=P^*\rho(s)P\); its [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/hilbert-schmidt-norms-of-the-blocks|Hilbert–Schmidt norms are second moments]]. The [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/bessel-translate|twisted translations]] \(r_\psi\) form a [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/bessel-translate-is-a-unitary-representation|unitary representation of \(\bar R_0\)]], and \(P\) is [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/equivariance-of-maps-with-amplitude-blocks|equivariant]] for it. The [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/second-moments-of-the-poincare-operators|two moment bounds]] follow:
\[
\sum_{s\in\Omega_i}\lVert P_s-r_\psi(s)\rVert^2\le b^{-12}\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{id}},\qquad\lVert P^*P-1\rVert^2\le b^{-12}\dim_S,\qquad\sum_{s\in\bar G}\lVert P_s\rVert^2\le(1+9b^{-4})\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{main}} .
\]
Finally, for central idempotents of the two factors of \(\bar G\) whose complements are small at the identity, [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/good-forms-near-isometry|the projected map \(e_0P\) is almost an isometry with error \(5b^{-4}\)]]. This uses the [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/remainder-against-a-central-idempotent|remainder against a central idempotent]], the [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/trace-of-the-twisted-translations|trace of \(r_\psi\)]] in terms of the [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/reduced-unipotent-character|reduced unipotent character]], the bound [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/square-sum-of-the-induced-character|\(\sum\lvert\bar\psi^\natural_{\mathsf U}\rvert^2\le2\)]] (the one result of this chapter given only in outline) and the resulting [[proofs/grh/spanning-unfolding-b/trace-against-a-small-central-idempotent|trace estimate]].
