**Theorem (existence of the data).** Let \(P=(q_0,d)\) be [[proofs/grh/reduction-main/parameters|parameters]] and \(0<\varepsilon<1/100\). There is \(x_0\) such that for every integer \(x\ge x_0\) there are [[proofs/grh/reduction-main/data|data]] \(D\) for \(P\) with

- scale \(x\); \(x_1=\lfloor\exp(x^{\varepsilon/6})\rfloor\); \(b\) the least multiple of \(q_1x_1\) exceeding \(\exp(x^{\varepsilon})\);
- \(m\) the least power of \(2\) with \(m\ge\sqrt x\); \(r\) the least power of \(3\) with \(r\ge x^5\);
- \(x^{\varepsilon/6}-1\le\log x_1\) and \(x^{\varepsilon}\le\log b\le x^{\varepsilon}+1\).

## Proof

Outline; the construction follows the Lean file, with the steps I verified marked.

*Scale.* Put \(y=\log x\), \(W=x^{\varepsilon/6}\). For a suitable fixed \(n\) (with \(99q_0d<n\varepsilon/6\)) and all large \(x\), \(W\) is a "large scale": \(100(6\log W+1)\le y\), \(\log W\ge10^{12}c_P\), \(W\ge2\cdot10^6y\), \(W\ge2h_K+2\) (\(h_K\) the [[algebra-fields-galois/class-number|class number]] of \(K\)), \(W\ge n+1\), \(n\log W\ge99q_0dy\), \(W\ge(5184y)^n\), \(W\ge7776yn2^n\). (Verified: each is a comparison of \(x^{\varepsilon/6}\) with a power of \(\log x\) or a constant.)

*Ranks and field.* \(m,r\) as stated; \(s\) with \(2^s=m^{22}\); then \(s\le32y\), \(W\le m\le2^s\le W^n\) and \(q_2(s)^2\le W^n\) (asserted: `exists_isLeast_two_pow_three_pow`, `IsLargeScale.two_pow_le`, `q₂_sq_le`).

*Places.* [[proofs/grh/reduction-main/sparse-places-exist|A sparse set]] \(\Phi_b\) of places \(\equiv-1\) modulo \(q_0\), sparse for primes up to \(kt\), with \(\operatorname{towerDeg}(s)\le\#\Phi_b\,W^6\le\#\Phi_b\log b\).

*Scales.* \(x_1=\lfloor e^W\rfloor\), and \(b\) the least multiple of \(q_1x_1\) above \(\exp(W^6)\); then \(W^6\le\log b\le W^6+1\) (asserted: `exists_isLeast_dvd_log_log_mem_Icc`). Weights by (P4), all in \([1,b]\).

*Primes.* By a consequence of the Siegel–Walfisz theorem, [[?lean:New.exists_prime_modEq_one_log_mem|primes in short logarithmic windows]]: for \(4\mid M'\), fixed \(A\) and large \(W\), every window \([U,U+W]\), \(W\le U\le W^A\), contains \(\log p\) for a prime \(p\equiv1\pmod{M'^k}\), whenever \(k>1\), \(M'^k\le W^A\). Used with \(M'=q_0d\), \(A=n+7\), modulus \(q_2(s)^2=(q_0d)^{2(s+6)}\).
 - \(p_0,p_1\) (verified): take one prime \(\equiv1\pmod{q_2^2}\) from each of the \(h_K+1\) disjoint windows starting at \(10\log b+2iW\), \(0\le i\le h_K\); all lie in \([b^{10},b^{11}]\) since \((2h_K+2)W\le\log b\). Each is \(\equiv1\pmod{q_1d}\), so \(\chi_K(p)=1\) and \(p\mathcal O_K=I_iJ_i\) splits. By the pigeonhole principle in the [[algebra-fields-galois/ideal-class-group|class group]], two of the \(I_i\) have the same class, \(i\ne j\); then \(I_iJ_j\) is principal (the class of \(J_j\) is inverse to that of \(I_j\)), generated by some \(\alpha\in\mathcal O_K\) with \(\alpha\bar\alpha=p_ip_j\).
 - \(N\) (verified): \(m\log b\le\log N_-\le24m\log b\) (from \(1\le\kappa\le b\), \(b^{20}\le M\le b^{22}\), \(1\le\mathrm{rd}_E\le q_1d\le b\), the last because \(q_1d\) lies in the different of \(E\)); take a prime \(N\equiv1\pmod{q_2^2}\) with \(\log N\in[\log N_-,\log N_-+W]\), and \(W\le\sqrt{\log b}\). Since \(4\mid q_2^2\), \(N\equiv1\pmod4\) is a [[?c:Fermat's theorem on sums of two squares|sum of two squares]] \(u^2+w^2\), so \(N=\pi_N\bar\pi_N\) with \(\pi_N=u+w\,i\in\mathcal O_E\) (\(E\) contains \(i\), as \(4\mid q_1\)).

*Assembly.* These objects ("admissible data", `New.Parameters.IsAdmissibleData`) satisfy all conditions of the data; in particular the scale inequalities (S1)–(S5) and the ranges (R1)–(R3) follow from the large-scale conditions (asserted: `New.Parameters.IsAdmissibleData.exists_hasFields`, whose proof I did not read line by line).
