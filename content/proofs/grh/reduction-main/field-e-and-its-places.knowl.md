Let \(P=(q_0,d)\) be [[proofs/grh/reduction-main/parameters|parameters]], \(K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-d})\subset\mathbb C\), and \(s\) a natural number, with \(q_1=q_1(s)\).

**The field.** \(E=E_s:=K(\zeta)\) is the [[algebra-fields-galois/cyclotomic-extension|cyclotomic extension]] of \(K\) generated by a [[algebra-fields-galois/primitive-root-of-unity|primitive \(q_1\)-th root of unity]] \(\zeta\) (constructed as a splitting field of \(X^{q_1}-1\) over \(K\), with a chosen \(\zeta\)). It is a [[algebra-fields-galois/number-field|number field]], abelian over \(\mathbb Q\) and totally complex; it is a [[?c:CM field|CM field]], and its complex conjugation is written \(x\mapsto\bar x\) (it inverts \(\zeta\)).

**The group of places.** Fix a \(K\)-embedding \(\varphi_0:E\to\mathbb C\). The map
\[
\operatorname{Gal}(E/K)\to\{\text{[[?c:infinite place of a number field|infinite places]] of }E\},\qquad g\mapsto\text{the place of }\varphi_0\circ g ,
\]
is a bijection ([[proofs/grh/reduction-main/field-e-properties|properties of \(E\)]]). The set \(\Phi=\Phi_s\) of infinite places of \(E\) is given the commutative group structure transported along it. Thus there are group isomorphisms
\[
\Phi\ \xrightarrow{\ v\mapsto\sigma_v\ }\ \operatorname{Gal}(E/K)\ \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\ (\mathbb Z/q_1)^\times ,
\]
the second sending \(\sigma\) to the class \(a\) with \(\sigma(\zeta)=\zeta^a\) (an isomorphism because the cyclotomic polynomial of order \(q_1\) is irreducible over \(K\)). A place is said to be *\(\equiv a\) modulo \(q_1\)* if it corresponds to \(a\).

- **CM type.** At the place \(v\) the embedding \(\Phi_v:=\varphi_0\circ\sigma_v:E\to\mathbb C\) is chosen; it induces \(v\) and fixes \(K\).
- **Place named by an integer.** For \(\nu\in\mathbb Z\), \(\operatorname{place}(\nu)\in\Phi\) is the place \(\equiv\nu\) modulo \(q_1\) if \(\gcd(\nu,q_1)=1\), and the identity place otherwise.
- **Class of a place.** For \(m\mid q_1\), the class of \(v\) modulo \(m\) is the image of \(v\) under the reduction homomorphism \(\Phi\cong(\mathbb Z/q_1)^\times\to(\mathbb Z/m)^\times\).

**The number \(\Omega\).** With \(\gamma\) [[?c:Euler-Mascheroni constant|Euler's constant]], \(L(s,\chi_K)\) the [[?c:Dirichlet L-function|Dirichlet \(L\)-function]] of the Legendre symbol modulo \(d\), and \(\mathrm{rd}_E=\lvert\operatorname{disc}E\rvert^{1/[E:\mathbb Q]}\) the [[?c:root discriminant|root discriminant]] of \(E\),
\[
\Omega=\Omega_s:=\exp\Bigl(\gamma-\operatorname{Re}\frac{L'}{L}(1,\chi_K)\Bigr)\cdot\frac{2\pi}{\mathrm{rd}_E}.
\]

## Remarks

Although written \(P.\Omega\) and \(P.q_2\) in the source, \(\Omega\), \(q_1\), \(q_2\), \(E\) depend on \(s\), which in the [[proofs/grh/reduction-main/data|data]] grows with \(x\). There \(s\) is the tower exponent with \(2^s=m^{22}\).
