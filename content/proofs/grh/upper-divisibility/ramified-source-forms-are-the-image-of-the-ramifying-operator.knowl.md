**Proposition.** Let \(D\) be [[?lean:New.Data|admissible data]], \(\mathcal S\) the space of source forms, \(z_w\) the [[proofs/grh/upper-divisibility/scaling-generator|scaling generators]] and \(R_s=\prod_{w\in s}(1-z_w)\) the [[proofs/grh/upper-divisibility/ramified-source-forms|ramifying operators]].

1. \(z_w^{N-1}=1\) on \(\mathcal S\) (and on the forms of the weight and level of every stage \(j\)).
2. \(\mathcal S^{\mathrm{ram}}=\operatorname{im}R_{\mathrm{all}}\), the image of the product over all primes \(w\mid\pi_N\). In particular, for a ramified source form \(\varphi\) and each \(w\) there is a source form \(\psi\) with \(\varphi=\psi-z_w\psi\).
3. For every set \(s\) of primes dividing \(\pi_N\), \(\dim\ker R_s\le\dfrac{\lvert s\rvert}{N-1}\dim\mathcal S\).
4. \(\dim\mathcal S^{\mathrm{ram}}\ge\Bigl(1-\dfrac{f}{N-1}\Bigr)\dim\mathcal S\), and \(\dfrac{f}{N-1}\le b^{-8}\).

## Proof

(1) By the [[proofs/grh/upper-divisibility/scaling-generator|corollary on powers]], the scalar matrix of \(z_w^{N-1}\) lies in the level, and forms of that level are fixed by it.

(2) Apply the [[proofs/grh/upper-divisibility/mean-zero-vectors-are-the-image-of-one-minus-translation|lemma on vectors of mean zero]] to the central representation of the commutative group \(U(1)(\mathbb{A}_f)\), the family \((z_w)\) and \(r=N-1\ne0\).

(3) First let \(s=\{w\}\). A source form fixed by \(z_w\) is fixed by the group \(K'\) generated by the level \(K_0\) of stage \(0\) and the scalar matrix of \(z_w\). The group \(K'\) lies in the principal congruence subgroup at the chosen places, is open and consists of integral elements, so it is a level. Both \(K_0\) and \(K'\) are free: this is [[?lean:New.Data.classAut_eq_bot_of_le_chosenLevel|`classAut_eq_bot_of_le_chosenLevel`]] (a level inside the principal congruence subgroup at the chosen places meets every conjugate of the rational points trivially, for rank at most \(2n_m\)), quoted here. At a free level the dimension of the space of forms is the number of classes times the dimension of the weight, and the number of classes is multiplied by the index when the level shrinks; hence \([K':K_0]\cdot\dim(\text{forms of level }K')=\dim\mathcal S\). Finally \([K':K_0]\ge N-1\): some power \(z_w^{i}\) with \(0<i\le[K':K_0]\) lies in \(K_0\), and then \((N-1)\mid i\). So \(\dim\ker(1-z_w)\le(N-1)^{-1}\dim\mathcal S\). The general case follows by induction on \(s\) from \(\dim\ker(fg)\le\dim\ker f+\dim\ker g\).

(4) By (2) and the [[linear-algebra/rank-nullity-theorem|rank–nullity theorem]], \(\dim\mathcal S^{\mathrm{ram}}=\dim\mathcal S-\dim\ker R_{\mathrm{all}}\), and there are exactly \(f\) primes dividing \(\pi_N\). The second bound follows from \(f\le b\) and \(b^{9}+1\le N\), which are among the size comparisons of the data ([[?lean:New.Data.le_of_hasLogBound|`le_of_hasLogBound`]]). \(\blacksquare\)

*Status.* The freeness of the levels and the size comparisons are quoted from other files; the rest is proved here.
