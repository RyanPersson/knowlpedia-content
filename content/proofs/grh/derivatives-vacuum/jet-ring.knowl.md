Let \(C\) be a [[?lean:New.CMLattice|CM lattice]] for \(d\), \(p\) a prime, \(\iota_p\) a \(p\)-adic place of \(\mathbb{C}\), and \(I\), \(X\) finite sets. Let \(\theta\in\mathbb{C}_p[\![T]\!]\) be the Taylor series at \(0\) of the theta function of \(C\) with \(\iota_p\) applied to its coefficients, \(g=-\log\theta\) ([[?lean:New.CMLattice.negLogTheta|the negative logarithm]], \(g(0)=0\)), and \(y\) the Taylor series, read through \(\iota_p\), of the [[?lean:New.CMLattice.translatedY|translated coordinate]] (see [[proofs/grh/derivatives-divisibility/cm-curve-setting|the CM lattice and its functions]]) \(y(z)=\bigl(\wp'(z+t)-a_1(\wp(z+t)-\wp(t))\bigr)/2\). For linear forms \(\mu_i(z)=\sum_{x\in X}\mu_{i,x}z_x\) with \(\mu_{i,x}\in\mathbb{C}_p\) (\(i\in I\)), let \(\mathcal{O}[\![y(\mu z)]\!]\) be the [[?lean:New.CMLattice.multiSeriesRing|ring of integral power series in the \(y(\mu_iz)\)]]: the series \(G(y(\mu_iz))_{i}\) with \(G\) a power series in variables indexed by \(I\) whose coefficients have absolute value \(\le1\).

The **jet ring** \(\mathcal{J}_C(\mu)\subset\mathbb{C}_p[\![z_x:x\in X]\!]\) is the subring generated by
\[ \mathcal{O}[\![y(\mu z)]\!],\qquad\theta(\mu_iz)\ (i\in I),\qquad g'(\mu_iz)\ (i\in I). \]

## Properties

It contains the integral constants, hence is stable under multiplication by them, and it contains the theta products \(\prod_i\theta(\mu_iz)^{m_i}\), \(m_i\in\mathbb{N}\).
