This chapter sets up the objects on which the upper bound of the proof is built; it proves few of the main theorems itself. Throughout, \(D\) is [[?lean:New.Data|admissible data]] for [[?lean:New.Parameters|parameters]] \((q_0,d)\): \(K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-d})\), \(E=K(\zeta)\) with \(f\) infinite places, \(m=2\varrho+2\) lifts, ranks \(r\), \(k=r+1-m\), \(t=5r\), \(n_j=k+j(t+1)\), gap \(\Delta=2r^8\), level primes \(p_0,p_1\) with \(M=p_0p_1\), the level \(N=\pi_N\bar\pi_N\), and lift weights \(w_j=\kappa_j(\tau)=\kappa(\tau)+(j+\tfrac12)\Delta\).

The picture is this. *Source forms* live on the definite space \(X_0\) (rank \(k\)); *target forms* on \(Y_0=X_0\oplus\)(\(\varrho\) hyperbolic planes)\(\oplus\)(a line of norm \(-N\)), of rank \(r\). The Poincaré map takes source forms to forms on \(Y_0\). Two chains of \(m\) theta lifts run in parallel: \(Y_0\to Y_1\to\dots\to Y_m\) (ranks \(r+jt\)) and \(X_0\to X_1\to\dots\to X_m\) (ranks \(n_j\)), and \(Y_m\subset X_m\) has corank one. The seesaw identity (another chapter) compares the first chain after the Poincaré map with the *composite lift*: a combination of chains along the \(X_j\), glued in pairs above \(N\), then restricted to \(Y_m\). This chapter defines all these maps and proves the formal properties they need.

## 1. Index sets, labels and the good idempotent

The bases of the spaces are indexed by explicit finite sets: see [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/index-sets-of-the-hermitian-spaces|index sets of \(X_j\) and \(Y_j\)]]; the row index set \(\mathcal R\) has \(r\) elements. The signs of the indefinite spaces \(W_j\) and the doubled target parameter are in [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/signs-and-parameter-of-w|signs and parameter of \(W_j\)]].

At the chosen places (half of the places above \(M\)) the level is principal, so the finite group \(\bar G=\prod_w\mathrm{GL}_r(\mathbb F_w)\) acts on forms. Its characters are named by labels. A [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/primitive-polynomial|primitive polynomial]] is an irreducible label with a root generating \(\mathbb F_{q^r}^\times\); such labels are [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/primitive-polynomial-scaling-and-norm|stable under scaling by suitable roots of unity, and the product of their roots generates \(\mathbb F_q^\times\)]]. A [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/good-tuple-of-labels|good tuple]] (over the residue fields at the chosen places above one prime) has no label with a root that is a \(q_2\)-th root of unity of the base field, and at least one primitive label. The [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/good-idempotent|good idempotent \(e_0\)]] is the sum of the idempotents of the families of labels that restrict to a good tuple above \(p_0\) and to a good tuple above \(p_1\) (so a primitive label is required above each of the two primes); it is central and [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/good-idempotent-kills-hyperplane-sums|kills hyperplane sums]].

## 2. Weights and parameters at infinity

On the definite spaces the forms are algebraic modular forms with values in Weyl modules. Their highest weights are fixed in [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/weights-of-the-definite-spaces|parameters, weights and lift diagrams of \(X_j\) and \(Y_m\)]], and described by column counts common to all lifts in [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/columns-of-the-weights|the column description]]: the weights of \(X_m\) and \(Y_m\) interlace, and the [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/branching-exponent-formula|branching exponent]] is \(n_\tau=a_\tau-m\Delta/2\).

On the indefinite spaces the forms have discrete series type, given by Harish-Chandra parameters. All parameters of the proof are built in one way: a [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/centre-system|centre system]] has a stem and blocks around centres \(\pm w_l\); its parameters [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/centre-system-parameter-has-gap|have a large gap]], and [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/inserting-a-block-satisfies-the-block-rule|inserting one block gives a pair of parameters that satisfies the block rule]] of the theta correspondence. This is applied to the two grids of spaces \(Y^i_j\), \(\tilde Y^j_i\): their [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/grid-nodes|index sets, signs and parameters]] have the [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/properties-of-grid-nodes|expected ranks, signs, gaps and an interlacing at the node \((m,m)\)]]; they come from [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/grid-centres|one separated centre system per grid]]; hence [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/grid-steps-correspond|the parameters at the two ends of every step correspond]].

## 3. The spaces \(Y_j\), \(W_j\), target forms and descent pairs

The [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/spaces-y-and-w|spaces \(Y_j\) and \(W_j\)]] are constructed with their lattices. [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/target-form|Target forms]] are the good forms (image of \(e_0\)) among the forms of principal level on \(Y_0\) with the target parameter at infinity; these spaces are [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/principal-forms-are-finite-dimensional|finite-dimensional because the parameter has gap \(\Delta\)]].

A single lift \(W\to V\) with \(V\) definite, together with its descent, is packaged as a [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/descent-pair|descent pair]]: its [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/block-datum|block datum]] obeys [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/block-datum-laws|two laws]] (a sum of translates; an eigenvector of the level), its [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/normalized-descent|normalized descent]] is a class sum, and a form on \(V\) can have [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/lift-hecke-polynomial|the Hecke polynomials of a lift]]. When a parameter [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/descent-pair-fitting-parameters|fits the pair]], [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/highest-vector-polynomial-is-an-edge|the polynomial of the highest vector is a product of joint harmonics and gives an edge]], and [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/normalized-descent-is-a-transposed-lift|the normalized descent is a multiple of the conjugate transposed lift]]. The data give [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/descent-pairs-of-the-data|descent pairs \((W_j,V_j)\) which the target parameter fits]].

## 4. The chain along the \(Y_j\) and the Poincaré map

With the [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/point-datum-on-y|levels, theta kernels and point data along the \(Y_j\)]], the [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/lower-chain|chain \(\Theta_\vartheta\colon\mathcal A(Y_0)\to\mathcal A(Y_m)\)]] is defined for every tame character \(\vartheta\); its image has [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/lower-chain-level-and-equivariance|a known level and character, and the chain commutes with \(\bar G\)]]. The [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/poincare-map|Poincaré map]] sends a source form to the form on \(Y_0\) representing its Bessel period.

## 5. Chains along the \(X_j\) and the composite lift

[[proofs/grh/upper-objects/source-form|Source forms]] are algebraic forms on \(X_0\) of weight \((a,0,\dots,0)\) at the \(a\)-places, with the [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/level-places-of-the-data|level of stage 0]]. In general, for a tower of abstract theta kernels, [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/chain-of-lifts|chains of lifts]] are [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/chain-equivariance|equivariant]], and for a [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/jointly-invariant-combination|jointly invariant combination]] of data at free levels [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/jointly-invariant-chain-is-a-plain-sum|the chain is a plain sum over tuples of classes]], with no denominators; [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/free-levels-at-the-chosen-places|levels principal at the chosen places are free]] in the relevant ranks, by [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/chosen-places-and-chosen-group-order|facts on the chosen places]].

For the data: the [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/lift-kernels-of-the-chain|kernels of the lifts \(X_j\to X_{j+1}\)]], the [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/branching-map-and-restriction-to-y|integral branching map and the restriction to \(Y_m\)]], and finally [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/chain-datum-and-composite-lift|chain data and the composite lift]].

## 6. Above the level \(N\)

The gluing in each double lift is a sum of translates with simple poles above \(N\). In the [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/glued-datum-in-the-schrodinger-model|Schrödinger model above \(N\)]] it is the glued datum. The abstract theory of such data is independent of theta series: at a [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/split-level|split level]], with the [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/data-modulo-n|direct, mirror and glued data]], the [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/mirabolic-subgroup-at-split-level|mirabolic subgroup]], the [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/up-operator|operator Up]] and [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/ramified-vector-at-split-level|ramified vectors]], any lift obeying the [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/lift-law|lift law]] satisfies: [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/lift-law-vanishing-on-ramified-vectors|scalar-invariant data kill ramified vectors]]; on ramified vectors, [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/mirror-lift-through-direct-lift|the translate by \(D_N=\operatorname{diag}(1,\dots,1,N^{-1})\) of a mirror lift is Up of the direct lift]]; on ramified vectors, [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/glued-double-lift|the \(D_N\)-translate of a glued double lift is the number of primitive vectors times \(\gamma'\gamma\) times the two direct lifts after Up]]; and so [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/chain-of-glued-lifts-becomes-direct|a chain of glued double lifts (each including its \(D_N\)-translate) of a ramified vector is an explicit scalar times a chain of direct lifts after a power of Up]]. Finally [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/chains-kill-ramified-source-forms|a chain one of whose data is fixed by a scaling above \(N\) annihilates the ramified source forms]].

## 7. Theta kernels between definite spaces: conjugates, size, integrality

The sizes and the \(p\)-adic integrality of the composite lift come from properties of single kernels. Galois conjugation acts on the Taylor coefficients of theta functions through a [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/jet-conjugation|semilinear jet conjugation]], which [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/existence-of-jet-conjugation|exists for every automorphism of \(\mathbb C\) over \(K\)]]. With [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/bounds-for-the-vacuum-and-its-conjugates|uniform bounds for the moved vacuum and its conjugates]] and a Cauchy estimate this gives [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/archimedean-bounds-for-jets|archimedean bounds for all conjugates of jets]] of combinations of translates of the integral vacuum. \(p\)-adically one considers [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/gauge-raising-data|data that raise the gauge of forms by at most a factor]]: [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/gauge-bounds-for-one-lift|standard data raise it by an explicit factor]], in particular by the predicted divisibility at primes not split in \(K\); [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/vacuum-data-of-functions-modulo-an-ideal|data given by functions modulo an ideal]] are of this kind; and [[proofs/grh/upper-objects/definite-kernel-scale|one explicit normalising constant makes the kernels with polynomial data integral and bounded]].
