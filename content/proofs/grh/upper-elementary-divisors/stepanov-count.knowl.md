**Lemma.** Let \(W\) be a [[algebra-commutative/local-ring|local ring]] whose residue field \(\kappa\) has \(q\) elements (with \(q:=0\) if \(\kappa\) is infinite), \(R\) a commutative ring, \(\iota\colon W\to R\) a ring homomorphism, \(E\ge0\), and \(\Phi\in R[t]\) a nonzero polynomial such that \(t^{E}\) divides \(\Phi(t+\iota(x))\) for every \(x\in W\). Then \(E\,q\le\deg\Phi\).

## Proof

If \(\kappa\) is infinite there is nothing to prove. Otherwise choose a lift \(\tilde z\in W\) of each \(z\in\kappa\) and put \(a_z=\iota(\tilde z)\). For \(z\ne z'\), \(\tilde z-\tilde z'\) is a unit of \(W\), so \(a_z-a_{z'}\) is a unit of \(R\); hence the polynomials \((t-a_z)^{E}\) are pairwise coprime (the ideal generated by \(t-a_z\) and \(t-a_{z'}\) contains the unit \(a_{z'}-a_z\)). Each divides \(\Phi\) by hypothesis, so their product, a monic polynomial of degree \(Eq\), divides \(\Phi\). Since \(R\ne0\) (as \(\Phi\neq0\)) and a monic polynomial cannot divide a nonzero polynomial of smaller degree, \(Eq\le\deg\Phi\). \(\square\)
