A **linear functional** on a [[linear-algebra/vector-space|vector space]] \(V\) over a field \(K\) is a [[linear-algebra/linear-map|linear map]] \(\lambda:V\to K\). Thus \(\lambda(av+bw)=a\lambda(v)+b\lambda(w)\) for all \(a,b\in K\) and \(v,w\in V\).
