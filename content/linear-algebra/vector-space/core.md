A **vector space** over a [[real-analysis/field-axioms|field]] $\mathbb{F}$ is
a [[shared-foundations/set|set]] $V$ equipped with two operations
([[shared-foundations/function|functions]]) $+:V\times V\to V$ and
$\cdot:\mathbb{F}\times V\to V$ such that for all $u,v,w\in V$ and
$a,b\in\mathbb{F}$:
\[
\begin{aligned}
&u+v=v+u,\qquad (u+v)+w=u+(v+w),\\
&\exists\,0\in V:\ v+0=v,\qquad \forall v\in V\ \exists\,(-v)\in V:\ v+(-v)=0,\\
&a\cdot(u+v)=a\cdot u+a\cdot v,\qquad (a+b)\cdot v=a\cdot v+b\cdot v,\\
&(ab)\cdot v=a\cdot(b\cdot v),\qquad 1\cdot v=v.
\end{aligned}
\]
