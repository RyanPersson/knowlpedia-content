Let \(K\) be a discretely valued field with inertia subgroup \(I_K\) and wild
inertia subgroup \(P_K\). An étale representation, or the corresponding
étale local system, is **tamely ramified** if
\[
P_K
\]
acts trivially. It may still have nontrivial action of the tame quotient
\(I_K/P_K\).

This étale definition should not be identified with regular singularity of a
complex connection without invoking a comparison theorem.
