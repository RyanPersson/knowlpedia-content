Let \(X\) be a complex algebraic variety with a fixed algebraic
stratification. A **perverse sheaf** on \(X\) is an object of the heart
\[
\operatorname{Perv}(X)
=\,{}^pD^{\leq 0}(X)\cap{}^pD^{\geq 0}(X)
\]
of the middle-perversity \(t\)-structure on the constructible derived
category.

Equivalently, its ordinary cohomology sheaves obey the support and cosupport
dimension inequalities. The shift is normalized so that a local system on a
smooth complex variety of dimension \(d\), placed in degree \(d\), is
perverse.
