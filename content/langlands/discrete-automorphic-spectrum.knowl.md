Let \(G\) be a connected [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/reductive-algebraic-group|reductive group]] over a
[[langlands-letter/knowls/global-local-fields-completions|global field]]
\(F\). After fixing a unitary
[[algebra-representation-theory/central-character|central character]] or
removing the split-center direction,
the **discrete automorphic spectrum** is the maximal closed
\(G(\mathbb A_F)\)-subrepresentation of the automorphic \(L^2\)-space that is
a Hilbert direct sum of irreducibles:

\[
L^2_{\mathrm{disc}}
\cong
\widehat{\bigoplus}_{\pi}
m_{\mathrm{disc}}(\pi)\,\pi.
\]

The integers \(m_{\mathrm{disc}}(\pi)\) are the discrete automorphic
multiplicities.

## The ambient quotient

One common convention uses
\(G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A_F)^1\), where

\[
G(\mathbb A_F)^1
=
\bigcap_{\chi\in X_F^*(G)}
\ker |\chi|_{\mathbb A}.
\]

Another fixes a unitary central character and works modulo an appropriate
central subgroup. The convention must be stated because
\(G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A_F)\) can have infinite volume in split central
directions.

## Cuspidal and residual parts

There is an orthogonal decomposition

\[
L^2_{\mathrm{disc}}
=
L^2_{\mathrm{cusp}}
\oplus
L^2_{\mathrm{res}},
\]

where the first term is generated by
[[langlands/cuspidal-automorphic-representation|cuspidal automorphic
representations]] and the second is the
[[langlands/residual-automorphic-spectrum|residual spectrum]]. The residual
part is built from poles and residues of
[[langlands-letter/knowls/eisenstein-series|Eisenstein series]] attached to
cuspidal data on proper
[[algebraic-geometry-foundations/levi-subgroup|Levi subgroups]].

## Discrete does not mean local discrete series

“Discrete automorphic” describes occurrence in a global \(L^2\)-quotient. It
does not require each local component to be a discrete-series
representation. Likewise, it is unrelated to discreteness of the adelic
group itself.

## Classification problem

A [[langlands/global-langlands-parameter|global parameter]] is expected to
determine a packet of discrete
[[langlands/automorphic-representation|automorphic representations]], while an [[langlands/arthur-multiplicity-formula|Arthur multiplicity formula]] selects
the members and multiplicities that actually occur. This makes the discrete
spectrum a central testing ground for endoscopy and functoriality.
