The **geometric Langlands** collection develops the categorical
correspondence from its curve, bundle, local-system, and Hecke-theoretic
dependencies. The
[[langlands/geometric-langlands-correspondence|correspondence]] has several
formulations and theorem statuses; this index covers the characteristic-zero
de Rham formulation and its ramified extensions.

For the ordinary arithmetic, automorphic, endoscopic, and function-field
dictionary, see the [[langlands/modern-langlands-index|modern Langlands
foundations index]].
