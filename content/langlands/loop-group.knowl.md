Let \(G\) be an affine algebraic group over a field \(k\). Its **loop group**
is the group-valued functor
\[
LG(R)=G(R((t)))
\]
on \(k\)-algebras \(R\). It records \(G\)-valued points on the punctured
formal disc.

The [[langlands/positive-loop-group|positive loop group]] \(L^+G\) is the
subfunctor of loops extending across the puncture.
