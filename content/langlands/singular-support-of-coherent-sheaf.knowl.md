Let \(Y\) be a quasi-smooth derived scheme or stack, and let
\(\operatorname{Sing}(Y)\) be its classical scheme of singularities. The
**singular support** of a coherent complex \(\mathcal F\) is a closed conical
subset
\[
\operatorname{SS}(\mathcal F)\subseteq\operatorname{Sing}(Y)
\]
defined by the action of cohomological operators associated with the
derived normal directions of \(Y\).

For a conical closed subset \(Z\subseteq\operatorname{Sing}(Y)\), the full
subcategory \(\operatorname{IndCoh}_Z(Y)\) consists of
[[langlands/ind-coherent-sheaf|ind-coherent sheaves]] whose singular support
is contained in \(Z\).

This derived-algebraic notion is distinct from the singular support of a
distribution or the microsupport of a constructible sheaf.
