Let \(G\) be a connected [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/reductive-algebraic-group|reductive group]] over a
[[langlands-letter/knowls/global-local-fields-completions|global field]]
\(F\). An
[[langlands/automorphic-form|automorphic form]] \(f\) is **cuspidal** if

\[
\int_{N(F)\backslash N(\mathbb A_F)} f(ng)\,dn=0
\]

for every proper
[[algebraic-geometry-foundations/parabolic-subgroup|\(F\)-parabolic subgroup]]
\(P=MN\), with [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/levi-subgroup|Levi subgroup]]
\(M\) and [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/unipotent-radical|unipotent
radical]] \(N\), and every
\(g\in G(\mathbb A_F)\). A **cuspidal automorphic representation** is an
[[algebra-representation-theory/irreducible-representation|irreducible representation]] occurring in the space generated by cuspidal
automorphic forms.

## Analytic position

After fixing a unitary
[[algebra-representation-theory/central-character|central character]] or
quotienting by the split center,
cuspidal forms are square-integrable. Their representation space decomposes
discretely with finite multiplicities:

\[
L^2_{\mathrm{cusp}}
\cong
\widehat{\bigoplus}_{\pi}
m_{\mathrm{cusp}}(\pi)\,\pi.
\]

Thus the cuspidal spectrum lies in the
[[langlands/discrete-automorphic-spectrum|discrete automorphic spectrum]].
The converse fails: the discrete spectrum can also contain
[[langlands/residual-automorphic-spectrum|residual representations]].

## Why every proper parabolic matters

The integral is the
[[langlands/automorphic-constant-term|constant term along \(P\)]]. Vanishing
only along a chosen
Borel is sufficient in some standard situations but is not the invariant
general definition. The condition must be imposed only for parabolic
subgroups defined over \(F\).

For an \(F\)-anisotropic group modulo center there are no proper
\(F\)-parabolics, so every automorphic form is cuspidal by this definition.

## Local versus global cuspidality

Cuspidality here is a global condition on constant terms. It is not the same
as [[harmonic-analysis/supercuspidal-representation|supercuspidality]] of a
local component. A cuspidal automorphic representation may have principal
series components at many places; indeed it is unramified at almost all
finite places.

## Langlands-program role

Cuspidal representations are the basic discrete global inputs.
[[langlands-letter/knowls/eisenstein-series|Eisenstein series]] built from
cuspidal data on Levi subgroups organize the remainder of the automorphic
spectrum, while [[langlands/global-langlands-reciprocity|global reciprocity]]
seeks arithmetic
parameters for the cuspidal pieces.
