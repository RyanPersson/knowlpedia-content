A **global field** in this letter means a number field: a finite extension \(F/\mathbb{Q}\).

A **place** \(v\) of \(F\) is an equivalence class of absolute values on \(F\). It is **archimedean** if it is induced by an embedding \(F\hookrightarrow\mathbb{R}\) or \(F\hookrightarrow\mathbb{C}\), and **nonarchimedean** otherwise.

The **completion** \(F_v\) is the completion of \(F\) with respect to a representative \(|\cdot|_v\) of the place.

A **local field** is a nondiscrete locally compact topological field. The completions of number fields are \(\mathbb R\), \(\mathbb C\), or finite extensions of \(\mathbb Q_p\).
