A **connected reductive algebraic group** over a field \(k\) is a smooth connected affine \(k\)-group \(G\) whose unipotent radical \(R_u(G)\) is trivial.

It is **split over \(k\)** if it contains a split maximal torus \(T\simeq\mathbb G_m^r\) defined over \(k\).

## Remarks

**Key properties (used in the letter):**
- Splitness lets one define roots and weights in \(X^*(T)\) and build the [[langlands-letter/knowls/langlands-dual-group|Langlands dual group]].
- A split reductive group over a nonarchimedean local field has hyperspecial maximal compact subgroups after choosing a suitable integral model.

## Examples

- The group \(\mathrm{GL}_n\) over \(k\) is split reductive.
