Let \(\pi:G\to \mathrm{GL}(V)\) be a representation on a finite-dimensional complex vector space \(V\), and let \(V^*=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb C}(V,\mathbb C)\). The **contragredient representation** is the representation \(\pi^\vee:G\to \mathrm{GL}(V^*)\) defined by
\[
(\pi^\vee(g)\ell)(v):=\ell(\pi(g^{-1})v)\quad (\ell\in V^*,\,v\in V).
\]

## Remarks

In the letter, this is the representation denoted \(\pi^e\).
