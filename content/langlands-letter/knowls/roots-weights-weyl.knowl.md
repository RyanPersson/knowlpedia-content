Let \(G\) be a [[langlands-letter/knowls/split-reductive-group|split]]
connected [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/reductive-algebraic-group|reductive group]] with split
[[langlands-letter/knowls/maximal-torus-weight-lattice|maximal torus]]
\(T\). The adjoint action of \(T\) on
[[lie-groups/lie-algebra|\(\operatorname{Lie}(G)\)]] gives

\[
\operatorname{Lie}(G)
=
\operatorname{Lie}(T)
\oplus
\bigoplus_{\alpha\in\Phi}\mathfrak g_\alpha.
\]

The nonzero characters \(\alpha\in X^*(T)\) occurring here are the
**roots**.

## Weyl group and positive roots

The **Weyl group**

\[
W=N_G(T)/T
\]

where \(N_G(T)\) is the
[[algebra-groups/normalizer|normalizer]]. The group \(W\) acts on
\(X^*(T)\) and is generated by the root reflections. Choosing a
[[algebraic-geometry-foundations/borel-subgroup|Borel subgroup]] \(B\supset T\) selects [[lie-groups/positive-root|positive roots]] \(\Phi^+\) and simple
roots \(\Delta\).

A character \(\lambda\in X^*(T)\) is **dominant** when

\[
\langle\lambda,\alpha^\vee\rangle\geq0
\]

for every \(\alpha\in\Delta\), using the
[[langlands-letter/knowls/coroots-and-pairing|root–coroot pairing]].

## Highest weights

Over an [[algebraic-geometry-foundations/algebraically-closed-field|algebraically closed field]] of characteristic \(0\), irreducible
finite-dimensional algebraic representations of \(G\) are classified by
dominant characters in \(X^*(T)\). The abstract [[lie-groups/weight-lattice|weight lattice]] of the root
system can be larger than \(X^*(T)\); only the characters allowed by the
[[langlands-letter/knowls/root-vs-weight-lattice-isogeny|isogeny form]]
occur as representations of \(G\).

For \(\operatorname{GL}_n\), the roots are \(e_i-e_j\), the [[lie-groups/weyl-group|Weyl group]] is
\(S_n\), and the standard Borel gives dominant weights
\(\lambda_1\geq\cdots\geq\lambda_n\).

## Langlands role

[[langlands-letter/knowls/langlands-dual-group|Langlands duality]] exchanges
roots and coroots. [[langlands/dominant-coweight|Dominant coweights]] of
\(G\) become dominant weights of \(\widehat G\), indexing both the Satake
basis and the Schubert strata used in
[[langlands/geometric-satake-equivalence|geometric Satake]].
