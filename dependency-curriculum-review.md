# Curriculum dependency review — second semantic pass

This batch follows [the foundational review](dependency-foundations-review.md). It reviews complete prerequisite lists against the actual definitions and theorem statements, including unlinked notation. Examples, consequences of a definition, and optional equivalent constructions are distinguished from defining inputs. The concepts used in a theorem’s conclusion remain prerequisites for understanding its statement.

Reviewed **184 lists**: **183 newly reviewed concepts** and a second review of vector bundles. Corrected **116 lists**, adding **168** edges and removing **72**. The full development graph has **3501 nodes**, **10180 edges**, and **zero cycles**.

Across both batches, **247 concepts** have completed semantic reviews; **239** also have every transitive prerequisite reviewed. This is scoped semantic coverage, not a claim that all 3,501 concepts have been reviewed. Global structural validation covers the entire graph.

## Main corrections

- Group and ring homomorphisms precede their kernels, images and quotient isomorphism theorems. Those theorems now include the previously omitted quotient/image vocabulary.
- Subspaces, spans, bases, quotients, ranks and dual spaces include the ingredients used in their formulas. Eigenvalues no longer require characteristic polynomials or determinants.
- Closed sets precede closure; topological bases precede subbases and product topologies. Subsequence and uniform-continuity definitions no longer import their later applications.
- Indicators can be defined before measurability. Probability measures precede probability spaces; expectation and conditional expectation now include their integration inputs.
- A smooth bundle is defined by a surjective submersion and explicit local product maps. Its definition introduces the model manifold, which is then called its typical fiber. Typical fibers therefore follow smooth bundles. This resolves the semantic bootstrap without inventing a new concept or deleting a necessary construction.

## Entry assumptions

- Elementary logic and set membership
- Elementary real/complex arithmetic and Euclidean spaces
- Euclidean C-infinity calculus and second countability where no dedicated canonical knowl currently exists

The axiom of choice is explicitly linked where the basis existence theorem assumes it; it was not silently added to the entry assumptions.
